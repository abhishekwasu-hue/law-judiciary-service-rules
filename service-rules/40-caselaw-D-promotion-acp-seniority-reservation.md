# 40-D  Case-law: Promotion, DPC delay, ACP/TBP, seniority, reservation in promotion

**Scope**: (i) right to be *considered* for promotion and consequences of DPC delay; (ii) reservation in promotion — *Nagaraj*, *Jarnail Singh* (2018 & 2022), *B.K. Pavitra*, and the Maharashtra litigation (*Vijay Ghogre*, SLP 28306/2017, GR 25.05.2004 / GR 07.05.2021 — see library file 31); (iii) seniority principles; (iv) denial of ACP/time-bound promotion where the employee refused promotion.

## 2. Case details

### 2.1 Union of India v. Hemraj Singh Chauhan (SC, 2010) ✖
- Respondents (State Civil Service officers) were denied consideration for IAS promotion because the Union/State delayed finalising seniority and holding the Selection Committee; they retired in the meantime. SC held the delay was arbitrary and violated the right to be considered (Art. 16); directed that they be deemed to have been considered and promoted from the date their juniors were, with consequential benefits.
- **Petitioners use**: court staff who retired while DPC was pending (e.g. Senior Clerk → Assistant Superintendent, Stenographer grades) claim notional promotion and pay fixation.
- **State uses**: distinguish — delay must be *unexplained* and attributable to the Government; where the delay was due to the High Court's own administrative process (Art. 229 — appointing authority is the Chief Justice/District Judge), the State is not the delinquent; where DPC was held and the petitioner was found unfit / had a pending DE (sealed cover), no notional promotion; relief limited by Tarsem Singh for arrears.

### 2.2 M. Nagaraj (2006) ✖ and Jarnail Singh I (2018) & II (2022) ✖
- **Nagaraj** (five-judge): Art. 16(4A) is an enabling provision; before providing reservation in promotion the State must collect quantifiable data showing backwardness, inadequacy of representation and that overall efficiency (Art. 335) is not compromised; 50% ceiling, creamy layer and no indefinite extension are constitutional requirements.
- **Jarnail Singh I** (five-judge): no need to revisit Nagaraj; the "backwardness" data requirement for SC/ST is contrary to Indra Sawhney and is invalid; inadequacy and efficiency data still required; creamy layer applies to SC/ST for promotion (Court won't apply it itself to exclude, but the State must).
- **Jarnail Singh II** (three-judge, 28.01.2022): Court will not fix a yardstick for inadequacy; unit for data collection is the **cadre**; data must precede the decision; periodic review; the decisions in Nagaraj apply prospectively (promotions before 2006 protected); State-specific challenges (Maharashtra, Bihar, Tripura, etc.) to be heard separately on their data.
- **State uses** (Maharashtra): the 2004 GR regime stands struck by *Vijay Ghogre*; till the SLP is decided, promotions follow GR 07.05.2021 — by seniority as on 25.05.2004, purely temporary, subject to SLP 28306/2017, with no reversion of already-promoted reserved-category employees (library file 31). Affidavits should say the matter is sub judice and that the High Court should not pass final orders on reserved-vs-unreserved promotion claims.
- **Petitioners use**: (a) reserved-category employees — demand restoration of reservation (Jarnail II allows State to collect data and act); (b) unreserved employees — challenge any promotion under reservation after 2017 as contrary to Ghogre; (c) both sides attack the "purely temporary" nature of promotions and seek regularisation/seniority.

### 2.3 B.K. Pavitra II (2019) ✖
- Karnataka's 2018 Act, enacted after collecting cadre-wise data through the Ratna Prabha Committee, upheld; the Court held "efficiency of administration" under Art. 335 must be defined in an inclusive sense and the State's data-based decision deserves deference.
- **State uses**: template for what Maharashtra must do to restore reservation in promotion — a data-based legislative/executive measure; until then no reservation in promotion can be applied to court staff beyond GR 07.05.2021.

### 2.4 Vijay Ghogre (Bom HC, 2017) ◐ and SLP 28306/2017 ◐
- LiveLaw (27.07.2017): a difference of opinion between Mohta J (26.07.2016) and Sayed J (21.12.2016) was resolved by Sonak J agreeing with Sayed J; the Court struck down GAD GRs (25.05.2004 and a companion GR) providing reservation in promotion as ultra vires Art. 16(4A), because the State had collected no quantifiable data on inadequacy and, on the figures available, SC representation already exceeded the prescribed percentage. (The exact judgment date is given in library file 31 as 04.08.2017 — the opinions differ in date; verify the operative order from bombayhighcourt.nic.in.)
- Supreme Court: State's SLP(C) 28306/2017 — no stay; in Nov 2017 the Maharashtra matter was among those from which the reference in the Jarnail Singh batch arose; after Jarnail II (2022) the State-specific matters are to be decided individually; as of this research no final order located.
- **Drafting note**: every promotion order of court staff in the reserved stream after 2017 must carry the GR 07.05.2021 conditions (temporary, subject to SLP). When answering petitions, annex GR 07.05.2021 and the SLP status.

### 2.5 Union of India v. Manju Arora (SC, 03.01.2022) ✖
- Employee refused regular promotion, then claimed financial upgradation under ACP. Held: ACP is a benefit against stagnation for those who got no promotion; one who declines promotion cannot be said to be stagnating; not entitled to ACP (and the refusal-related debarment under the scheme applies).
- **Maharashtra parallel**: FD ACP/TBP GRs 2010 and 2019 (library files 10–11) contain a similar condition — benefit not available/withdrawn where the employee refused promotion; also conditions on departmental examination, DPC clearance and no pending DE (GAD GR 15.12.2017).
- **State uses**: deny ACP/TBP to court staff who declined promotion/transfer-linked promotion (common: Junior Clerk declining Senior Clerk posting at another station); also to withdraw ACP once promotion is refused after grant.
- **Petitioners use**: argue refusal was for a valid reason (medical/family) or that the scheme (10-20-30) does not contain an express forfeiture clause for their period — check the exact GR text.

### 2.6 Seniority — Direct Recruit Class II (1990) ✖; K. Meghachandra Singh (2019) ✖
- Direct Recruit (Constitution Bench, Maharashtra case): (A) once an incumbent is appointed to a post according to rule, seniority counts from the date of appointment, not confirmation; (B) ad-hoc/stop-gap appointment outside the rules does not count for seniority unless followed by regular appointment per rules without break (then from the date of regularisation per rules).
- Meghachandra: seniority cannot relate back to a date before the person entered the cadre merely because the vacancy arose earlier (N.R. Parmar overruled prospectively).
- **State uses**: in court-staff seniority disputes between promotees and direct recruits (e.g. Senior Clerk cadre), and against claims for seniority from the date of "vacancy" or from the date of a temporary (GR 07.05.2021) promotion.

## 3. Drafting checklist — promotion/ACP/seniority petitions
1. Appointing/promoting authority for the post (CJ/Registrar General for HC staff; District Judge for district court staff under Art. 235/District Court Rules) — State's role limited to pay/financial sanction.
2. Dates: vacancy, DPC, seniority list publication, objections, petitioner's retirement.
3. Any DE/criminal case → sealed cover (Jankiraman) (file G).
4. Reservation stream → GR 07.05.2021 conditions + SLP 28306/2017 status; no final relief while sub judice.
5. ACP/TBP → refusal of promotion? departmental exam? DPC clearance? (Manju Arora; FD GRs).
6. Seniority → Direct Recruit rules; ad-hoc service; Tarsem Singh third-party exception.

## 4. मराठी सारांश
- पदोन्नतीसाठी *विचारात घेतले जाण्याचा* हक्क हा अनुच्छेद 16 अंतर्गत मूलभूत हक्क; शासनाच्या अकारण विलंबाने DPC न झाल्यास काल्पनिक पदोन्नती द्यावी लागते (*हेमराज सिंग चौहान*).
- पदोन्नतीतील आरक्षण: *एम. नागराज* (2006) — प्रतिनिधित्वाची अपुरीता व प्रशासकीय कार्यक्षमता यांचा परिमाणात्मक डेटा आवश्यक; *जरनैल सिंग I* (2018) — मागासलेपणाचा डेटा अनावश्यक, क्रीमी लेयर लागू; *जरनैल सिंग II* (2022) — डेटा संवर्गनिहाय, वेळोवेळी पुनरावलोकन.
- महाराष्ट्र: *विजय घोगरे* (मुं.उ.न्या. 2017) — शा.नि. 25.05.2004 अनुच्छेद 16(4A) विरुद्ध; शासनाची SLP 28306/2017 प्रलंबित, स्थगिती नाही; तोपर्यंत पदोन्नती शा.नि. 07.05.2021 नुसार सेवाज्येष्ठतेने, निव्वळ तात्पुरती.
- *मंजू अरोरा* (2022): पदोन्नती नाकारणाऱ्याला ACP/कालबद्ध पदोन्नतीचा आर्थिक लाभ नाही (वित्त विभागाच्या 2010/2019 शासननिर्णयांतही तशीच अट).
- ज्येष्ठता: नियमानुसार नियुक्तीच्या तारखेपासून, तदर्थ सेवा मोजली जात नाही (*डायरेक्ट रिक्रूट क्लास II*, *मेघचंद्र सिंग*).
