# NPS / DCPS employees (appointed on or after 01.11.2005) — family pension, death/retirement gratuity, prior-service counting, contribution procedure
# राष्ट्रीय निवृत्तिवेतन प्रणाली / परिभाषित अंशदान निवृत्तिवेतन योजना — कुटुंब निवृत्तिवेतन, उपदान, पूर्वसेवा जोडणे, अंशदान कार्यपद्धती

**Issuing department:** Finance Department, Desk सेवा-4 (file prefixes अंनियो / रानिप्र / संकीर्ण)
**Status:** In force.

## 1. FD GR dated 31.03.2023 — family pension & gratuity to NPS employees under MCS (Pension) Rules 1982
- **Not fetched; number not verified.** Its existence and content are reflected in the Higher & Technical Education Dept GR below, which extends the same benefits to universities/colleges. Petitioners and departments cite it as the GR by which the State extended (a) family pension and death gratuity on death in service, (b) invalid pension and gratuity on invalidation, and (c) retirement gratuity on retirement, to employees covered by DCPS/NPS, with the ex-gratia (सानुग्रह अनुदान) scheme discontinued.

## 2. H&TE Dept GR संकीर्ण-2304/प्र.क्र.87/विशि-1, dated 25.05.2023 — same benefits for DCPS/NPS employees of non-agricultural universities & aided colleges
- Fetched: https://www.unishivaji.ac.in/uploads/admin/2023/Circulars/June/20-6-23/GR%2025-05-2023.pdf (copy hosted by Shivaji University, a State university; verify on gr.maharashtra.gov.in)
- **Verbatim (Marathi):**
  > "सेवेत असतांना मृत्यू झाल्यास त्याच्या कुटुंबाला कुटुंबनिवृत्ति वेतन आणि मृत्यू उपदान"
  > "सानुग्रह अनुदान योजना या शासन निर्णयाच्या दिनांकापासून बंद करण्यात येत आहे"
  > "नमुना-3 मधील विकल्प कार्यालय प्रमुखाकडे सादर करावा लागेल" (families of those who died 01.11.2005–GR date may opt for arrears + regular family pension, adjusting ex-gratia already paid with Govt contribution and returns)
  > "शासन निर्णयाच्या दिनांकापासून एक महिन्याच्या आत" (existing NPS employees to file option; new recruits within 8 days of joining)
  > "नमुना-1 प्रमाणे कुटुंबाचा तपशील सादर करणे अनिवार्य आहे"
- Summary: benefits of the 1982 Rules (family pension, death gratuity, invalid pension/gratuity, retirement gratuity) extended to DCPS/NPS employees appointed on/after 01.11.2005; ex-gratia scheme closed; option-based.

## 3. FD GR रानिप्र-2024/प्र.क्र.35/सेवा-4, dated 30.05.2024 — counting prior service for service/death gratuity of NPS employees
- Fetched: https://mahapolice.gov.in/uploads/e03d8bd64ed679e7957a7d3168b02769.pdf (signed Manisha Yuvraj Kamte, Dy Secy)
- **Verbatim:** > "पूर्वीची सेवा सध्याच्या पदास जोडून देण्यास परवानगी देता येईल" subject to conditions:
  (अ) earlier appointment was regular and through proper channel per recruitment rules — > "नियमित स्वरुपाची वैध मार्गाने झालेली असावी";
  (ब) probation in earlier post satisfactorily completed (or counted once confirmed in later post);
  (क) application for the new post made with prior permission of competent authority;
  (ड) gap between the two appointments not more than joining time admissible on transfer.
- Procedure: administrative department verifies and sends proposal with justification to FD for approval. Applies mutatis mutandis to ZP, aided schools/colleges, agricultural universities subject to orders of concerned departments.

## How it is used in litigation
- Petitioners (families of NPS employees who died in service; NPS retirees) claim: family pension & gratuity under 1982 Rules retrospectively from 01.11.2005 with interest; counting of ad-hoc/contract service before regular appointment for gratuity; refund of employer contribution plus pension; OPS parity (esp. employees selected before 01.11.2005 but appointed later).
- State's stand: NPS employees are governed by the DCPS/NPS GRs and not by the 1982 Rules except to the extent extended by GR 31.03.2023/25.05.2023 on option; prior service counts only if the four conditions of GR 30.05.2024 are met and FD approves; the cut-off 01.11.2005 is a valid policy classification; Bombay HC rulings excluding judicial officers from NPS (All India Judges Assn. framework) do not extend to non-judicial staff.

## मराठी सारांश
- दि. 01.11.2005 नंतर नियुक्त (DCPS/NPS) कर्मचाऱ्यांना सेवेत मृत्यू झाल्यास कुटुंब निवृत्तिवेतन व मृत्यू उपदान, रुग्णता निवृत्तिवेतन व सेवानिवृत्ती उपदान — वित्त विभाग शा.नि. 31.03.2023 (पडताळलेला नाही) व उच्च व तंत्र शिक्षण विभाग शा.नि. 25.05.2023 (विद्यापीठे/महाविद्यालये); सानुग्रह अनुदान योजना बंद; विकल्प एक महिन्यात.
- पूर्वसेवा उपदानासाठी जोडण्याच्या अटी — शा.नि. 30.05.2024 (नियमित नियुक्ती, परिविक्षा, पूर्वपरवानगी, सेवाखंड नसणे).
- अंशदान कपात कार्यपद्धती — शा.नि. 30.12.2024; प्रतिनियुक्तीवरील कर्मचारी — शा.नि. 10.07.2025.
