## 4. How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner claims:** (i) DA arrears for the period between effective date and cash-payment date (e.g. Nov 2025–Jan 2026 gap where "separate orders" were promised); (ii) DA on the Central pattern from the same date as the Centre; (iii) DA to contractual / daily-wage / consolidated-pay employees; (iv) DA on 6th CPC scale where pay fixation under 7th CPC is disputed.
- **State's usual stand:** DA is a policy decision of the Finance Department taken with reference to financial position; the effective date and the mode of payment (cash from a later date, arrears by instalments/separate orders) are within executive discretion; no vested right to DA from the Central date; consolidated-pay/contract staff are governed by their contract and not by FD DA GRs; the GRs apply only to "full-time State Government employees and other eligible employees" drawing pay in the pay matrix.
- For court staff: DA GRs apply to subordinate court staff as State Government employees under the L&J Department's establishment; the High Court Registry draws on the same FD GRs.
