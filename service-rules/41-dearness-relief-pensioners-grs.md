## 3. How it is used in litigation
- Pensioners/family pensioners (incl. retired court staff) claim DR arrears for the gap between effective date and cash-payment date, or DR on the Central pattern. State's stand: DR follows DA policy; payment timing is a financial-policy decision; no discrimination since all State pensioners are treated alike; AG/Treasury computes individual entitlement.
- NPS/DCPS retirees have no "pension" in the 1982 Rules sense, hence no DR — relevant to NPS-family-pension claims (see file 46).
