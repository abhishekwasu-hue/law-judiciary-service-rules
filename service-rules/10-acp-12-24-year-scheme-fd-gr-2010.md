### Key provisions — summary (verify against original)
- The scheme (commonly called "सुधारित सेवांतर्गत आश्वासित प्रगती योजना" after the 6th Pay Commission) granted **two financial up-gradations**: the first on completion of **12 years** of regular service without a functional promotion, and the second on completion of **24 years** (or 12 years after the first benefit) without a second promotion.
- Benefit = pay in the pay band + grade pay of the **next promotional post** in the employee's hierarchy (for employees in a promotion chain), or the **next higher grade pay** for employees on isolated posts (एकाकी पदे).
- Conditions usually attached (as carried forward into the 2019 GR): the employee must satisfy all conditions required for regular promotion — prescribed departmental examination, qualifying ACR grading, no pending departmental inquiry / criminal case (per GAD GR 15.12.2017, एसआरव्ही-2015/प्र.क्र.310/काया-12), and caste validity certificate where applicable; DPC must verify.
- Refusal of regular promotion: under the central MACP pattern, an employee who refuses promotion is debarred from the financial up-gradation for a specified period — **whether the Maharashtra GR contains an identical bar is not verified**; verify the GR text before pleading.
- Earlier scheme lineage (verified numbers from the 2019 citation list): GAD GR एसआरव्ही-1095/प्र.क्र.1/95/बाबा dated 08.06.1995 and 01.11.1995; GAD GR 20.03.1997; FD GR वेतन 1199/प्र.क्र.2/99/सेवा-3 dated 20.07.2001; FD GR वेतन 2002/प्र.क्र.1/सेवा-3 dated 11.01.2002; FD GR वेतन 1109/प्र.क्र.1/09/सेवा-3 dated 15.10.2009 (6th CPC transition); FD GR 21.05.2010 (वेतन 1109/प्र.क्र.1/सेवा-3); FD GR 05.07.2010 (वेतन 1109/प्र.क्र.41/सेवा-3); FD GR 01.07.2011 (वेतन 1111/प्र.क्र.8/सेवा-3) with corrigenda of 09.12.2016 and 11.05.2017; FD GRs आप्रयो 1012/प्र.क्र.71/सेवा-3 dated 19.01.2013 and आप्रयो 1014/प्र.क्र.21/सेवा-3 dated 06.09.2014 (reiterations/clarifications), 10.02.2015, 10.12.2015, 23.12.2015, 03.03.2016, 16.07.2016.

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's typical claims:** (a) completed 12/24 years but benefit denied for want of departmental exam / "not fit"; (b) benefit refused because post is "isolated" though a promotional hierarchy exists; (c) delayed grant with arrears and interest; (d) court staff claim parity with Mantralaya staff.
- **State's usual stand:** the scheme is a non-functional financial benefit, not a promotion; eligibility is identical to promotion eligibility (exam, ACR, no DE); benefit is counted per hierarchy under the recruitment rules; refusal of promotion / unwillingness forfeits the benefit; after 01.01.2016 the 12/24 scheme stands replaced and claims are to be examined under the 10-20-30 GR with transition counting (12+8=20, 24+6=30).
- For court staff, the High Court administration (not the Law & Judiciary Dept.) is the sanctioning authority; the State's affidavit generally states that the FD scheme is extended to judiciary staff through the High Court and that fixation/sanction lies with the Registrar General / Principal District Judge.

### मराठी सारांश
- वित्त विभागाचा दि. ०१.०४.२०१० चा शासन निर्णय (क्र. वेतन 1109/प्र.क्र.44/सेवा-3) हा १२ व २४ वर्षांनंतर पदोन्नती न मिळाल्यास पुढील पदाचा ग्रेड पे देणारी योजना आहे.
- पदोन्नतीसाठीच्या सर्व अटी (विभागीय परीक्षा, गोपनीय अहवाल, विभागीय चौकशी नसणे) लागू.
- दि. ०१.०१.२०१६ पासून ही योजना १०-२०-३० योजनेने (वि.वि. शा.नि. ०२.०३.२०१९) बदलली; जुने लाभ १२+८=२०, २४+६=३० असे मोजले जातात.
- मूळ शासन निर्णयाची प्रत gr.maharashtra.gov.in वरून तपासून घ्यावी (या फाईलमध्ये फक्त क्रमांक/दिनांक पडताळलेले आहेत).
