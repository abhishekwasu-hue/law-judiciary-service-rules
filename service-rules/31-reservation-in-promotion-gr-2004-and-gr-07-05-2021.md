**Provisions (summary of fetched text; quotes marked):**
1. Supersedes the GR of 20.04.2021.
2. Records that in W.P. 2797/2015 the Bombay High Court on 04.08.2017 fixed the reservation period in promotion and that the Supreme Court has not stayed that judgment.
3. All vacant posts in the promotion quota are to be filled **by seniority as on 25.05.2004** — quote: "दिनांक 25.05.2004 च्या स्थितीनुसार सेवाज्येष्ठतेने भरण्यात यावीत".
4. Backward-class employees who gained seniority through promotion-reservation under GR 25.05.2004: (a) if in service on or before 25.05.2004 — eligible for further promotion on their seniority as on 25.05.2004; (b) if joined after 25.05.2004 — on their original seniority from date of entry.
5. These promotions are **purely temporary** and subject to the final decision of the Supreme Court in **SLP (C) No. 28306/2017**.
6. **No reversion** (पदावनती) of any backward-class employee already promoted under reservation while implementing this.

**Earlier interim arrangement:** GAD GR **एसआरव्ही-2018/प्र.क्र.159/काया.-12 dated 01.08.2019** (cited in Home Dept. orders) — summary not verified; understood to permit temporary promotions on the 67% open-quota posts / subject to court decision. Verify content before citing.

**Standard conditions in promotion orders issued under this regime (verbatim from Home Dept. order 14.08.2025, OCR-cleaned):** "उक्त पदोन्नती ही विशेष अनुमती याचिका क्र.28306/2017 या प्रकरणात मा. सर्वोच्च न्यायालयाच्या न्यायनिर्णय व आदेश यांच्या अधीन राहून देण्यात येत आहे." ... "उक्त पदोन्नती ही निव्वळ तात्पुरत्या स्वरुपाची असल्याने या तात्पुरत्या पदोन्नतीमुळे संबंधित अधिकारी यांना नियमाधीनतेचा (regularization) व सेवाज्येष्ठतेचा (seniority) कोणताही हक्क असणार नाही."

### How it is used in litigation (court staff context)
- **Petitioner's claims:** (i) reserved-category court employee: denial of promotion reservation under s. 5 of the Act / GR 25.05.2004; demand for collection of quantifiable data; challenge to GR 07.05.2021; (ii) open-category employee: challenge to promotions given to reserved candidates after 04.08.2017; demand for reversion / re-drawing of seniority; (iii) demand that temporary promotions be regularised with seniority.
- **State's / High Court's stand:** promotions after 2017 are made strictly by seniority as on 25.05.2004 under GR 07.05.2021, are temporary and expressly subject to SLP 28306/2017; no reversion of already promoted reserved employees (para 6); temporary promotion confers no right to seniority/regularisation; the matter is sub judice before the Supreme Court — High Court should not pass final orders; for court staff, promotions are made by the High Court / District Judge as appointing authority applying the Government's reservation policy mutatis mutandis.

### मराठी सारांश
- सा.प्र.वि. शा.नि. दि. २५.०५.२००४ (क्र. 2001/1887/प्र.क्र.64/01/16-ब) नुसार पदोन्नतीतील आरक्षण; मुंबई उच्च न्यायालयाने रिट याचिका २७९७/२०१५ मध्ये दि. ०४.०८.२०१७ रोजी निर्णय दिला; शासनाची SLP (C) 28306/2017 सर्वोच्च न्यायालयात प्रलंबित, स्थगिती नाही.
- सा.प्र.वि. शा.नि. दि. ०७.०५.२०२१ (क्र. बीसीसी-2018/प्र.क्र.366/16-ब): पदोन्नती कोट्यातील रिक्त पदे दि. २५.०५.२००४ च्या स्थितीनुसार सेवाज्येष्ठतेने, निव्वळ तात्पुरत्या स्वरूपात, SLP च्या निकालाच्या अधीन भरावीत; आधी पदोन्नत झालेल्या मागासवर्गीयांची पदावनती नाही.
