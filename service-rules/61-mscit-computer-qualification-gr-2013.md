### Key provisions (summary of fetched text; quote marked)
- **Mandatory MS-CIT:** "राज्य शासनाच्या गट अ, ब अणि क मधील कर्मचाऱ्यांना MSCIT ... प्रमाणपत्र धारण करणे अनिवार्य आहे." (Group A, B and C employees must hold the MS-CIT certificate.)
- **Equivalent qualifications** deemed to satisfy the test: DOEACC/NIELIT certificates (CCC etc.); SSC/HSC with IT/computer subjects (Std. 9–12, State Board or CBSE); MSBTE diploma/degree in Computer Technology/Engineering/IT and listed advanced diplomas; equivalent university degrees; certificates of the **86 computer courses** listed in Appendix 'A' conducted through the Vocational Examination Board.
- Time limit to acquire the qualification after appointment, consequences of failure (stoppage of increment / non-confirmation) and age-based exemption (50+): **not in the fetched text** — these flow from the earlier GR of 19.03.2003 and the recruitment rules; **not verified**.

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims:** increment withheld / confirmation or promotion denied for want of MS-CIT; claim that a degree/diploma is equivalent; exemption by age; court staff claim that the High Court never notified the requirement.
- **State's stand:** MS-CIT or listed equivalent is a condition of service under GR 19.03.2003 as revised by GR 04.02.2013 and incorporated in recruitment rules (e.g. Rule 8, 17.03.2018 notification); the employee must acquire it within the prescribed period; equivalence only for listed qualifications; for court staff, the requirement is as incorporated in the High Court's/District Courts' recruitment rules.
