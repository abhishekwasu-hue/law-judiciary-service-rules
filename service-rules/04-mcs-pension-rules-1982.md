## A. MCS (Pension) Rules, 1982 — summary (verify against original)

### Chapter I–II: Preliminary, general conditions
- **Rule 2 – Application**: Government servants under the rule-making control of the Governor appointed substantively/temporarily to civil posts on or after 16.08.1981 (earlier retirees under Bombay Civil Services Rules). **Rule 2 excludes persons recruited on/after 01.11.2005** (amendment via FD notification PEN-1005/124/SER-4 of **31.10.2005**, as recorded in the Commutation Rules footnote "Rule 2(2) added, 31 October 2005 (PEN 1005/124/SER-4)" ✔).
- **Rule 9 – Definitions** (summary): "emoluments", "qualifying service", "pension" (includes gratuity except where distinguished), "family", "Head of Office", "Audit Officer" (AG), "pension sanctioning authority", "provisional pension".
- **Rule 20 ff.** (summary): pension subject to future good conduct; pensioner convicted of serious crime or guilty of grave misconduct may have pension withheld/withdrawn.
- **Rule 27 – Right of Government to withhold or withdraw pension** ✔(number per standard usage; mirrors CCS Rule 9) (summary): (1) Government reserves the right to withhold a pension or gratuity, or both, in full or in part, **permanently or for a specified period**, and to **order recovery** from pension/gratuity of the whole or part of any pecuniary loss caused to Government, if in **departmental or judicial proceedings** the pensioner is found guilty of **grave misconduct or negligence** during service including re-employed service. (2) Departmental proceedings (a) if instituted while in service — deemed to continue after retirement as if the employee had continued in service; (b) if instituted after retirement — only with **sanction of Government**, only in respect of an event which took place **not more than four years before** institution, and conducted by the authority and in the manner applicable to proceedings while in service. (3) Judicial proceedings if not instituted while in service — deemed instituted on the date the complaint/report of police is made / plaint presented. (4) The **Maharashtra Public Service Commission is consulted** before final orders. (5) **No pension can be withheld on grounds of pendency** of proceedings alone — provisional pension under Rule 130 ✔ is payable. (Also note: "grave misconduct" includes communication of official secrets, per Explanation.)
- **Rule 30 – Commencement of qualifying service** (summary): qualifying service of a Government servant commences from the date he takes charge of the post to which he is first appointed substantively or in an officiating/temporary capacity **provided the officiating/temporary service is followed without interruption by substantive appointment** / confirmation. Service **below 18 years** of age and **apprentice service** do not count (specific rules). Casual/daily-wage service does not count unless regularised and specifically allowed by Government order.
- **Rules 31–50 – Conditions / counting of service** (summary, headings as commonly cited): conditions subject to which service qualifies (31); counting of service on probation (33); service as apprentice (34); service on contract (35); pre-retirement civil service re-employed (36); military service (37–38); war service (39); periods of **suspension** (count only if regularised as duty/leave; Rule 45 ✔?); periods of **leave** — all leave with pay counts, **extraordinary leave without pay counts only if on medical certificate or sanctioned for a reason beyond control/higher studies** (Rule 46 ✔?); deputation/foreign service (47); **interruptions and condonation** of breaks (Rules 48–49 — interruption condoned up to the limit prescribed; **resignation, dismissal, removal** entail forfeiture of past service unless withdrawn/set aside; **unauthorised absence** is an interruption unless regularised); addition to qualifying service (**Rule 66A** — *deleted w.e.f. 01.01.2006* by the 7th-CPC GR ✔).
- **Rule 58–62** (summary): verification of qualifying service after 25 years / 5 years before retirement; certificate of verification; **Rules 121–123**: preparation of pension papers by Head of Office two years before retirement (ZP Jalgaon checklist titles "as per Rule 121 to 123" — official district site).

### Chapter VII: Classes of pension
- **Rule 63 – Superannuation pension** ✔ (summary): granted to an employee who retires on attaining the age of superannuation (58 years for Groups A–C and now 60 years per GAD GR of 2020-21 and 60 years for Group D — age is governed by Rule 10 of MCS (General Conditions of Service) Rules 1981, not the Pension Rules).
- **Rule 64 – Retiring pension** ✔ (summary): on retirement before superannuation under Rules 65/66.
- **Rule 65 – Retirement on completion of 20 years' qualifying service** ✔ (summary): a Government servant may retire **voluntarily** after **20 years' qualifying service** by giving **three months' notice** in writing to the appointing authority; the notice may be withdrawn only with permission; retirement under this rule requires **acceptance** by the appointing authority (deemed acceptance if not refused before the notice expires); not available to an employee under suspension except with specific approval; weightage (addition of up to five years) stood deleted with Rule 66A from 01.01.2006.
- **Rule 66 – Government's right to retire in public interest** ✔ (summary): the appointing authority may, after giving three months' notice or pay in lieu, require an employee to retire in **public interest** after he has completed **(a) 50 years of age (Group A/B who entered service before 35) or 55 years** / or 30 years' qualifying service, per review (compulsory retirement review as per GAD GR; not a penalty).
- **Rule 67 – Pension on absorption** ✔ in PSU/autonomous body; **Rule 81 – Compensation pension** ✔ (abolition of post); **Rule 82 ff. – Invalid pension**; **Rule 86 ff. – Compulsory retirement pension** (as penalty: pension not less than two-thirds and not more than full compensation pension); **Rule 89 – Compassionate allowance** on dismissal/removal (not exceeding two-thirds of pension/gratuity).

### Chapter VIII: Emoluments and amount of pension
- **Rule 108 – Emoluments** (number: verify; summary): "emoluments" means **basic pay** (pay in pay band + grade pay under 2009 Rules; **pay in the Pay Matrix** under 2019 Rules, per para 4 of GR 01.03.2019 ✔) which the employee **was receiving immediately before retirement or death**, including NPA for medical officers; excludes special pay/allowances unless specifically ordered (Naxalite-area extra pay excluded ✔). Pay during leave preceding retirement = pay that would have been drawn had he not been on leave; pay during suspension not counted unless regularised.
- **Rule 109 – Average emoluments** (number: verify; summary): average of emoluments drawn during the **last ten months** of service (with adjustments for leave/suspension).
- **Rule 110 – Amount of pension** ✔:
  - **110(1)** ✔: **qualifying service less than 10 years** — no pension; **service gratuity** only (half month's emoluments for every completed six-monthly period). Benefits limited to permanent employees (temporary employees with 10+ years also get pension per Rule 2 amendments — verify).
  - **110(2)(a)** ✔: pension at **50% of last basic pay or average emoluments of last 10 months, whichever is more beneficial**, on completing **10 years' qualifying service** (the earlier requirement of **33 years** for full pension and proportionate pension for less was **removed w.e.f. 01.01.2006** ✔ by the 6th-CPC pension GR and confirmed by GR 01.03.2019).
  - **Minimum pension**: Rs **7,500 p.m.** (7th CPC, from 01.01.2016) and **maximum** = 50% of the highest pay (Rs 2,20,000) = **Rs 1,10,000 p.m.** ✔ (GR 01.03.2019 states maximum = 50% of highest pay; Rs 7,500 minimum is from the pre-2016 pensioners GR — number not verified here; verify).
  - **Additional pension for aged pensioners**: 80–85 yrs +20%, 85–90 +30%, 90–95 +40%, 95–100 +50%, 100+ +100% of basic pension (introduced for Maharashtra w.e.f. **01.04.2014** per AG list "10% pension increase for pensioners aged 80+ from 1.4.2014"; revised scale under 7th CPC GR — verify figures).
- **Rule 111 – Retirement gratuity and death gratuity** ✔:
  - **111(1) Retirement gratuity** ✔: **one-fourth of emoluments for each completed six-monthly period** of qualifying service, subject to a maximum of **16½ times emoluments** or **Rs 14,00,000**, whichever is less (7th CPC, from 01.01.2016 ✔; earlier ceilings Rs 3.5 lakh (5th CPC) and Rs 7 lakh (6th CPC, from 01.01.2006)). Minimum qualifying service for retirement gratuity: 5 years.
  - **111(2) Death gratuity** ✔ (table from GR 01.03.2019): service < 1 yr — 2 × emoluments; 1–<5 yrs — 6 ×; 5–<11 yrs — 12 ×; 11–<20 yrs — 20 ×; 20 yrs+ — half of emoluments for every completed six-monthly period, max 33 × emoluments; overall cap **Rs 14 lakh** ✔ ("Maximum limit of death gratuity will be Rupees Fourteen lakhs").
  - "Emoluments" for gratuity include **DA** admissible on the date of retirement/death (verify).
  - **Rule 112–115**: nomination for gratuity; persons to whom gratuity is payable; lapse to Government.
- **Rule 116 – Family pension** ✔ (Family Pension Scheme 1964): (1) payable to the family of an employee who dies in service after one year's service (or earlier if medically examined) or after retirement while in receipt of pension; **(2) rate: uniform 30% of pay** (7th CPC ✔), minimum Rs 7,500 (verify), maximum 30% of highest pay = **Rs 66,000** ✔; **(4)(a)(i) enhanced family pension on death in service**: **50% of pay** for **10 years from death** — the earlier age condition (till the deceased would have reached 65/67) **deleted** w.e.f. 01.01.2016 ✔; **(4)(a)(ii) death after retirement**: enhanced rate (50% of pay or pension whichever is less) for **7 years or till 67 years of age**, whichever earlier — unchanged ✔; order of eligibility: widow/widower, then children (son till 25 / daughter till marriage or 25 / unmarried-widowed-divorced daughter and disabled children for life), dependent parents (as per later amendment GRs — verify).
- **Rule 117–120**: dearness relief; **Rule 118–119**: two pensions / family pension plus pension.

### Chapter IX–X: Sanction, provisional pension, recovery
- **Rules 121–128** (summary): preparation of pension papers two years before retirement (121–123 ✔ per ZP Jalgaon checklist); forwarding to the Accountant General six months before; **Rule 126** ✔ (provisional pension where papers are delayed — 100% of pension admissible, for six months, extendable); authorisation by AG; **Rule 128**: payment of pension/gratuity on the date of retirement — delay attracts interest per FD orders (verify rule for interest on delayed gratuity).
- **Rule 129** (summary): withholding of gratuity pending assessment of Government dues (licence fee, overpayment, advances); **Rule 130 – Provisional pension where departmental or judicial proceedings are pending** ✔: the AG/pension sanctioning authority shall authorise **provisional pension not exceeding the maximum pension** admissible (in practice 100%) for the period till conclusion of proceedings; **no gratuity** till conclusion (gratuity is withheld, and per Government decisions 100% of pension may be provisionally paid); on conclusion, final orders under Rule 27; **no recovery of provisional pension** already paid except where pension is reduced/withheld.
- **Rules 131–135 (recovery)** (summary): **recovery of Government dues from gratuity** — only dues ascertained and assessed (demands for licence fee, overpayment of pay/allowances, advances, loss to Government); recovery **requires the pensioner's consent in writing** or an order under Rule 27 (post-retirement recovery otherwise barred — *State of Punjab v. Rafiq Masih* (2015) principle applied by Bombay HC against recovery from Group C/D retirees for excess paid without fraud); **Rule 134/135** ✔?: dues to be intimated and adjusted before issue of PPO; **Rule 140** ✔ (provisional family pension).
- **Rule 141 ff.** (summary): payment of pension; **commutation** governed by the 1984 Rules; **restoration** after 15 years (Commutation Rules 8A ✔).

## B. MCS (Commutation of Pension) Rules, 1984 — verbatim/near-verbatim (official FD publication, fetched)
- Rule 2: applies to Government servants entitled to service pension under Chapter VII of the 1982 Rules; **Rule 2(2)** (added 31.10.2005, PEN-1005/124/SER-4) excludes those **recruited on or after 1 November 2005**.
- Rule 4: no commutation while departmental/judicial proceedings are pending.
- **Rule 5(1)** (verbatim phrase): a Government servant may "commute for a lump sum payment a fraction not exceeding" **forty per cent** of his pension (40% substituted w.e.f. **27.02.2009**; earlier one-third).
- Rule 6(1) (verbatim phrase): "The commutation of pension shall become absolute" — on the date the Head of Office receives the Form A application (without medical examination) / date of medical authority's signature (with medical); reduction in pension from the earlier of receipt of commuted value or three months after AG authorisation.
- Rule 7: death before receiving commuted value — paid to heirs.
- **Rule 8(1)** (verbatim phrase): lump sum "shall be calculated in accordance with the Table of the values prescribed from time to time" (Table I; **Table II added w.e.f. 27.02.2009**); **Rule 8(2)** (verbatim phrase): "A commutation once applied for and given effect to cannot be rescinded".
- **Rule 8A(1)(a)** (verbatim phrase): "his pension shall be restored after completion of fifteen years of his retirement" (inserted w.e.f. 30.05.1988); legal heirs may claim arrears; **Rule 8B** (26.05.1997): revision of restored one-third for PSU absorbees per GRs.
- Rule 9: provisional pension — commutation of up to 10% of basic pension for medical purposes unless otherwise indicated.
- Rule 10: on retrospective revision of pension, difference in commuted value payable; up to 40% of revised final pension may be commuted; six-month window for pay-commission revision differences.
- Rules 11–15: commutation without medical examination for superannuation (R.63), retiring (R.64–66), absorption (R.67), compensation (R.81) pensions; **Form A within one year of retirement**; Form B before retirement (superannuation cases); late applications in Form C with medical examination (Rules 17–31; medical authority R.22; fee R.23; second examination R.26; appeal R.27). Form F deleted 02.07.2015; Form G added 01.04.1985.
- **7th-CPC commutation** (GR 01.03.2019 para 8 ✔): 40% of revised basic pension × 12 × commutation value (Annexure I; age 60 = **8.287**, age 65 = 7.731); **effective 01.01.2019 only for those retiring on/after 01.01.2019**; those retired 01.01.2016–31.12.2018 initially excluded (para 8.2), later given revised commutation by **FD GR dated 05.02.2021 (सेनिवे-2019/प्र.क्र.348/सेवा-4)** ✔, supplementary letter **12.07.2021** ✔ and procedural circular **22.03.2022** ✔ (deadline 30.09.2022 for proposals to AG).

## How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner claims**: (a) **delay in pension/gratuity** — interest and compensation; (b) **withholding of gratuity/pension on pendency of DE or criminal case** — challenge that no proceedings were "instituted" before retirement or that the four-year bar under Rule 27(2)(b) applies, that DE was initiated without Government sanction after retirement, or that MPSC was not consulted; (c) **recovery of excess payment** from retiral benefits without consent (Rafiq Masih); (d) **counting of service** — ad hoc/contract/daily-wage/CRT period, suspension, EOL, break-in-service condonation; (e) **voluntary retirement** under Rule 65 refused or notice period dispute; (f) **family pension** to second wife/unmarried daughter/dependent parents; (g) 7th-CPC revision of pension, commutation and gratuity ceiling for 2016–18 retirees; (h) **notional increment** for pension (file 05); (i) DCPS employees claiming old pension (file 06).
- **State's usual stand**: pension is governed strictly by the 1982 Rules; provisional pension under Rule 130 is being paid, gratuity lawfully withheld till conclusion of proceedings instituted before retirement (charge-sheet date = institution); Rule 27 permits recovery of loss after a finding of grave misconduct; service not followed by regular appointment / not on a sanctioned post does not qualify (Rule 30); unauthorised absence/EOL without MC does not count; voluntary retirement needs acceptance; excess payment recovered only with consent or as per Rafiq Masih exceptions (Group A/B, fraud, within one year of retirement) — otherwise waived; 7th-CPC benefits given strictly per GR 01.03.2019 and the commutation GR 05.02.2021 (no interest for the interregnum); for court staff the High Court is the appointing/pension-sanctioning authority (Art. 229) but the State funds and the AG authorises — the State's affidavit pleads limited role and relies on AG/Treasury records.

## मराठी सारांश
महाराष्ट्र नागरी सेवा (निवृत्तिवेतन) नियम, १९८२ हे वित्त विभागाचे नियम असून दि. ०१.११.२००५ नंतर नियुक्त कर्मचाऱ्यांना लागू नाहीत. नियम २७ – गंभीर गैरवर्तणुकीबद्दल निवृत्तिवेतन रोखणे/वसुली (विभागीय चौकशी निवृत्तीनंतर सुरू करायची असल्यास शासनाची मंजुरी व चार वर्षांची मर्यादा); नियम ३० – अर्हताकारी सेवेचा प्रारंभ; नियम ६३ – नियत वयोमानानुसार निवृत्ती; नियम ६५ – २० वर्षांनंतर स्वेच्छानिवृत्ती; नियम ११० – १० वर्षांनंतर अंतिम वेतनाच्या/१० महिन्यांच्या सरासरीच्या ५०% निवृत्तिवेतन; नियम १११ – उपदान (कमाल रु. १४ लाख); नियम ११६ – कुटुंब निवृत्तिवेतन ३०%; नियम १३० – चौकशी प्रलंबित असताना तात्पुरते निवृत्तिवेतन. अंशराशीकरण नियम १९८४ नुसार ४०% पर्यंत अंशराशीकरण व १५ वर्षांनी पुनःस्थापना. सातव्या वेतन आयोगाचे सुधारित दर वित्त विभाग शासन निर्णय दि. ०१.०३.२०१९ (सेनिवे-२०१९/प्र.क्र.५८/सेवा-४) मध्ये आहेत. मूळ नियमांचा मजकूर अधिकृत संकेतस्थळावर स्कॅन स्वरूपात असल्याने येथे सारांश दिला आहे – पडताळणी आवश्यक.
