# 40-E  Case-law: Pension, gratuity, retiral benefits, interest on delay, forfeiture/withholding, NPS vs old pension

**Scope**: Claims by retired court staff / their widows for (i) pension and gratuity withheld pending DE/criminal case, (ii) interest on delayed retiral dues, (iii) family pension arrears, (iv) parity of pension (cut-off dates), (v) Old Pension Scheme (OPS) for those recruited around 01.11.2005 (DCPS/NPS cut-off — library files 46–48). Rule references: Maharashtra Civil Services (Pension) Rules 1982 — Rr. 26–27 (withholding/withdrawal, DE after retirement), 118–125 (timely payment), 129–130 (provisional pension).

## 2. Case details

### 2.1 Deokinandan Prasad (1971) ✖ and D.S. Nakara (1982) ✖
- Deokinandan: pension is a right, not bounty; a writ lies to enforce it. Used by petitioners for the proposition that pension cannot be denied without authority of rules.
- Nakara: cut-off date for liberalised pension (31.03.1979) struck down; all pensioners entitled to the liberalised formula from the specified date (not retrospective arrears). **State's reading** (and the SC's later gloss in *Indian Ex-Services League* (1991) 2 SCC 104, *K.L. Rathee* (1997) 6 SCC 7, *Union of India v. SPS Vains* (2008) 9 SCC 125, *UOI v. All India Services Pensioners' Assn* (1988)): Nakara does not mandate that every new benefit must be extended to past retirees; a cut-off tied to a pay-commission date / rational basis is valid. Petitioners use Nakara whenever a GR (e.g. 7th CPC pension GR 01.03.2019; additional pension GRs — library files 44–45) draws a date line.

### 2.2 State of Kerala v. M. Padmanabhan Nair (1985) ✖; Dr. Uma Agrawal (1999) ✖
- Delay in paying pension/gratuity attracts interest; officers responsible can be made personally liable. In Maharashtra, the 1982 Rules (Rr. 118–125) and FD circular 2025 (library file 48) prescribe timelines; where pension papers were delayed by the office (District Court/High Court establishment), petitioners claim interest at 9–18%.
- **State uses**: interest is not automatic — not payable where delay is attributable to the employee (non-submission of papers, pending recovery/DE, unsettled dues) or where provisional pension was paid; the rate, if any, is in the Court's discretion (usually 6–9%). Plead the dates of receipt and sanction of pension papers.

### 2.3 S.K. Mastan Bee (2002) ✖ and Maya Banerjee (2026) ◐
- Mastan Bee: illiterate widow of a railway gangman; the employer's duty to inform her of family pension; arrears from the date of death with 12% interest. Maya Banerjee (Sept 2026, per LiveLaw) followed it and declined to apply Tarsem Singh's three-year cap in a widow's case where the delay was not her fault (date-of-death dispute and wrongful post-death dismissal).
- **Petitioners use**: widows of Group C/D court staff claim family pension arrears from death. **State's answer**: distinguish — where the family was informed / had applied and was paid, or where the delay was the claimant's, Tarsem Singh applies; Mastan Bee is confined to cases of uninformed illiterate dependants.

### 2.4 State of Jharkhand v. Jitendra Kumar Srivastava (SC, 14.08.2013) ✔
- Source: https://hcraj.nic.in/sci/2013/3-6770-2013_2013-08-14.pdf (C.A. 6770/2013 with 6771/2013).
- Facts: Bihar/Jharkhand withheld 10% pension and full gratuity pending criminal proceedings relying on a circular; no rule authorised it.
- **Verbatim ¶7**: "It is an accepted position that gratuity and pension are not the bounties." **¶8**: "It is thus hard earned benefit which accrues to an employee and is in the nature of 'property'." … right "cannot be taken away without the due process of law". **¶11**: "There is no provision in the rules for withholding of the pension/ gratuity." **¶14**: "A person cannot be deprived of this pension without the authority of law." **¶15**: "The executive instructions are not having statutory character and, therefore, cannot be termed as 'law'." — the State therefore "cannot withhold even a part of pension or gratuity" on the basis of such a circular.
- **Maharashtra position**: the MCS (Pension) Rules 1982 are statutory (Art. 309). Rule 27 permits the Government to withhold/withdraw pension (whole or part) and order recovery of pecuniary loss where the pensioner is found guilty of grave misconduct/negligence in a DE/judicial proceeding (including one instituted after retirement, subject to the 4-year limit on the event and sanction of the Governor for post-retirement DE). Rule 130 provides provisional pension during pendency; gratuity may be withheld till conclusion. Hence Jitendra Srivastava is **distinguishable** where the withholding is under Rr. 27/130 (as in *Mahanadi Coalfields* and *Haresh Banerjee*). It applies **against** the State where (a) no DE/judicial proceeding was pending at retirement (or was not deemed instituted — charge-sheet not issued before retirement), (b) the proceeding relates to an event more than 4 years before its institution, (c) gratuity is withheld merely for "pending audit objections/recovery" without a rule, or (d) withholding rests on a GR/circular alone.

### 2.5 Mahanadi Coalfields v. Rabindranath Choubey (2020) ✖; Dev Prakash Tewari (2014) ✖; Haresh C. Banerjee (2006) ✖
- Choubey (three-judge): where the standing orders/rules allow DE to continue after superannuation and permit forfeiture of gratuity as punishment, the employer may withhold gratuity during the DE; Jitendra Srivastava distinguished on the existence of such rules.
- Dev Prakash Tewari: no rule → DE lapses on retirement → full benefits.
- Haresh Banerjee: Rule 10 of W.B. pension rules (withholding after conviction/grave misconduct) is a valid "law" for Art. 300A.
- **State uses**: these three together with Rule 27/130 of the 1982 Rules and GAD GRs on post-retirement DE (library file 52) justify provisional pension and withheld gratuity when a DE/criminal case was pending on the date of retirement.

### 2.6 M.L. Patil v. State of Goa (SC, 20.05.2022) ✔
- Source: https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2020/28175/28175_2020_10_1507_36042_Judgement_20-May-2022.pdf
- Bombay HC (Goa Bench) employee; retirement age dispute; SC: "the High Court has erred in observing that the appellant will not be entitled to any arrears of pension" (¶3); "as far as the pension is concerned, it is a continuous cause of action" (¶3); entitled "to pension at the revised rates from the date he attains the age of 60 years" (¶4). (The Goa pension-rule episode for Bombay HC employees that followed — Goa amended its rules after SC's displeasure, 2024–25 — is reported in Bar & Bench/Verdictum ◐.)
- **Use**: petitioners — pension as continuing cause; State — note that even here arrears were tied to a specific date, and three-year cap arguments remain available for other claims.

### 2.7 NPS / OPS in Maharashtra — Khillari (W.P. 2270/2021, 28.04.2023) ✖ and related Bombay HC decisions ◐
- Maharashtra DCPS GR 31.10.2005 (NPS for employees appointed on/after 01.11.2005); later GRs on option, family pension/gratuity for NPS employees (31.03.2023 / 25.05.2023), counting of prior service (30.05.2024), Revised NPS/UPS (20.09.2024) — library files 46–47.
- Pattern of Bombay HC rulings (from the cases located ◐): (i) *Anjali Tanksale* / Full Bench *Deshmukh Dilipkumar Bhagwan* — aided-school teachers appointed before 01.11.2005 get OPS; (ii) *Augustine Anthony Thomas* (Nagpur, 2025) — date of actual appointment governs, not later administrative approval; (iii) Khillari (per department brief) — selection process initiated before the cut-off → OPS. The common thread: the cut-off is applied to the **date the employee's right accrued (appointment/selection)**, and the State's administrative delay cannot shift an employee into NPS.
- **State's stand** (file 46/47): cut-off 01.11.2005 is a valid policy classification (*Nakara* does not apply to a new scheme for new entrants — cf. SC decisions upholding NPS cut-offs); appointment order date is the operative date; prior ad-hoc/contract service is not "appointment"; the FD one-time option GR (2024) for pre-cut-off-advertised recruits (verify number) is the remedy, and those outside it cannot claim OPS; judicial-officer NPS exclusion (2017) rests on the All India Judges framework and does not extend to ministerial staff.
- **Petitioners use**: Khillari and the above line for staff whose selection by the District Judge/High Court was completed before 01.11.2005 but whose appointment orders issued later; also Art. 14 parity with colleagues from the same select list who got OPS.

## 3. Drafting checklist — pension matters
1. Date of retirement; whether any DE/criminal proceeding was *instituted* (charge-sheet served) before retirement; Rule 27 four-year limit; Governor's sanction for post-retirement DE.
2. Provisional pension paid? gratuity withheld under which rule? (cite Rule, not GR — Jitendra Srivastava ¶15).
3. Dates of submission/processing of pension papers (interest defence).
4. Family pension: when was the family informed/applied? (Mastan Bee vs Tarsem Singh).
5. OPS/NPS: advertisement date, select-list date, appointment-order date, joining date; option GR eligibility.
6. Cut-off challenges: show the rational basis (pay commission date, scheme for new entrants).

## 4. मराठी सारांश
- निवृत्तिवेतन व उपदान ही "मालमत्ता" (अनु. 300A) आहे, बक्षीस नव्हे (*देवकीनंदन प्रसाद*, *जितेंद्रकुमार श्रीवास्तव* ¶8, ¶14); ती **वैधानिक नियमाशिवाय** — केवळ परिपत्रक/शासननिर्णयाच्या आधारे — रोखता येत नाही (¶15).
- महाराष्ट्र नागरी सेवा (निवृत्तिवेतन) नियम 1982 चे नियम 27/130 वैधानिक असल्याने, निवृत्तीपूर्वी सुरू झालेल्या विभागीय/न्यायिक कारवाईत तात्पुरते निवृत्तिवेतन व उपदान रोखणे समर्थनीय (*महानदी कोलफील्ड्स*, *हरेश बॅनर्जी*); पण कारवाई नसल्यास/नियम नसल्यास पूर्ण लाभ (*देवप्रकाश तिवारी*).
- विलंबाने दिलेल्या निवृत्तिवेतनावर व्याज (*पद्मनाभन नायर*, *उमा अग्रवाल*) — विलंब कर्मचाऱ्याचा असल्यास बचाव; कुटुंबनिवृत्तिवेतन मृत्यूच्या तारखेपासून (*मस्तान बी*, *माया बॅनर्जी* 2026) — पण माहिती दिलेली असल्यास *तरसेम सिंग* ची 3 वर्षांची मर्यादा.
- *नाकारा*: कट-ऑफ तारीख मनमानी असल्यास अनु. 14 भंग; पण नव्या प्रवेशकांसाठी नवी योजना (NPS, 01.11.2005) हा वैध वर्गीकरणाचा बचाव.
- जुनी पेन्शन: मुंबई उच्च न्यायालयाचा कल — नियुक्ती/निवड 01.11.2005 पूर्वी झाली असल्यास प्रशासकीय विलंबामुळे NPS लागू होत नाही (*खिल्लारी* 2270/2021 — मजकूर पडताळावा; *तंकसाळे*, *ऑगस्टिन थॉमस*).
