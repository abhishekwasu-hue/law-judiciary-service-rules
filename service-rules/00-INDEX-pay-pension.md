# INDEX — Pay, Revised Pay, Increment and Pension rules of Maharashtra (files 01–06)
Prepared 28.10.2026 for use in affidavits/parawise replies in High Court service matters of non-judicial court employees. All content is drawn from official Government sources fetched on that date; where the official file was an image-only scan or blocked to the fetch tool, the text is marked "summary (verify against original)" and the official URL is given for verification. **No GR number or date has been invented — anything unverifiable is marked "not verified".**
