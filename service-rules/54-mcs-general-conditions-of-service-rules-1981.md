## Superannuation (age of retirement) — governed by MCS (Pension) Rules, 1982
- **Rule 10, MCS (Pension) Rules, 1982 (summary — verify)**: every Government servant retires on the afternoon of the **last day of the month** in which he attains the age of superannuation; **58 years** for Group A, B and C; **60 years** for **Group D (Class IV)**; (employee born on the 1st of a month retires on the last day of the preceding month). Officially restated on ZP Dharashiv Finance page: "वर्ग 3 च्या बाबतीत वयास 58 वर्ष व वर्ग 4 च्या बाबतीत 60 वर्ष पूर्ण झाल्यानंतर" (https://zpdharashiv.maharashtra.gov.in/%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4-%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%AD%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%97/).
- **GR raising Class IV retirement age from 58 to 60**: GAD GR of **1998** (commonly cited as dated 29.05.1998 / 09.06.1998) — **number and date NOT verified from an official page in this exercise; obtain from gad.maharashtra.gov.in "शासन सेवा"/"निवृत्तीवेतन" pages or gr.maharashtra.gov.in**.
- **Retirement age 60 for all Groups**: as of this research no notification raising the superannuation age of Group A–C State employees to 60 was found on official sites; several Cabinet-level proposals were reported in the press but **no GR/notification verified**. Teachers / medical teachers / specific cadres have separate GRs (e.g., medical teachers 60/62, 64 etc. — not verified). Do not plead 60 for Group A–C staff without verifying. Official GAD page (robots-blocked): https://gad.maharashtra.gov.in/%E0%A4%B6%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%A8-%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE/
- **Premature / compulsory retirement in public interest**: Rule 10(4) / Rule 65 of Pension Rules read with GAD review GRs (review at 50/55 years and after 30 years' qualifying service; GAD GR dt. 10.06.2019 on review of performance — **not verified**).

## How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner**: probation deemed completed by efflux of time / long officiating → confirmation; termination of probationer stigmatic; lien retained on parent post → repatriation; DOB correction on the basis of school leaving certificate; retirement date wrongly fixed (last day of month; born on 1st); Group D retirement at 60 wrongly denied to a Group C employee (e.g., ex-Class IV promoted to Class III); resignation withdrawal before acceptance.
- **State**: no deemed confirmation absent a declaration order / permanent post (Rule 9(14) and Appendix I); probation extended / terminated on assessment — not punitive; **Rule 38(2) five-year bar** on DOB alteration; superannuation under Pension Rule 10 is 58 for Group C and 60 only for Group D posts held at retirement; retirement age is a condition of service that the employee cannot claim as a vested right; resignation accepted under rules; medical fitness / Medical Board opinion final.

## मराठी सारांश
महाराष्ट्र नागरी सेवा (सेवेच्या सर्वसाधारण शर्ती) नियम, १९८१ (वित्त विभाग अधिसूचना क्र. एमएससी-१०८१/१/एमसीएसआर-सेल, दि. २३.०७.१९८१; अंमल १५.०८.१९८१; दि. ३०.१२.२०२१ पर्यंत सुधारित आवृत्ती अधिकृत संकेतस्थळावर) यात सेवाप्रवेशाची वयोमर्यादा (नियम १०), वैद्यकीय पात्रता (११–१७), धारणाधिकार (२०–२७), सेवापुस्तक व जन्मदिनांक (नियम ३८ — प्रवेशानंतर ५ वर्षांनी दुरुस्ती नाही, अधिसूचना दि. २४.१२.२००८), परिविक्षा/स्थायित्व (शासन निर्णय दि. ०२.०६.२००३, २७.०२.२००९, ०३.०३.२०१६) व राजीनामा यांच्या तरतुदी आहेत. सेवानिवृत्तीचे वय (गट अ–क: ५८; गट ड: ६०) हे महाराष्ट्र नागरी सेवा (निवृत्तिवेतन) नियम, १९८२ च्या नियम १० नुसार आहे; वर्ग-४ चे वय ६० करणाऱ्या १९९८ च्या शासन निर्णयाचा क्रमांक/दिनांक अधिकृतरीत्या पडताळता आला नाही आणि सर्व गटांचे वय ६० करणारी अधिसूचना आढळली नाही.
