# Daily-wage / work-charged / temporary employees — Kalelkar Award (1967), Converted Temporary Establishment, regularisation and the Umadevi principle
# रोजंदारी / कार्यव्ययी / अस्थायी कर्मचारी — कालेलकर पुरस्कार, रूपांतरित अस्थायी आस्थापना, सेवा नियमित करणे व उमादेवी तत्त्व

**Status:** Kalelkar Award (industrial award, in force since 1967) continues to govern PWD/Irrigation workers; State GRs regulate its benefits; Umadevi (2006) governs regularisation claims generally.

## 1. Supreme Court — The Secretary, PWD & Ors. v. Tukaram Pandurang Saraf & Ors., 2024 INSC 703, C.A. 1689/2016, decided 19.09.2024 (Sandeep Mehta & R. Mahadevan JJ.)
- Official source fetched: https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2016/4586/4586_2016_14_1502_55860_Judgement_19-Sep-2024.pdf
- Summary (verify): Kalelkar Award came into effect in 1967 and lays down service conditions of PWD workers (Demand No. 5 — holidays; Demand No. 6 — overtime). Maharashtra GRs/circulars referred to:
  - GR No. PAS-1070/741883-E(2)(2) dated 10.01.1974 (purported withdrawal of public holidays / 2nd & 4th Saturdays for field staff);
  - GR No. LAB-1080/1047(119)-A(14) dated 12.09.1980 (consolidated holiday guidelines for Irrigation workmen; office staff & workshop workmen eligible for 2nd/4th Saturdays);
  - Government Circular dated 27.05.1996 (relied on by the State; held confined to the Industrial Court Bombay cases).
- Holding: respondents, placed on the Converted Temporary Establishment (CTE) on 27.02.2004 under the Award, are temporary (not daily-wage) employees and > "entitled to all the holiday benefits and other emoluments stipulated under the Kalelkar Award" (para 21). Under Demand No. 5, daily-wage employees get only three fixed paid holidays and four optional paid leaves (paras 20–21). The Award reflects > "a specific agreement between the parties" (para 20). Appeal dismissed; compliance within eight weeks.

## 2. Regularisation — Umadevi principle
- Secretary, State of Karnataka v. Umadevi (3), (2006) 4 SCC 1 (Constitution Bench, 10.04.2006) — **not fetched in this session** (search budget exhausted). Core principle (summary): no regularisation of irregular/illegal appointees as a matter of right; one-time measure for duly-qualified employees irregularly (not illegally) appointed against sanctioned posts who had worked 10+ years as on the date of judgment, without court orders; later clarified in State of Karnataka v. M.L. Kesari (2010) 9 SCC 247 and Nihal Singh v. State of Punjab (2013) 14 SCC 65. Maharashtra GRs on regularisation of daily wagers (e.g. GAD/FD GRs of 1990s and the Industries, Energy & Labour Dept GRs on CTE conversion after 240 days' service under the Kalelkar Award) — **not fetched; numbers not verified**.
- Comparative (fetched, Gujarat): State of Gujarat v. PWD & Forest Employees Union, C.A. 1684-1686/2019, 15.02.2019 — https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2018/43592/43592_2018_Judgement_15-Feb-2019.pdf — only years with 240+ working days count; permanent daily wagers cannot get pay above regular employees; cut-off for GPF vs NPS upheld.

## How it is used in litigation (court staff context)
- Petitioners: daily-wage/contract sweepers, peons, computer operators, court managers etc. in subordinate courts seek regularisation on sanctioned posts, Kalelkar-type benefits, counting of service for pension and time-bound promotion, and equal pay.
- State's usual stand: appointments were contractual/daily-wage without following recruitment rules and without sanctioned posts, hence Umadevi bars regularisation; Kalelkar Award is confined to PWD/Irrigation workmen under the Industrial Disputes Act framework and does not apply to court establishments; service rendered before regular appointment is not qualifying service unless a specific rule/GR so provides; equal pay requires equivalence of post, qualification, mode of recruitment and duties (State of Punjab v. Jagjit Singh (2017) 1 SCC 148 recognises equal pay for temporary employees doing identical work — petitioners rely on it).

## मराठी सारांश
- कालेलकर पुरस्कार (1967) सार्वजनिक बांधकाम/पाटबंधारे विभागातील कामगारांच्या सेवाशर्ती ठरवतो; सर्वोच्च न्यायालयाने (PWD वि. तुकाराम सराफ, 19.09.2024) रूपांतरित अस्थायी आस्थापनेवरील कर्मचाऱ्यांना पुरस्कारातील सर्व सुट्ट्या व लाभ देय ठरवले; शासनाचे 1974/1980 चे शासन निर्णय व 1996 चे परिपत्रक त्यावर मात करू शकत नाहीत.
- उमादेवी (2006) तत्त्वानुसार अनियमित नियुक्त्यांचे नियमितीकरण हक्क म्हणून नाही; एकदाच 10 वर्षे सेवेच्या पात्र कर्मचाऱ्यांसाठी.
- रोजंदारी/कार्यव्ययी सेवा निवृत्तिवेतनासाठी गणण्याबाबतचे वित्त विभागाचे शासन निर्णय या सत्रात पडताळता आले नाहीत.
