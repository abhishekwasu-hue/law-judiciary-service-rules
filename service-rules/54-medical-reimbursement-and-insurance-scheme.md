# Medical reimbursement — Maharashtra State Services (Medical Attendance) Rules 1961 and the voluntary Medical Reimbursement Insurance Scheme

## 2. Medical Attendance Rules / reimbursement GRs
- Maharashtra State Services (Medical Attendance) Rules 1961 and the Public Health Dept GRs on reimbursement rates (e.g. rate lists, private-hospital treatment approval, 2019 GR on reimbursement of treatment in empanelled/private hospitals) — **not fetched in this session; numbers/dates not verified.**

## How it is used in litigation
- Court employees/pensioners seek reimbursement of treatment in non-empanelled private hospitals or beyond rate-list, or interest on delayed reimbursement; State's stand: reimbursement limited to Medical Attendance Rules rates and approved hospitals; emergency treatment needs post-facto approval of the competent authority; the insurance scheme is voluntary and its claim rules (72-hour/30-day) bind the member.
