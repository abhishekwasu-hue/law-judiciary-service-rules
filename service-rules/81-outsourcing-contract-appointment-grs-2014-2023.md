### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims (court establishments included):** outsourced/contract staff (data entry operators, sweepers, drivers, security) seek regularisation, equal pay, continuation after withdrawal of the agency panel, or absorption against sanctioned posts.
- **State's / High Court's stand:** engagement through an agency creates no employer-employee relationship with the State; GR 06.09.2023 stands cancelled by GR 31.10.2023 and departments are to make their own arrangements; sanctioned posts are filled only by the prescribed recruitment process (MPSC / High Court central recruitment) with reservation; Uma Devi bars regularisation; for court establishments the High Court/District Judge engages outsourced services under its own administrative sanction and the State is only the budget provider.
