### Typical structure of District Court Group C/D recruitment rules — summary (verify against original)
- Appointing authority: District Judge for Group C and D of the district establishment; High Court sanction for creation of posts; Government (L&J Dept / FD) for pay scales and establishment strength.
- Junior Clerk: direct recruitment, graduate, typing 30/40 wpm (Marathi/English), MS-CIT, age 18–38 (relaxations), written test + typing + interview; Stenographer Gr-3: shorthand 100/80 wpm; Peon: 7th/4th standard (now generally SSC), age 18–38.
- Promotion chain: Junior Clerk → Senior Clerk → Assistant Superintendent → Superintendent; Stenographer Gr-3 → Gr-2 → Gr-1 → Personal Assistant; by seniority-cum-fitness/merit with departmental examination (High Court's departmental exam rules); probation two years.
- Pay scales: as fixed by Government for comparable State posts (6th/7th CPC) plus Shetty Commission / SNJPC benefits where directed by the Supreme Court (file 72).

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims:** regularisation of daily-wage/contract court staff; promotion denied for want of departmental exam; parity of pay with Mantralaya/High Court staff; challenge to centralised recruitment (district-wise vacancy vs merit list); transfer outside district; compassionate appointment in the court where the deceased served; age relaxation.
- **State's stand:** District Judge / High Court is the appointing and disciplinary authority under Art. 235 and the Civil Courts Act; State's role limited to sanctioning posts, pay scales and budget; recruitment rules framed/approved by the High Court govern; equal pay requires equation of posts by the competent authority; contract/daily-wage engagement confers no right to regularisation (Uma Devi); reservation and compassionate policies apply as adopted by the High Court.

### मराठी सारांश
- जिल्हा न्यायालयांतील गट-क/ड कर्मचाऱ्यांची नियुक्ती प्रमुख जिल्हा न्यायाधीश करतात (अनुच्छेद २३५, मुंबई दिवाणी न्यायालये अधिनियम १८६९); भरती सध्या उच्च न्यायालयामार्फत केंद्रीय ऑनलाईन पद्धतीने (लघुलेखक श्रेणी-३, कनिष्ठ लिपिक, शिपाई/हमाल).
- भरती नियमांचे अधिकृत शीर्षक/दिनांक (२००९/२०१८) अधिकृत संकेतस्थळांवर सापडले नाहीत — not verified; उच्च न्यायालय प्रशासनाकडून प्रत घ्यावी.
- बदली अधिनियम २००५ चे प्रकरण-२ लागू नाही; आरक्षण व अनुकंपा धोरण राज्य शासनाचे, अंमलबजावणी उच्च न्यायालय/जिल्हा न्यायाधीश.
- शासनाची भूमिका: पदनिर्मिती, वेतनश्रेणी व अर्थसंकल्पापुरती; नियुक्ती/पदोन्नती/शिस्त हे न्यायालयीन प्रशासनाचे क्षेत्र.
