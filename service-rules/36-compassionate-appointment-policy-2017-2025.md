### Key provisions verified from the 2025 circulars (summary; quotes marked)
1. **Quota:** compassionate appointments are made against **20% of the posts falling vacant each year in the direct-recruitment quota of Group C and Group D**. For **Group D the 20% ceiling is relaxed for two years from the date of GR 17.07.2025**; where Group D posts have been declared "dead" (व्यपगत), an equal number may be **re-created (पुनर्जीवित)** and filled; a re-created post exists only while the incumbent holds it and dies again on his retirement/promotion; such posts need no separate roster point (बिंदू नामावली). Offices whose revised organisational structure (आकृतिबंध, FD GR 11.02.2016) is not yet approved get a one-time relaxation to fill all vacant Group D posts within the waiting-list limit, reporting vacancies to the Collector.
2. **Vacancy year:** vacancies counted for the recruitment year **1 September to 31 August**; reported category-wise in Annexure-F because reserved points cannot be given to open-category candidates. Multi-district establishments compute 20% on total annual vacancies and apportion district-wise, rounding to nearest whole number (worked example: 65 vacancies → 13 posts across Pune/Satara/Sangli/Solapur/Kolhapur).
3. **Common waiting list (सामायिक प्रतीक्षासूची) with the District Collector:** "अनुकंपा नियुक्ती ही प्रतीक्षासूचीतील ज्येष्ठतेनुसार करण्यात येते" — appointment strictly by seniority on the waiting list. Seniority date = date of submission of the **complete application** to the office head (even if the office forwards it late, or the Collector's office delays entry). Registration is with the Collector of the district **where the deceased employee was serving**, even if the family lives elsewhere (rule continued from the 1994 scheme). Group C applicants go to the Collector's list; police constable (Group C) and police Group D lists remain with the Commissioner/SP under Home Dept GR 23.02.2004.
4. **Group D → Group C priority abolished:** the 2017 provision (clause 16) giving Group D appointees priority for a Group C vacancy is **not** in the 2025 policy; those appointed to Group D before 17.07.2025 under the old clause may still be moved to Group C when a vacancy arises; candidates given Group D for want of Group C posts before 17.07.2025 need not be re-listed.
5. **Joining / preferences / qualification:** failure to join within the prescribed period = deemed refusal, name removed; if the candidate's preferences do not match available posts, he is recommended for an available post of his group matching his qualification; if he lacks the educational/technical qualification, the next senior candidate is considered.
6. **Roster:** Group C and D compassionate appointments must follow the roster (except re-created Group D posts); reserved candidates selected on open points by seniority may later be adjusted to reserved points.
7. **Special categories:** sportsperson — only after verification report of Commissioner, Sports & Youth Services, Pune (SE&SD GR 30.06.2022); Divyang — only after medical board under GAD GR 06.05.2004; **PESA (Scheduled Area) posts — no compassionate appointment** (they are filled under GAD (Reservation-5) GR 05.10.2024 on contract basis).
8. **Age limit, eligible heirs, time limit for application, married/divorced daughter, financial condition, Marathi / MS-CIT requirements:** **not stated** in the fetched circulars — these are in GR 17.07.2025 and GR 21.09.2017 (as amended 2019). Commonly cited figures (age limit 45 years; application within one year of death; heirs — spouse, son, unmarried/married/divorced daughter, in certain cases daughter-in-law / adopted child) are **not verified** here — obtain the GR text from gr.maharashtra.gov.in (GAD, म.लो.आ. desk).

### Related
- GAD circular 11.09.2025 also regulates appointment of MPSC-recommended Clerk-Typist 2023 candidates (ACS (Services) letter 28.07.2025; MPSC recommendation 11.07.2025) — shows compassionate posts are counted outside the MPSC recommendation quota.
- GAD circular 07.05.2024 (file 35): no compassionate benefit to heirs of supernumerary (invalid-caste) employees.
- Court staff: High Court / Principal District Judge is the appointing authority; the High Court applies the State scheme to court establishments (practice; official HC circular not located).

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims:** delay/denial of appointment despite being senior on waiting list; name wrongly placed by forwarding date; refusal because family "not indigent"; age crossed while on waiting list; demand for Group C though only Group D vacancy; demand for appointment in a court establishment where the deceased served; married daughter's eligibility; claim after invalidation of caste; claim where deceased was on a supernumerary post.
- **State's / High Court's stand:** compassionate appointment is not a vested right but an exception to Article 16 to tide over immediate crisis (Umesh Kumar Nagpal; State of Haryana v. Rani Devi); limited to 20% of annual vacancies and to seniority of the common waiting list; eligibility judged on the date of application and under the policy in force on the date of consideration; a candidate who refuses/does not join is deleted; no appointment on PESA posts or in Group A/B cadres (separate 27.09.2021 scheme for heirs of Group A/B officers — not verified); for court establishments, the High Court is the appointing authority and vacancies are reported to the Collector's common list.
