## How it is used in litigation
- Stenographers / clerks / bailiffs of subordinate courts claim the pay level recommended by Bakshi Vol. 2 or by the High Court's proposal from 01.01.2016 with arrears; PAs claim parity with Selection Grade Stenographers from 2023 (now conceded prospectively by circular 19.12.2025).
- State's stand: pay levels are fixed by the MCS (Revised Pay) Rules 2019 schedule; anomalies are addressed only on the committee's recommendation and FD GR, prospectively from the GR date unless the GR says otherwise; court staff cadres' pay is revised on the High Court's proposal with FD concurrence; equation with High Court staff (governed under Art. 229 by the Chief Justice's rules) is not automatic.
