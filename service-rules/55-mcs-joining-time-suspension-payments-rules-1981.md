## Part I — Joining time (पदग्रहण अवधी) — summary
| Rule (~) | Gist |
|---|---|
| ~3–5 | Definitions; joining time admissible on transfer in public interest to join a new post at the same or a new station; not admissible on transfer at own request (except as limited by Government). |
| ~6–8 | Joining time for transfer within the same station: **1 day** (if no change of residence); for change of station: **a fixed number of days by distance** (e.g., up to 1,000 km — 10 days; 1,001–2,000 km — 12 days; beyond — 15 days; plus actual travel time in certain cases — verify table); 6 days' preparation time under the old FR 105 pattern (the UJALA-type "unused joining time as special casual leave" is NOT a Maharashtra provision). |
| ~9–11 | Joining time may be combined with leave; unavailed joining time credited as earned leave (verify); extension by competent authority. |
| ~12–14 | **Pay during joining time**: pay of the old post or new post whichever is less (joining time = duty for pay/leave/pension). |
| ~15 | Overstayal of joining time = unauthorised absence; no pay; period not counted. |

## Part II — Foreign service (स्वीयेतर सेवा) — summary
- Rules ~20–60: transfer to foreign service only with consent of the employee and in public interest; pay fixation in foreign service; **pension and leave-salary contributions** by the foreign employer; reversion; lien retained; leave during foreign service.

## Part III — Payments during suspension, dismissal and removal — summary
| Rule (~) | Subject | Gist |
|---|---|---|
| **~65–68 (Rule 68 commonly cited)** | **Subsistence allowance (निर्वाह भत्ता)** during suspension | Leave salary equal to **half pay leave salary** (50% of pay) plus DA/HRA/CCA on that amount for the first **three months**; thereafter the competent authority may **increase up to 75%** if the prolongation is not attributable to the employee, or **reduce to 25%** if it is; employee must furnish a **certificate of not being engaged in other employment/business**; no payment without the certificate. **Subsistence allowance cannot be withheld** — non-payment vitiates the DE (SC: *Ghanshyam Das Shrivastava*, *Jagdamba Prasad Shukla*). No leave may be sanctioned during suspension. |
| ~69 | Recoveries from subsistence allowance | Compulsory deductions (GPF subscription not recoverable — verify) etc. |
| **~70** | **Reinstatement after dismissal/removal/compulsory retirement is set aside** (departmental appeal/review) | Authority to pass a specific order on pay and allowances for the intervening period and whether it is treated as duty: **full pay and allowances if the employee is fully exonerated** / penalty set aside on merits; otherwise such proportion as the authority may prescribe after **notice and representation**; period treated as duty if fully exonerated, else as directed; set-off of earnings elsewhere. |
| **~71** | **Reinstatement pursuant to a court order** setting aside dismissal/removal | Same principles; if set aside purely on technical ground and further inquiry contemplated, authority may order proportion; notice mandatory. |
| **~72** | **Reinstatement after suspension (where not dismissed/removed)** — *regularisation of suspension period* | Where the employee is **fully exonerated** (or suspension held wholly unjustified) → **full pay and allowances** for the suspension period and period treated as duty; otherwise the authority fixes the proportion **after issuing a show-cause notice** and considering the representation; period of suspension not treated as duty unless so ordered; **subsistence allowance not refundable** even when proportion is less. GAD circular 21.02.2015 directs that after the final order / Rule 72 notice, regularisation must be completed **within one month**. |
| ~73 | Leave during suspension | Leave not admissible to a suspended employee. |

## How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner**: subsistence allowance not paid / not revised to 75% after 3 months → DE vitiated; suspension period not regularised for years; full back wages on exoneration/acquittal; period to count as duty for increments, promotion and pension; interest on delayed payment; joining time denied on transfer at request.
- **State**: subsistence allowance paid on furnishing the non-employment certificate; enhancement discretionary and refused because delay attributable to employee's adjournments / stay orders; **acquittal on benefit of doubt is not "full exoneration"** — Rule 72 notice issued and proportion fixed after considering representation ("no work no pay" where reinstatement follows a technical acquittal or a minor penalty); period treated as duty only to the extent ordered; employee gainfully employed during the period (set-off); joining time inadmissible on request transfer.

## मराठी सारांश
महाराष्ट्र नागरी सेवा (पदग्रहण अवधी, स्वीयेतर सेवा आणि निलंबन, बडतर्फी व सेवेतून काढून टाकणे यांच्या काळातील प्रदाने) नियम, १९८१ (वित्त विभाग) यात बदलीवरील पदग्रहण अवधी व त्या काळातील वेतन, स्वीयेतर सेवेतील वेतन/अंशदान आणि निलंबन काळातील निर्वाह भत्ता (पहिले ३ महिने अर्धवेतनी रजा वेतनाइतका; नंतर ७५% पर्यंत वाढ किंवा २५% पर्यंत कपात; अन्यत्र नोकरी नसल्याचे प्रमाणपत्र आवश्यक) यांच्या तरतुदी आहेत. नियम ७०–७१ बडतर्फी/सेवेतून काढून टाकणे रद्द झाल्यावर पुनर्स्थापनेवेळी वेतन-भत्ते ठरवण्याबाबत, तर नियम ७२ निलंबन कालावधीच्या नियमितीकरणाबाबत (पूर्ण निर्दोष असल्यास पूर्ण वेतन व कर्तव्य कालावधी; अन्यथा नोटीस देऊन प्रमाण ठरवणे) आहेत. सा.प्र.वि. परिपत्रक दि. २१.०२.२०१५ नुसार अंतिम आदेश/नियम ७२ नोटीसनंतर एक महिन्यात नियमितीकरण करणे बंधनकारक आहे. मूळ नियमांचा मजकूर अधिकृत PDF मधून मिळाला नाही; वापरण्यापूर्वी ताडून घ्यावा.
