## Key provisions — summary (verify against original)

| Rule | Heading | Gist |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Short title, commencement, application | Applies to every Government servant of the State except (a) All-India Service officers, (b) persons governed by other conduct rules notified by Government, (c) specified categories. |
| 2 | Definitions | "Government servant", "members of family", "Government", etc. |
| 3 | General | Every Government servant shall at all times (i) maintain absolute integrity; (ii) maintain devotion to duty; (iii) do nothing unbecoming of a Government servant. Supervisory officers must ensure integrity and devotion of subordinates. No Government servant shall act otherwise than in his best judgment except on direction of an official superior; such direction should ordinarily be in writing, and oral directions should be confirmed in writing. **(This is the omnibus rule under which most charge-sheets are framed: "अत्यंत सचोटी", "कर्तव्यपरायणता", "शासकीय कर्मचाऱ्यास न शोभणारे वर्तन".)** |
| 3A / 3B (if present in current edition) | Promptness and courtesy; observance of Government policies | Insertions on the CCS pattern — **existence in MCS edition not verified**. |
| 4 | Employment of near relatives in firms enjoying Government patronage | Prior sanction / report requirements. |
| 5 | Taking part in politics and elections | No membership of political parties / canvassing; voting permitted without display of preference. |
| 6 | Joining of associations | Not to join an association whose objects are prejudicial to sovereignty, integrity, public order or morality. |
| 7 | Demonstrations and strikes | Prohibits participation in any strike, demonstration prejudicial to public order etc.; relevant to mass-casual-leave / "pen-down" agitations. |
| 8 | Connection with press or radio | No ownership/participation in publications without sanction. |
| 9 | Criticism of Government | No public statement adversely criticising Government policy. |
| 10 | Evidence before committees | Not without sanction, except judicial / departmental enquiries / Parliament/Legislature committees. |
| 11 | Unauthorised communication of information | No official document/information to unauthorised persons. |
| 12 | Subscriptions | Not to ask for or accept subscriptions without sanction. |
| 13 | Gifts | Acceptance restricted; thresholds by Group (Rs. limits revised from time to time — verify current ceilings). |
| 14 | Dowry | Prohibits giving/taking/demanding dowry. |
| 15 | Public demonstrations in honour of Government servants | No complimentary / valedictory functions except farewells of a simple nature. |
| 16 | Private trade or employment | No private business / employment / coaching without sanction; family members' business to be reported. |
| 17 | Investments, lending and borrowing | No speculation; no lending at interest; no habitual borrowing; report of indebtedness. |
| 18 | Insolvency and habitual indebtedness | Must report insolvency proceedings; habitual indebtedness is misconduct. |
| 19 | Movable, immovable and valuable property | Declaration of assets on first appointment and **annual property returns** (मालमत्ता विवरणपत्र); prior sanction/intimation for immovable property transactions and movable property above the notified limit. **Non-filing of property returns is a ground for denial of promotion (see GR 01.08.2019 on promotion procedure, item in file 52).** |
| 20 | Vindication of acts and character of Government servant | Not to approach court/press to vindicate official acts without sanction. |
| 21 | Canvassing of non-official or other outside influence | No political/outside influence to further service interests (MLAs' letters etc.). |
| 22 | Restriction regarding marriage | No bigamy; no marriage with a person having a living spouse, without permission; relevant to compassionate-appointment and family-pension disputes. |
| 23 | Consumption of intoxicating drinks and drugs | Not to be under influence while on duty; obey prohibition law. |
| 24 | Prohibition regarding employment of children below 14 years | Inserted later (CCS pattern) — verify. |

## How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claim**: charge-sheet under Rule 3 is vague / "omnibus"; conduct alleged (e.g., unauthorised absence, insubordination, late attendance) is not "misconduct" under any specific rule; Rule 3 cannot be invoked without specifying which sub-clause; prior sanction was not needed for the private activity; property-return default is technical.
- **State's usual stand**: Rule 3(1)(i)–(iii) is a substantive standard of conduct and not merely a preamble; absence of a specific rule is immaterial where the act is "unbecoming"; the charge-sheet (articles of charge + statement of imputations) gives adequate particulars; conduct rules apply to court non-judicial staff through adoption by the High Court; promotion rightly withheld for non-filing of property returns under the promotion-procedure GR.
- Pair with: D&A Rules Rule 5 (penalties) and Rule 8/10 (procedure) — file 51; sealed-cover and DE time-limit GRs — file 52.

## मराठी सारांश
महाराष्ट्र नागरी सेवा (वर्तणूक) नियम, १९७९ हे सामान्य प्रशासन विभागाने अनुच्छेद ३०९ खाली केलेले नियम असून ते राज्य शासकीय कर्मचाऱ्यांच्या (आणि उच्च न्यायालयाच्या अन्यायिक आस्थापनेवरील गट अ ते ड कर्मचाऱ्यांच्या, न्यायालयाने स्वीकारल्याप्रमाणे) वर्तणुकीचे मानक ठरवतात. नियम ३ (अत्यंत सचोटी, कर्तव्यपरायणता, न शोभणारे वर्तन न करणे) हा बहुतेक दोषारोपपत्रांचा आधार असतो. राजकारणात भाग, संप/निदर्शने, भेटवस्तू, खाजगी व्यवसाय, मालमत्ता विवरणपत्र, विवाहावरील निर्बंध, मद्यपान इत्यादींवरील नियम महत्त्वाचे आहेत. या फाईलमधील नियमांचा मजकूर अधिकृत संकेतस्थळावरून मिळू शकला नाही; शपथपत्रात उद्धृत करण्यापूर्वी छापील प्रत तपासावी.
