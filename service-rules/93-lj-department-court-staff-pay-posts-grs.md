## 4. Pay-parity agitation (Oct 2026)
- Maharashtra State Judicial Employees Federation protest 16–17.10.2026 demanding High Court staff pay scales for subordinate court employees; proposal pending with CMO since Jan 2025 (The Print; UNI — non-official).

## How it is used in litigation
- Typical petitions: (i) Shetty-scale arrears for stenographers/clerks/bailiffs from 01.04.2003 or 01.01.2006; (ii) parity with High Court staff (Art. 229 rules give HC staff higher levels); (iii) regularisation of court managers/contract staff; (iv) sanction of posts.
- State's stand: subordinate court staff service conditions are framed under Art. 235/High Court rules with L&J Department as administrative department and FD concurrence for financial sanctions; Shetty recommendations for staff were implemented through specific GRs on specified dates and cannot be extended by analogy; High Court staff are a distinct establishment under Art. 229, so no parity claim lies; post creation and contractual engagements are policy/financial matters.
