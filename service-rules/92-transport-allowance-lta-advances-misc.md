# Transport allowance, Leave Travel Concession, festival/other advances — miscellaneous allowance instruments
# वाहतूक भत्ता, रजा प्रवास सवलत, सण अग्रिम व इतर अग्रिमे — संकीर्ण भत्ता साधने

**Status:** Mostly **not verified** from official sources in this session (search budget exhausted); recorded here so the gaps are explicit.

## 1. Transport allowance (वाहतूक भत्ता)
- Per FD HRA GR 05.02.2019 (file 42): > "स्थानिक पूरक भत्ता व वाहतूक भत्ता हे दोन्ही भत्ते हे 6 व्या वेतन आयोगाच्या कालावधीत..." — i.e. transport allowance and local compensatory allowance continued at the 6th CPC-period rates after 01.01.2019. A separate FD GR revising transport allowance under the 7th CPC (if any) was **not located; not verified**.

## 2. Leave Travel Concession (रजा प्रवास सवलत)
- Maharashtra LTC rules (home-town LTC once in a block of 4 years; all-India LTC) are in the MCS (Leave) Rules 1981 / FD GRs — **not fetched; not verified**. (See file 53 by another agent on Leave Rules.)

## 3. Advances to Government employees (MH 7610)
- Home Dept GR बीयुडी-0625/प्र.क्र.37/आस्था-1, dated 18.03.2026 — release of Rs 103.32 crore for 2025-26 under Demand B-11, MH 7610 "Loans to Government Employees" for housing (201), motor-vehicle (202) and computer (204) advances to police/Home offices — fetched: https://www.mahapolice.gov.in/uploads/1005033517124335dd63750c7312b397.pdf. Verbatim: > "संबंधित नियमांच्या अधीन राहून उपलब्ध निधीच्या मर्यादित मंजूर करावीत." Expenditure subject to FD circular dated 07.04.2025 (not fetched). Shows that housing/vehicle/computer advances remain operative for State employees subject to budget.
- Festival advance (सण अग्रिम) GR — **not fetched; not verified**.

## How it is used in litigation
- Rarely litigated as stand-alone claims; appear as ancillary reliefs (transport allowance to differently-abled employees at double rate on Central pattern; LTC encashment). State's stand: allowances are policy matters; Central OMs are not automatically applicable.

## मराठी सारांश
- वाहतूक भत्ता व स्थानिक पूरक भत्ता 01.01.2019 नंतरही 6 व्या वेतन आयोगाच्या दरानेच (घरभाडे शा.नि. 05.02.2019 मधील उल्लेख).
- रजा प्रवास सवलत व सण अग्रिम शासन निर्णय या सत्रात पडताळलेले नाहीत.
- घरबांधणी/वाहन/संगणक अग्रिमे (मुख्य लेखाशीर्ष 7610) 2025-26 मध्येही सुरू — गृह विभाग शा.नि. 18.03.2026 उदाहरण.
