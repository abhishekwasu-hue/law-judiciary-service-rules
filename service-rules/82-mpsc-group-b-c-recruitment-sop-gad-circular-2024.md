# Bringing Group B (non-gazetted) and Group C posts under MPSC recruitment — GAD GR 18.07.2024 and SOP circular 10.12.2024

### Key provisions (summary of fetched text)
1. Group B (non-gazetted) and Group C posts presently outside MPSC's purview to be brought under MPSC in phases, as recommended by the coordination committee.
2. Departments submit proposals in prescribed format to GAD (Seva-4); committee recommends phasing.
3. Legal amendment to the 1965 exemption rules and revision of recruitment rules precede requisitions to MPSC under the 02.11.2022 procedure.
4. Existing reservation policy and allied concessions continue for posts moved to MPSC.

### Court-staff relevance
- High Court and District Court staff are recruited by the High Court (Art. 229 / Art. 235) and are **not** within this circular (it addresses posts under administrative departments); it is useful when a petitioner demands that court recruitment be conducted through MPSC or alleges non-uniformity — the State can explain that judiciary staff recruitment is constitutionally separate.

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims:** recruitment conducted departmentally after 18.07.2024 is invalid; demand for MPSC recruitment; stale select lists.
- **State's stand:** transfer to MPSC is phased and requires rule amendment; till then the existing recruitment rules and GAD recruitment procedure apply; MPSC-recommended candidates' appointment is subject to document verification and reservation (GAD circular 11.09.2025 for Clerk-Typist 2023).
