# House Rent Allowance (HRA) — 7th CPC rates and city classification
# घरभाडे भत्ता — सुधारित दर व शहरांचे वर्गीकरण

**Issuing department:** Finance Department, Desk सेवा-5
**Status:** In force (rates stepped up with DA: 27/18/9 % once DA crossed 25 %, 30/20/10 % once DA crossed 50 %).

## 1. GR घभाभ-2019/प्र.क्र.2/सेवा-5, dated 05.02.2019 — HRA at revised (7th CPC) rates w.e.f. 01.01.2019
- **Source caveat:** fetched only from a college-hosted copy (https://svmhs.somaiya.edu.in/media/pdf/KJSP_7%20Pay%20HRA%20Rate%20Gr%20Dt%205.2.2019.pdf). Number/date/rates below are read from that copy — **verify on gr.maharashtra.gov.in before citing**. Not found on an official domain in this session.
- Basis: GoI OM 07.07.2017; city reclassification GR 16.12.2016.
- Rates (% of basic pay):
  | Class | 6th CPC | 7th CPC (from 01.01.2019) | when DA > 25 % | when DA > 50 % |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | X | 30 % | 24 % | 27 % | 30 % |
  | Y | 20 % | 16 % | 18 % | 20 % |
  | Z | 10 % | 8 % | 9 % | 10 % |
- Minimum fixed HRA: Rs 5,400 (X), Rs 3,600 (Y), Rs 1,800 (Z).
- **Verbatim (Marathi, from fetched copy):** > "हे आदेश दिनांक 01.01.2019 पासून अंमलात येतील." ; > "स्थानिक पूरक भत्ता व वाहतूक भत्ता हे दोन्ही भत्ते हे 6 व्या वेतन आयोगाच्या कालावधीत..." (i.e. local compensatory allowance and transport allowance continue at 6th CPC-period rates).
- Special pay etc. excluded from "basic pay" for HRA; all other existing HRA conditions continue.

## 2. GR घभाभ-1015/प्र.क्र.19/सेवा-5, dated 16.12.2016 — Reclassification of cities/villages for HRA
- Fetched (copy hosted by Maharashtra Building & Other Construction Workers Welfare Board, a State body): https://mahabocw.in/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/201612161737280805.pdf (GR unique code 201612161737280805)
- Replaces the 24.08.2009 classification; follows GoI 2015 reclassification on Census 2011. Annexure-1 lists X and Y class cities; everything else is Z.
- **Verbatim:** > "...जशाच्या तशा लागू राहतील" (other conditions continue) ; > "हा शासन निर्णय निर्गमित झाल्याच्या महिन्यापासून अंमलात येईल"
- Rates then: X 30 %, Y 20 %, Z 10 %.

## 3. Related
- Transport allowance / local compensatory allowance: continued at 6th CPC rates per para of the 05.02.2019 GR; no separate 7th CPC transport allowance GR was located (not verified).
- DA GRs (file 36) — the HRA step-up is triggered by DA crossing 25 % / 50 %.

## 4. How it is used in litigation
- Claims: HRA at X-class rate for employees posted in areas later merged into a municipal corporation; HRA step-up arrears from the date DA crossed 25 %/50 %; HRA to employees in Government quarters / disciplinary suspension.
- State's stand: city classification is per the GR annexure based on Census population and GoI pattern; HRA is admissible by place of posting, not residence; employees allotted Government accommodation are not eligible; HRA is an allowance, not pay, and is a policy matter.
