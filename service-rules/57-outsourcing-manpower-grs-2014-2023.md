## How it is used in litigation
- Outsourced staff in courts/Government offices (sweepers, data-entry operators, drivers, security) claim regularisation, continuity after agency change, equal pay, and protection against termination when the panel was cancelled.
- State's stand: no employer–employee relationship (clause quoted above); engagement is through the agency on a service contract; cancellation of the panel does not create any right against the State; departments may engage fresh agencies by tender; regularisation barred by Umadevi; sanctioned posts are filled only through MPSC/recruitment rules.
