# Timely payment of retirement benefits — MCS (Pension) Rules 1982, Rules 118–125 — FD Circular 01.08.2025
# सेवानिवृत्ती लाभ वेळेवर अदा करणे — म.ना.से. (निवृत्तिवेतन) नियम 1982, नियम 118 ते 125 — वित्त विभाग परिपत्रक दि. 01.08.2025

**Circular No.:** संकीर्ण-2023/प्र.क्र.4/सेवा-7
**Date:** 01.08.2025
**Issuing department:** Finance Department
**Official source fetched:** https://www.mahapolice.gov.in/uploads/0f63b3bea8774d3f171e50ff0499ca35.pdf
**Status:** In force.

## Key provisions (verbatim where fetched)
- Purpose: > "निवृत्तिवेतनधारकांना लाभ विलंबाने प्रदान झाल्याने त्यांना आर्थिक, मानसिक त्रास सहन करावा लागतो"
- Pension case preparation to begin two years before retirement: > "सेवानिवृत्तीच्या तारखेच्या 2 वर्षे अगोदर सुरुवात करण्यात येते"
- Service book to be kept complete (Annexure-1 lists entries); DDO/Head of Office to collect Form 1, Forms A & B; papers to Treasury; pending cases to be cleared.
- Accountability: > "सर्वतोपरी जबाबदारी संबंधित विभाग प्रमुख व कार्यालय प्रमुख यांची राहील"
- If interest becomes payable on delayed benefits, responsible officers face disciplinary action and adverse CR entry.
- Benefits covered: pension, family pension, provisional pension/gratuity, commutation, gratuity, GPF final payment.

## How it is used in litigation
- Retired court employees seek interest on delayed gratuity/pension/leave encashment; State replies that delay was due to incomplete service book / pending departmental inquiry / non-submission of forms by the employee; this circular is used by petitioners to show the two-year timeline and officer accountability, and by the State to show it has issued systemic directions.

## मराठी सारांश
- वित्त विभाग परिपत्रक दि. 01.08.2025: निवृत्तिवेतन नियम 118–125 चे काटेकोर पालन; सेवानिवृत्तीच्या 2 वर्षे आधी निवृत्तिवेतन प्रकरण सुरू; सेवापुस्तक पूर्ण ठेवणे.
