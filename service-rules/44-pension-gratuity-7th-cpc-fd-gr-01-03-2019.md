# Revision of pension, family pension, gratuity (Rs 14 lakh ceiling) and commutation under 7th CPC — FD GR 01.03.2019
# सातव्या वेतन आयोगानुसार निवृत्तिवेतन / कुटुंब निवृत्तिवेतन / उपदान (कमाल रु. 14 लाख) / अंशराशीकरण — वित्त विभाग शासन निर्णय दि. 01.03.2019

**GR No.:** सेनिवे-2019/प्र.क्र.58/सेवा-4 (PEN-2019/C.R.58/SER-4)
**Date:** 01.03.2019
**Issuing department:** Finance Department

**Note on Rs 20 lakh:** No Maharashtra GR raising the ceiling to Rs 20 lakh (as the Centre did) was found — **not verified / apparently not issued** as of the sources seen.

## Key provisions — summary (verify against original; fetched copy is Marathi OCR)
**Pension**
- 50 % of last basic pay or 50 % of average basic pay of last 10 months, whichever is more beneficial; full pension on 20 years' qualifying service; payable only to permanent employees (as per 1982 Rules).
- Maximum pension = 50 % of highest pay in revised structure (Rs 2,20,000) = Rs 1,10,000 p.m.

**Family pension (Family Pension Scheme 1964 / Rule 116)**
- Uniform 30 % of pensionable pay; max 30 % of highest pay (≈ Rs 66,000).
- Enhanced family pension on death in service: payable for 10 years from death, age condition removed.

**Gratuity (उपदान)**
- Retirement gratuity: **1/4th of emoluments for each completed six-monthly period, max 16.5 times emoluments or Rs 14,00,000, whichever is less.**
- Death gratuity slabs (qualifying service → amount):
  | < 1 year | 2 × emoluments |
  |---|---|
  | 1 to < 5 years | 6 × |
  | 5 to < 11 years | 12 × |
  | 11 to < 20 years | 20 × |
  | ≥ 20 years | half of emoluments per completed six-monthly period, max 33 × |
  Death gratuity ceiling also Rs 14 lakh.

**Commutation**
- Up to 40 % of pension; lump sum = basic pension × 40 % × 12 × commutation factor (Annexure-I by age next birthday; e.g. 9.19 at 20, 7.731 at 65).
- Revised commutation only for those retiring on/after 01.01.2019; retirees of 01.01.2016–31.12.2018 not eligible for re-commutation of the differential.

**DR rates on revised pension:** 0 % (01.01.2016), 2 % (01.07.2016), 4 % (01.01.2017), 5 % (01.07.2017), 7 % (01.01.2018), 9 % (01.07.2018).

**Effective dates / arrears**
- Notional from 01.01.2016; actual cash from 01.01.2019; arrears for 2016–2018 in five equal annual instalments starting with June 2019 pension.

**Applicability**
- State Govt employees retiring/dying on or after 01.01.2016; pre-2016 pensioners under a separate GR (listed on CAG page as "Revision of Pension/FP of pre-1-1-2016 pensioners as per 7th CPC" — number not verified); extended to aided institutions, universities, ZPs. Excludes AIS, political/freedom-fighter/gallantry pensions, and PSU-absorbed employees whose 1/3rd commuted pension is restored.

## How it is used in litigation
- Claims: gratuity ceiling Rs 20 lakh (Central parity) with interest; counting of ad-hoc/daily-wage/work-charged service for the 16.5-month computation; interest under Rule 129-A (sic — verify) for delayed gratuity; family pension at enhanced rate beyond 10 years; commutation of differential for 2016–18 retirees.
- State's stand: pension/gratuity are governed strictly by the 1982 Rules as amended by this GR; Rs 14 lakh is the ceiling adopted by the State on the Bakshi Committee recommendation; Central ceilings do not bind the State; the five-instalment arrears scheme is a policy decision upheld in other contexts; qualifying service excludes non-regular service unless specifically counted under Rules 30–40 (verify numbers) of the 1982 Rules.

## मराठी सारांश
- वित्त विभाग शासन निर्णय सेनिवे-2019/प्र.क्र.58/सेवा-4, दि. 01.03.2019: दि. 01.01.2016 नंतर सेवानिवृत्त/मृत कर्मचाऱ्यांचे निवृत्तिवेतन 7 व्या वेतन आयोगानुसार.
- निवृत्तिवेतन = शेवटच्या वेतनाच्या 50% (कमाल रु. 1,10,000); कुटुंब निवृत्तिवेतन 30%.
- सेवानिवृत्ती उपदान कमाल 16.5 पट वेतन किंवा रु. 14 लाख यापैकी कमी; मृत्यू उपदानही कमाल रु. 14 लाख.
- अंशराशीकरण कमाल 40%; प्रत्यक्ष लाभ दि. 01.01.2019 पासून, 2016–18 ची थकबाकी पाच समान वार्षिक हप्त्यांत.
- रु. 20 लाख मर्यादेचा राज्याचा शासन निर्णय आढळला नाही.
