# Revised National Pension Scheme (सुधारित राष्ट्रीय निवृत्तिवेतन योजना) & adoption of Central Unified Pension Scheme — FD GR 20.09.2024
# सुधारित राष्ट्रीय निवृत्तिवेतन योजना / एकीकृत निवृत्तिवेतन योजना — वित्त विभाग शासन निर्णय दि. 20.09.2024

**GR No.:** सेनिवे-2024/प्र.क्र.54/सेवा-4
**Date:** 20.09.2024
**Issuing department:** Finance Department
**Official source fetched:** https://www.mahapolice.gov.in/uploads/a5564d2de2a6084556248136d404d90e.pdf

**Status:** In force; option window closed 31.03.2025 (unless extended — not verified). News reports of May 2026 say the State clarified that migration to the revised scheme is optional for existing NPS employees (not verified from official source).

## Key provisions
- **Verbatim:** > "सुधारीत राष्ट्रीय निवृत्तिवेतन योजना दिनांक 01.03.2024 पासून लागू करण्यात येत आहे."
- **Verbatim:** Central UPS adopted as is — > "शासकीय कर्मचाऱ्यांना जशीच्या तशी लागू करण्यास मान्यता देण्यात येत आहे."
- **Verbatim:** choice — > "दोन्ही योजनेमधील कोणत्याही एका योजनेत सहभागी होण्यास मुभा राहील."
- **Verbatim:** option deadline — > "दिनांक 31.03.2025 पर्यंत संबंधित कार्यालय प्रमुख / विभाग प्रमुख यांचेकडे सादर करावा."
- **Verbatim:** finality — > "तद्नंतर सुधारीत राष्ट्रीय निवृत्तिवेतन योजनेंतर्गत दिलेला विकल्प कोणत्याही कारणास्तव बदलता येणार नाही."
- **Verbatim:** default — > "राष्ट्रीय निवृत्तिवेतन प्रणाली अंतर्गत निवृत्तिवेतन योजना आपोआप लागू राहील." (non-opters stay in NPS)
- Annexure-A (Revised NPS) — summary (verify): 20+ years' service → 50 % of last pay as assured pension; family pension 60 % of that; 10–20 years → proportionate; minimum Rs 7,500 after 10 years.
- Annexure-B (Central UPS) — summary: 50 % of average basic pay of last 12 months for 25 years' service; minimum Rs 10,000 p.m. after 10 years.
- Extended mutatis mutandis to aided educational institutions, certain universities, ZP employees.

## Related

## How it is used in litigation
- Petitioners: OPS restoration; extension of option deadline; inclusion of pre-01.11.2005-selected employees; treatment of contractual/ad-hoc service as qualifying for the 20/25-year thresholds.
- State's stand: Revised NPS/UPS is a complete, self-contained policy scheme with assured 50 % pension; option is one-time and final by design; qualifying service counts only regular service in a pensionable post; OPS demand is a policy matter beyond judicial review.
