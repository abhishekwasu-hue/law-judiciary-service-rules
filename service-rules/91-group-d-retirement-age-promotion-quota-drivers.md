# Group D (चतुर्थ श्रेणी) employees — retirement age 60, promotion quota to clerk, qualification relaxation, drivers' cadre
# चतुर्थ श्रेणी कर्मचारी — सेवानिवृत्ती वय 60, लिपिक पदावर पदोन्नती कोटा, अर्हता शिथिलता, वाहनचालक संवर्ग

(Overlaps with file 80 by another agent on retirement age / re-employment; this file records what this session could and could not verify.)

**Status:** Rule 10, MCS (Pension) Rules 1982 governs retirement age; GAD recruitment-rule GRs govern promotion quota.

## 1. Retirement age
- **Rule 10, MCS (Pension) Rules 1982:** Group A/B/C retire at 58 (raised to 60 for all State employees? — **not verified**: as of the sources seen, the general State retirement age remains 58 with 60 for Group D and for specified cadres such as Medical Officers under the Rule 10 provisos). Group D (Class IV) employees retire at **60** under Rule 10 — long-standing provision (summary; rule text not extracted). The "GAD GR 2015/2016 raising Group D age to 60" mentioned in the brief could **not be located/verified**; the 60-year age for Class IV predates it in the 1982 Rules.
- Bombay HC, Dr. Mahendra Vilas Phalke v. State of Maharashtra, W.P. 11453/2023 etc., interim order 05.10.2023 (Nitin Jamdar & Manjusha Deshpande JJ.) — fetched: https://www.livelaw.in/pdf_upload/ordjud-39-497584.pdf (copy on LiveLaw; the judgment itself is an official record — verify on bombayhighcourt.nic.in). Shows the mechanism: Public Health Dept GR 29.08.2018 raised Medical Officers' age 58→60; Notification 23.02.2022 amended Rule 10 provisos (in force till 31.05.2023); FD affidavit 24.07.2023 — officer > "shall be deemed to retire on the last date of the month in which the Medical Officer attains the age of 60 years". Useful as precedent on how Rule 10 provisos are read and on the Finance Department's role.

## 2. Promotion quota Group D → Clerk / Junior Clerk
- GAD GRs fixing a percentage quota (10 % / 20 %) of clerk posts for promotion of Group D employees possessing the prescribed qualification (SSC/HSC + MS-CIT, typing), with relaxation of age — **not fetched; numbers/dates not verified**. For subordinate courts the quota is in the District Court staff Recruitment Rules (file 71 by another agent) framed by the High Court/L&J Department.

## 3. Drivers (वाहनचालक) cadre
- Drivers are Group C in the State (not Group D); pay level revised under MCS (Revised Pay) Rules 2019; GAD GRs on driver recruitment via outsourcing (e.g. MLD GR 28.06.2018 — file 57) and on driver-cum-peon posts — **pay-scale GR not fetched; not verified**.

## Related
- Uniform/washing allowance GR 10.10.2024 (file 43) — Group D specific.
- Compassionate appointment (file 36 by another agent) — most Group D appointments in courts arise there.

## How it is used in litigation
- Group D court employees (peons, hamals, watchmen, sweepers) claim: promotion to junior clerk under the quota despite lacking typing/MS-CIT; counting of ad-hoc service for the quota; continuation till 60 where an order retires them at 58 (clerical error cases); drivers claim Group C pay/technical allowance.
- State's stand: quota promotion requires the qualification in the recruitment rules; relaxation is only where the rules/GR provide; retirement age is as per Rule 10 for the post actually held; drivers' pay is per the Revised Pay Rules schedule.

## मराठी सारांश
- म.ना.से. (निवृत्तिवेतन) नियम 1982 नियम 10 नुसार चतुर्थ श्रेणी कर्मचाऱ्यांचे सेवानिवृत्ती वय 60 वर्षे; इतर गटांसाठी 58 (वैद्यकीय अधिकाऱ्यांसारख्या संवर्गांसाठी परंतुकाने 60).
- चतुर्थ श्रेणीतून लिपिक पदावर पदोन्नती कोटा (10/20%) व अर्हता शिथिलतेचे शासन निर्णय, तसेच वाहनचालक वेतनश्रेणीचे शासन निर्णय या सत्रात अधिकृत स्रोतावरून पडताळता आले नाहीत.
