# Additional pension to pensioners / family pensioners aged 80 and above
# 80 वर्षे व त्यावरील निवृत्तिवेतनधारकांच्या निवृत्तिवेतनात वाढ

## 3. FD Circular PEN-2019/C.R.59/SER-4, dated 30.07.2019 — clarifications

- Refers to GR PEN-1014/C.R.26/SER-4 dt 09.06.2014 (10 % from 01.04.2014 for 80+) and GoI OM 38/37/08-P&PW(A) Pt.II dt 03.10.2008.
- Slabs from 01.01.2019: 80–85: 10 %; >85–90: 15 %; >90–95: 20 %; >95–100: 25 %; >100: 50 %.

## How it is used in litigation
- Claims by very senior pensioners (incl. retired court staff) for arrears of enhanced slab from 01.01.2019 or from the Central date; computation on "basic pension" vs. pension plus DR.
- State's stand: increase is on basic pension only; prospective from the GR's effective date (GR 16.01.2024 expressly bars arrears); Central OMs do not apply proprio vigore to State pensioners.
