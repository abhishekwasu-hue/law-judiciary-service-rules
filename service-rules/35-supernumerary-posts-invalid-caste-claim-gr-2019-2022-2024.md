### Key provisions (summary of fetched text; quote marked)
- Employees appointed against ST-reserved posts whose tribe claims were invalidated by Scrutiny Committees are placed on **supernumerary posts (अधिसंख्य पदे)** for 11-month spells with a **one-day technical break** after each spell, until retirement or a final decision on the study-group report, whichever is earlier (origin: Supreme Court in Chairman & MD, FCI v. Jagdish Balaram Bahira (2017) — not fetched).
- They get service and retirement benefits, but **no promotion and no compassionate-appointment benefit**: "यामध्ये पदोन्नती व अनुकंपा धोरण याचा लाभ मिळणार नाही."
- No annual increment or other service benefit accrues because of the technical breaks; increments granted after 21.12.2019 are to be cancelled and recovered; service before supernumerary posting counts as qualifying service for retirement benefits.

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims:** regularisation on the original post; protection under earlier GRs (e.g. GAD GR 15.06.1995 — not verified) for appointments before 2001; grant of increments/promotion while on supernumerary post; compassionate appointment to heirs; challenge to recovery.
- **State's stand:** appointment obtained on an invalid caste claim is void ab initio under the Caste Certificate Act 2000 / Jagdish Bahira; supernumerary post is a humanitarian measure under GR 21.12.2019 / 14.12.2022, not a right; no promotion, no increment, no compassionate appointment (circular 07.05.2024); recovery of excess increments. For court staff, the High Court administration implements the same scheme.

### मराठी सारांश
- अजा दावा अवैध ठरलेल्या कर्मचाऱ्यांना सा.प्र.वि. शा.नि. २१.१२.२०१९ व १४.१२.२०२२ नुसार ११ महिन्यांच्या कालावधीसाठी, एक दिवसाच्या तांत्रिक खंडासह, अधिसंख्य पदांवर ठेवले जाते.
- परिपत्रक ०७.०५.२०२४ (क्र. बीसीसी-1124/प्र.क्र.45/आरक्षण-2): पदोन्नती व अनुकंपा धोरणाचा लाभ नाही; वेतनवाढ नाही; २१.१२.२०१९ नंतर दिलेल्या वेतनवाढी रद्द करून वसुली.
