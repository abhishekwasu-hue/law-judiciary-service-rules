## Rule-by-rule summary (verify against original for exact wording)
- **Rule 1** Short title; commencement 01.01.2016.
- **Rule 2** Application and exclusions (a)–(h): part-time; consolidated pay; contract (unless contract provides); contingency-paid; specified work-charged; daily-rated; retired on/before 31.12.2015 and re-employed on that date; others excluded by Government.
- **Rule 3** Definitions: "existing basic pay" (pay in pay band + grade pay under 2009 Rules), "existing emoluments" (existing basic pay + DA admissible on 01.01.2016, i.e. 125%), "**Pay Matrix**" (Annexure II — Levels **S-1 to S-34** horizontally, **Cells** vertically), "Level", "revised pay structure", "revised basic pay", "revised emoluments", "Schedule".
- **Rule 4** Level of pay of posts: every post carries the Level specified in the Schedule (department-wise index of Schedules in the PDF).
- **Rule 5** Drawal of pay in the revised structure; option to continue in existing structure until next increment/vacating post; **no option for appointees on or after 01.01.2016**.
- **Rule 6** Exercise of option — in **Annexure III** within one month of publication, with undertaking in **Annexure IV**; deemed option from 01.01.2016 if not exercised; option final.
- **Rule 7 – Fixation of pay in the revised structure**: existing basic pay × **2.57**, rounded to the nearest rupee; placed in the applicable Level at the **equal cell or next higher cell**; if below the first cell, fixed at the first cell. Provisos: **bunching** — one increment for every two stages bunched at the same cell; stagnation at maximum; officiating pay not below substantive pay; personal pay protection.
- **Rule 8** Direct recruits on or after 01.01.2016: pay at the **first cell (minimum) of the Level**.
- **Rule 9** Increments: movement **one cell down** in the same Level (3% built into the matrix).
- **Rule 10 – Date of increment** (key rule): two dates — **1 January and 1 July** — but only **one increment in a year**. Where appointment / promotion / financial upgradation takes effect **between 2 January and 1 July** (both inclusive) the first increment falls on **1 January** following; where it takes effect **between 2 July and 1 January** (both inclusive) the first increment falls on **1 July** following. After fixation on promotion, the next increment on completion of **six months' qualifying service** on the next increment date, thereafter annually. [Mirrors Rule 10 of CCS (Revised Pay) Rules 2016 — see file 05 for the DoE OM 28.11.2019 clarification on the central rule.]
- **Rule 11** Employees opting for the revised structure later — fixed under Rule 7(1)(A) on that date.
- **Rule 12** Re-appointment to a post held before 01.01.2016 — benefit of the proviso to **Rule 11 of the MCS (Pay) Rules 1981** as if the post were held on 01.01.2016.
- **Rule 13 – Pay on promotion on or after 01.01.2016**: **one increment in the existing Level** (next cell), then placed at the equal or next higher cell in the Level of the promotion post; **option** to fix from the date of promotion or from the date of next increment (1 Jan/1 July), to be exercised within one month.
- **Rule 14 – Arrears** for 01.01.2016–31.12.2018: credited to GPF / applicable pension account in **five equal annual instalments from FY 2019-20**; GPF withdrawals of those instalments barred for two years; cash to those leaving service. (Subsequent FD GRs modified the instalment schedule / allowed cash to certain categories — not verified here.)
- **Rule 15 – Overriding effect**: MCS (Pay) Rules 1981 do not apply to the extent inconsistent.
- **Rule 16 – Power to relax** (undue hardship, just and equitable).
- **Rule 17 – Interpretation**: Finance Department's decision final.
- **Annexures**: I (pay bands/grade pay to Level mapping), **II (Pay Matrix)**, III (option form), IV (undertaking), Schedule (department-wise posts and Levels; index of >25 departments in the PDF).

## How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner claims**: (a) pay fixation / bunching under Rule 7 and Level assignment (parity with central or other cadres); (b) **first increment after promotion** — whether 1 Jan or 1 July applies and whether six months' qualifying service is needed (Rule 10/13); (c) **notional increment for 30 June / 31 December retirees** under Rule 10; (d) arrears instalments in cash under Rule 14 (retirees, legal heirs); (e) denial of option under Rule 6/13.
- **State's usual stand**: Rules are statutory under Art. 309 and FD's interpretation under Rule 17 is final; fixation strictly per matrix; increment "accrues" only to an employee in service on 1 Jan/1 July (FR-17 principle); the FD GRs of 28.06.2023 / 19.12.2025 now concede notional increment **for pension fixation** to 30 June / 31 December retirees who completed the qualifying year with satisfactory service — so the State's current stand is to grant it within those GRs' limits (three-year arrears cap, pension-only scope per GoI pattern) and to resist extension beyond them (e.g., retirees before 01.01.2016 for the 1 January increment, gratuity/leave encashment recalculation, interest), subject to the FD's October 2026 direction to file reviews/hold action (file 05). For High Court staff the 7th CPC was extended by the High Court/Law & Judiciary Department by separate order adopting these Rules — plead that date.

## मराठी सारांश
वित्त विभागाच्या दि. ३०.०१.२०१९ च्या अधिसूचनेने (क्र. वेपुर-२०१९/प्र.क्र.१/सेवा-९) सातव्या वेतन आयोगानुसार सुधारित वेतन नियम २०१९ दि. ०१.०१.२०१६ पासून लागू झाले. नियम ७ – विद्यमान मूळ वेतन × २.५७ करून वेतन मॅट्रिक्समधील समान/पुढील टप्प्यावर निश्चिती; नियम ८ – नवीन नियुक्तांना स्तराच्या पहिल्या टप्प्यावर वेतन; **नियम १० – वेतनवाढीचे दोन दिनांक, १ जानेवारी व १ जुलै (वर्षात एकच वेतनवाढ)**; नियम १३ – पदोन्नतीवर एक वेतनवाढ देऊन वरच्या स्तरात निश्चिती व विकल्प; नियम १४ – थकबाकी पाच हप्त्यांत; नियम १५ – १९८१ च्या वेतन नियमांवर अधिभावी; नियम १७ – अर्थ लावण्याचा अंतिम अधिकार वित्त विभागाला. ३० जून / ३१ डिसेंबर रोजी निवृत्त होणाऱ्यांच्या काल्पनिक वेतनवाढीचे शासन निर्णय (२८.०६.२०२३, १९.१२.२०२५) याच नियम १० वर आधारित आहेत.
