## 6. How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner claims**: (a) appointed after 01.11.2005 but **selected/advertised before** that date — claims old pension under GR 02.02.2024 / parity with GoI OM 03.03.2023; (b) ad hoc/contract/CRT service before 01.11.2005 followed by regularisation — claims the pre-2005 date as "date of appointment"; (c) DCPS deductions not credited / no interest / no PRAN — claims interest and compensation; (d) death in service of NPS employee — family pension and gratuity (FD extended death-cum-retirement gratuity and family pension to NPS employees by GR dated 31.03.2023 — verify); (e) challenge to the cut-off 01.11.2005 as arbitrary (Art. 14).
- **State's usual stand**: the cut-off of 01.11.2005 is a policy decision under GR 31.10.2005, upheld by courts; Pension Rules 1982 Rule 2 excludes post-cut-off recruits; the 02.02.2024 GR is a concession limited to its terms (advertisement date, option window, finality); prior ad hoc/contract service on a non-sanctioned post is not "appointment to Government service"; DCPS accumulations with interest have been/are being transferred to NPS (Sevaarth records annexed); for court staff the appointing authority is the High Court/District Judge, and the Law & Judiciary Department GR of 19.03.2020 governs applicability to subordinate-court employees — plead the Registry's records.
