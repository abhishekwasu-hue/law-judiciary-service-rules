## 2. Group Personal Accident Insurance Scheme (समूह वैयक्तिक अपघात विमा योजना)
### FD GR संकीर्ण-2017/प्र.क्र.69/विमा प्रशासन, dated 11.08.2017 — scheme (extension to non-government institutions)
- Fetched (Shivaji University copy — verify): https://www.unishivaji.ac.in/uploads/admin/2023/GR/GR%2011-8-2017-24-01-2023.pdf
- Verbatim: > "यावर्षी दि.1 ऑगस्ट,2017 पासून ही योजना लागू करण्यात येत आहे." Original: sum insured Rs 10 lakh for Groups A–D, premium Rs 300 + GST; nomination compulsory; contract employees excluded; premium recovered from August salary; claims within 3 months with PF-rate interest on delay; exclusions (natural death, suicide, etc.).
### FD GR संकीर्ण-2019/प्र.क्र.64/विमा प्रशासन, dated 24.01.2023 — revised premium & sum insured from 01.04.2023
| Group | Sum insured | Premium + 18 % GST |
|---|---|---|
| A | Rs 25 lakh | 750 + 135 = 885 |
| B | Rs 20 lakh | 600 + 108 = 708 |
| C | Rs 15 lakh | 450 + 81 = 531 |
| D | Rs 15 lakh | 450 + 81 = 531 |
- Death/disability to be reported to Directorate of Insurance within one month; premium per the post actually held under time-bound promotion schemes.

## How it is used in litigation
- Heirs of deceased employees claim GIS insurance-fund amount plus interest and accident-insurance sum; disputes over nomination, over whether death was "accidental", and over non-recovery of premium by DDO (State's stand: no premium, no cover; DDO lapse is an inter se matter, often conceded with recovery from the officer).
