## Key provisions — summary (verify against original)

### Chapter II – General (Rules 9–12)
- **Rule 9 – Definitions** (summary): "pay", "time-scale of pay", "increment", "personal pay", "special pay", "substantive pay", "presumptive pay", "officiating", "cadre", etc. "Personal pay" = additional pay granted to save an employee from loss of substantive pay due to revision of pay or reduction of pay otherwise than as a penalty, or in exceptional circumstances on other personal considerations.
- **Rule 10 – Pay on first appointment** (summary): pay fixed at the minimum of the time-scale unless higher initial pay is sanctioned.
- **Rule 11 – Pay on appointment/promotion to a post carrying higher duties and responsibilities** (summary — the Maharashtra analogue of FR 22(I)(a)(1)):
  - **Rule 11(1)(a)**: where an employee holding a post (substantive / temporary / officiating) is promoted or appointed to another post carrying duties and responsibilities of **greater importance**, initial pay in the higher time-scale is fixed at the **stage next above the pay notionally arrived at by adding one increment** (at the stage at which it accrued) to the pay in the lower post. If there is no such stage, pay is fixed at the maximum.
  - **Rule 11(1)(b)**: where the new post does **not** carry duties of greater importance, pay is fixed at the stage equal to pay in the old post or, if no such stage, at the stage next above (no benefit of extra increment).
  - **Proviso / option**: the employee may elect to have pay fixed from the date of promotion or from the date of the next increment in the lower post (option to be exercised within the prescribed period; this option concept is carried into Rule 13 of the 2009 Rules and Rule 13 of the 2019 Rules).
  - **Proviso (re-appointment)**: on re-appointment to a post previously held, pay is not fixed lower than what would have been drawn had the employee continued in that post (referred to in Rule 12 of the 2019 Rules as "the proviso to Rule 11 of the MCS (Pay) Rules, 1981").
  - **Rule 11(2)/(3)** (summary): fixation where appointment is to a post on an identical scale; pay on reversion; pay on transfer to a lower post at own request.
- **Rule 12** (summary): pay when a lower post is held in an officiating capacity / "presumptive pay".

### Chapter on Increments (Rules 36–41)
- **Rule 36 – Conditions for drawal of increment** (summary): an increment accrues as a matter of course on the due date unless **withheld** by order of the competent authority; an order withholding an increment must state the period and whether it has the effect of postponing future increments (penalty under the MCS (Discipline & Appeal) Rules 1979). The competent authority must certify that the employee's work and conduct were satisfactory. (This is the rule the FD circular of 29.04.2009 preserves for the 1 July increment.)
- **Rule 37** (summary): increment in a time-scale is granted from the first of the month? — **Maharashtra practice before 2006**: increment was drawn from the actual anniversary date; **not** advanced to the 1st of the month. Verify.
- **Rule 38** (summary): efficiency bar — crossing only on certificate of fitness (largely obsolete after 6th CPC).
- **Rule 39 – Periods counting for increment** (summary): all **duty** in a post on a time-scale, **service in a higher post**, **leave with pay** (all kinds of leave other than extraordinary leave), **joining time**, **deputation/foreign service**, count for increment in the time-scale. **Extraordinary leave (असाधारण रजा)** does **not** count unless (a) granted on medical certificate, or (b) sanctioned by the competent authority on the ground that the employee could not join duty for reasons beyond his control / for prosecuting higher studies in public interest — in which case the competent authority may order that the EOL shall count for increment. Suspension period counts for increment only if treated as duty / regularised as leave with pay under the MCS (Joining Time, Foreign Service and Payments during Suspension, Dismissal and Removal) Rules 1981. [Verify sub-rule numbering against original.]
- **Rule 40 / 41** (summary): increment on return from leave; pay on re-employment.

### Stepping up of pay (anomaly between senior and junior)
- Provided in the **Notes/instructions under Rule 11** and in FD orders consolidated in the **Compendium of Pay Fixation, 1992** (summary, verify): where a senior, promoted earlier, draws less pay than a junior promoted later in the same cadre because of the operation of the fixation rule, the senior's pay may be **stepped up** to equal the junior's, from the date of the junior's fixation, subject to the usual conditions: (i) both belong to the same cadre and are promoted to identical posts in the same cadre; (ii) the senior was senior in the lower post and was drawing equal or more pay there; (iii) the anomaly is a direct result of the fixation rule and not of advance increments, personal pay or option exercised by the junior; (iv) the senior's next increment is drawn on completion of the required qualifying service from the date of stepping up. Stepping up is **not** admissible where the junior's higher pay results from a qualification/advance increment, EOL of the senior, or the senior's own option.

### Personal pay (वैयक्तिक वेतन)
- Granted to protect pay on revision (e.g., Note under Rule 7 of the 2009 Rules: if existing emoluments exceed revised emoluments, the difference is allowed as personal pay to be absorbed in future increases) and on reduction of pay otherwise than as penalty. (Summary, verify.)

### Annexures / Schedules (summary)
- Annexures contain the forms of option, the pay-fixation statement proforma, and illustrations. The 1992 "Compendium of Pay Fixation" (FD, First Edition) consolidates the fixation rules, illustrations and GRs up to 1992.

## How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner claims**: (a) pay on promotion wrongly fixed under Rule 11(1)(b) instead of 11(1)(a) ("higher duties and responsibilities"); (b) denial of stepping-up vis-à-vis junior; (c) EOL period wrongly excluded for increment (Rule 39); (d) increment withheld without a speaking order (Rule 36); (e) pay protection / personal pay on transfer or re-appointment.
- **State's usual stand**: fixation strictly per the rule and the FD Compendium; stepping-up only where the anomaly is a *direct* result of the fixation rule and all conditions are satisfied (relying on Supreme Court law that a junior's higher pay by reason of advance increments/option does not entitle stepping-up); EOL without medical certificate or without a specific order under Rule 39 does not count; for High Court non-judicial staff, the Bombay High Court Appellate Side / Original Side Service Rules apply first, and MCS (Pay) Rules apply by adoption only where those rules are silent (Art. 229(2) — see file 70). Any relief sought contrary to the 2009/2019 Rules is met with the overriding-effect rules (Rule 16 of 2009 / Rule 15 of 2019).

## मराठी सारांश
महाराष्ट्र नागरी सेवा (वेतन) नियम, १९८१ हे वित्त विभागाचे नियम असून ते १६.०८.१९८१ पासून लागू आहेत. नियम ११ नुसार पदोन्नतीवर एक वेतनवाढ देऊन पुढील टप्प्यावर वेतन निश्चित होते; नियम ३६ मध्ये वेतनवाढ मिळण्याच्या अटी (समाधानकारक काम व वर्तणूक, रोखण्याचा आदेश) व नियम ३९ मध्ये वेतनवाढीसाठी कोणते कालावधी गणले जातात (असाधारण रजा वगळून) हे दिले आहे. सुधारित वेतन नियम २००९ (नियम १६) व २०१९ (नियम १५) विसंगत असतील तेवढ्या मर्यादेत या नियमांवर मात करतात. अधिकृत प्रत वित्त विभागाच्या संकेतस्थळावर स्कॅन स्वरूपात आहे; येथे दिलेला मजकूर सारांश आहे — मूळ नियमाशी पडताळून घ्यावा.
