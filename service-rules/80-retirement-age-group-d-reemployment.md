# Retirement age (Group D — 60 years; others — 58 years), re-employment and extension
## सेवानिवृत्तीचे वय (गट-ड — ६० वर्षे; इतर — ५८ वर्षे), पुनर्नियुक्ती व मुदतवाढ

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Governing rule | **Maharashtra Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1982 — Rule 10** (age of superannuation): Government servants in **Class IV (Group D) retire on attaining 60 years**; all other Government servants on attaining **58 years** (with specified exceptions, e.g. certain medical/teaching posts). **Official text not fetched — not verified** (copy hosted by VJTI college found in search: https://vjti.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/MCS-Pension-Rules-1982-modifed-up-to-31st-july-1992.pdf — non-official, not used). |
| Judicial officers | Retirement at 60 (Supreme Court, AIJA 19.05.2023 — verified, file 72). |
| High Court staff | Age of superannuation as per Chief Justice's rules under Art. 229 (pension rules need Governor's approval); generally follows the State pattern (58 / 60 for Group D) — not verified. |
| Demands to raise Group A–C age to 60 | Pending demand; no GR raising the general retirement age to 60 located on finance.maharashtra.gov.in — **not verified / no such GR found**. (FD GR of 2015–2016 raising age of Government doctors to 60/62 and of teachers etc. are sector-specific — not verified.) |
| Re-employment / extension | MCS (Pension) Rules 1982 — Rule 10 provisos and GAD/FD instructions permit **extension of service** only in exceptional public interest with Government sanction; **re-employment after superannuation** on contract/honorarium basis under GAD GR on engaging retired employees (commonly cited GAD GR dated 17.12.2016 — number not verified) with ceiling of age 65 and pay minus pension; prohibited where regular candidates are available. |

### Key principles — summary (verify against original)
- Date of retirement: afternoon of the last day of the month in which the employee attains the age (if born on the 1st, retires on the last day of the previous month) — Rule 10 / FD instructions.
- Date of birth recorded in the service book at entry is conclusive; alteration only under GAD rules within the prescribed period after entry (commonly within 5 years) with documentary proof — GAD GR number not verified.
- Group D employee **promoted to Group C** retires at 58 (the age attaches to the post held, not the entry post) — standard State stand; verify Rule 10 wording.
- Compulsory/premature retirement at 50/55 or after 30 years' qualifying service under Rule 10(4)/(5) read with GAD instructions (periodic review) — not verified.

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims:** Group D employee (peon/hamal) retired at 58 instead of 60; employee promoted from Group D to Group C claims 60; correction of date of birth near retirement; re-employment/extension claimed as right; premature retirement challenged.
- **State's / High Court's stand:** Rule 10 — 60 years only for Class IV; on promotion to Class III the Class III age (58) applies; date of birth as per service book, no change at the fag end of service (State of Maharashtra v. Gorakhnath Sitaram Kamble (2010) 14 SCC 423); extension/re-employment is discretionary; premature retirement in public interest is not punishment.

### मराठी सारांश
- महाराष्ट्र नागरी सेवा (निवृत्तिवेतन) नियम १९८२, नियम १०: वर्ग-४ (गट-ड) कर्मचारी ६० व्या वर्षी, इतर ५८ व्या वर्षी निवृत्त (अधिकृत मजकूर पडताळलेला नाही).
- गट-ड मधून गट-क मध्ये पदोन्नत झालेल्या कर्मचाऱ्यास ५८ वर्षेच लागू — शासनाची नेहमीची भूमिका.
- मुदतवाढ/पुनर्नियुक्ती फक्त अपवादात्मक सार्वजनिक हितासाठी, शासन मान्यतेने; जन्मतारखेत सेवेच्या अखेरीस बदल नाही.
- न्यायिक अधिकाऱ्यांचे निवृत्ती वय ६० (सर्वोच्च न्यायालय १९.०५.२०२३).
