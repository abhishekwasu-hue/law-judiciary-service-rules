### Key provisions (verbatim quotes marked " ")
**Section 2 — definitions:** (a) "appointing authority" — "the authority empowered to make appointment to such services or posts"; (b) De-notified Tribes — "the Tribes declared as such by the Government from time to time"; (f) Nomadic Tribes — "the Tribes wandering from place to place in search of their livelihood as declared by Government from time to time"; (g) OBC — "any socially and educationally backward classes of citizens as declared by the Government"; (j) "recruitment year" — "the English calendar year during which the recruitment is actually made"; (m) Special Backward Category — "socially and educationally backward classes of citizens declared as a Special Backward Category by the Government."

**Section 3(1) — exemptions:** Act does not apply to "the super specialised posts in Medical, Technical and Educational field"; "the posts to be filled by transfer or deputation"; "the temporary appointments of less than forty-five days duration"; "the posts which is single (isolated) in any cadre or grade."

**Section 4 — reservation.** 4(1): reserved posts "shall not be filled in by the candidates not belonging to that caste". 4(2) direct recruitment percentages:

| Category | % |
|---|---|
| Scheduled Castes | 13 |
| Scheduled Tribes | 7 |
| De-notified Tribes (A) | 3 |
| Nomadic Tribes (B) | 2.5 |
| Nomadic Tribes (C) | 3.5 |
| Nomadic Tribes (D) | 2 |
| Special Backward Category | 2 |
| Other Backward Classes | 19 |
| **Total** | **52** |

Proviso: "the principle of 'Creamy Layer' shall be applicable to all categories mentioned above" except SC and ST; Explanation II: creamy layer "as declared by Government". 4(3): categories at serial nos. (3) to (6) (VJ-A, NT-B, NT-C, NT-D) "shall be inter transferable" — if candidates unavailable in the same recruitment year, "the posts shall be filled by appointing suitable candidates from any of the other said categories." 4(4): for certain Group C and D posts recruited at divisional/district level, reservation "maintained at such divisional or district level only."

**Section 5 — reservation in promotion.** 5(1): "The reservation in promotion shall be at all stages of promotions." 5(2): existing promotion-reservation orders "shall continue to be in force unless modified or revoked, by Government." (Operation of promotion reservation is now governed by the GR 25.05.2004 litigation — see file 31.)

**Section 6 — carry forward.** 6(1): unfilled reserved vacancy "shall be carried forward upto five years in case of direct recruitment and three years in case of promotion." 6(2): carried-forward vacancy "shall not be counted against the quota ... for the recruitment year to which it is carried forward." Proviso: appointing authority "may, at any time undertake a special recruitment drive".

**Section 7** — Government may entrust appointing authorities/officers "with the responsibility of ensuring the compliance". **Section 8 — penalty:** wilful contravention — "imprisonment for a term which may extend to ninety days or fine which may extend to five thousand rupees, or with both"; cognizance only with previous sanction of Government (s. 8(2)).

The Act contains **no roster section**; rosters (बिंदुनामावली) are prescribed by GAD GRs/circulars (e.g. GAD circular 16.03.1999 एसआरव्ही 1097/प्र.क्र.31/98/16-अ; roster to be validated by the Backward Class Cell / Divisional Commissioner's office — see file 32).

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims:** roster not followed / backlog not filled (s. 6); reserved post filled by open candidate (s. 4(1)); inter-transferability misapplied (s. 4(3)); promotion reservation at every stage (s. 5(1)) denied after 2017 HC judgment; creamy-layer applied wrongly; district-level roster (s. 4(4)) ignored for Group C/D court staff.
- **State's / High Court's stand:** reservation applies post-wise per validated roster; isolated/single posts excluded (s. 3(1)); carry-forward limited to 5/3 years; for court staff the High Court is the appointing authority and maintains rosters; promotions since 2017 made by seniority under GR 07.05.2021 and are temporary, subject to SLP 28306/2017; penal s. 8 needs sanction.
