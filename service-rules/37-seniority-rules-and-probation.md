### Key principles — summary (verify against original text of the 1982 Rules)
- Rule 4 (general): seniority in a cadre is determined by the date of **regular appointment** to the post (not ad-hoc/temporary/fortuitous service); for direct recruits, by order of merit in the select list; for promotees, by order in the DPC select list; direct recruits and promotees interleaved as per quota/rota in the recruitment rules; where quota-rota not followed, continuous officiation counts only from the date the appointment is within the quota.
- Rule on "deemed date" — seniority given from a notional date only where rules/GR so provide (e.g. promotion delayed for no fault, sealed-cover cases).
- Seniority of reserved-category employees promoted under GR 25.05.2004 — now governed by GR 07.05.2021 (file 31).
- Gradation lists (ज्येष्ठतासूची): GAD requires provisional list, objections, final list annually; an employee who does not object to a published list within time is generally precluded (laches).
- Probation: two years from date of appointment; satisfactory completion certified by the appointing authority; termination of a probationer for unsatisfactory work is not punitive if no stigma (simpliciter); confirmation not automatic but deemed where rules so say (Dharam Singh / Maharashtra rules differ — verify).

### Court-staff specific
- Seniority of Bombay High Court staff is governed by the High Court's own rules under Article 229 (file 70) and for District Court staff by the rules framed by the High Court / Government under Article 235 and the Bombay Civil Courts Act, 1869 (file 71); the State's 1982 Rules apply only where adopted.

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims:** seniority from date of ad-hoc appointment; inter-se seniority between direct recruits and promotees; delayed promotion causing loss of seniority; deemed date; challenge to final gradation list; termination during/after probation without inquiry.
- **State's stand:** ad-hoc/stop-gap service not counted (Direct Recruit Class II Engineering Officers' Assn. (1990) principles); seniority list finalised after objections; delay/laches; probationer has no right to the post; for court staff — High Court administration is the competent authority and the State is a formal party.
