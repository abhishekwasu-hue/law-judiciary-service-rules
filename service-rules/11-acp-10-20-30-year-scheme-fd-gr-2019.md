### Companion GR — pay fixation of earlier ACP beneficiaries
- **FD GR वेतन-2019/प्र.क्र.23/सेवा-3 dated 01.03.2019** (same source): fixation as on 01.01.2016 for employees who had received the 1st or 2nd ACP benefit before 01.01.2016. Summary (verified from fetched text): employees with a functional promotion before 01.01.2016 are fixed under Rule 7 / Rule 11 of the FD Notification वेपुर-2019/प्र.क्र.1/सेवा-9 dated 30.01.2019 (7th CPC pay rules); those with a promotional hierarchy but no functional promotion are fixed at the level of the promotional post; isolated-post employees are moved to the next level (one benefit) or next-to-next level (two benefits); fitment factor 2.57, rounded to the next matrix cell. Also cites FD Circular वेपुर-2019/प्र.क्र.8/सेवा-9 dated 20.02.2019.

### Key provisions of GR 02.03.2019 (summary of fetched text; quotes marked)
1. **Scheme:** "तीन लाभांच्या सुधारित सेवांतर्गत आश्वासित प्रगती योजना" — three non-functional financial benefits on completion of **10, 20 and 30 years** of regular continuous service without promotion, w.e.f. **01.01.2016**, replacing the 12/24-year structure.
2. **Coverage ceiling:** employees drawing pay in pay-matrix levels **up to S-20** (6th CPC equivalent PB ₹15,600–39,100 + GP ₹5,400). Quote: "वेतन मॅट्रिक्समधील वेतनस्तर एस-20 पर्यंत वेतन घेणाऱ्या कर्मचारी". Employees who reach S-21 or above by any means are outside the scheme. The GR speaks of State Government employees and ZP / other teaching staff; it does not separately list Group A/B/C/D.
3. **Maximum three benefits in the entire service.** Two functional promotions in the same chain → only one ACP benefit; **three functional promotions → no benefit** (quote: "ज्या कर्मचारी / अधिकारी याना तीन कार्यात्मक पदोन्नत्या मिळाल्या आहेत" are excluded). A benefit already given in a chain is not counted again as a promotion.
4. **Nature of benefit:** for employees in a promotion chain — pay level of the promotional post; for isolated posts (एकाकी पद) — the immediately next pay level in the matrix. A non-functional higher scale granted after a fixed number of years under recruitment rules counts as the first (or second) benefit.
5. **Conditions (promotion-chain benefit):** qualification, seniority, eligibility, prescribed **departmental examination**, ACR grading and caste validity certificate (where required) must be satisfied, confirmed by the **DPC**; no departmental inquiry / judicial case pending (per GAD GR एसआरव्ही-2015/प्र.क्र.310/काया-12 dated 15.12.2017). The fetched text does **not** expressly address refusal of promotion — verify in the original and in subsequent FD clarifications before relying on it.
6. **Transition from 12/24 scheme:** first benefit at 12 years → second at 12+8 = 20 years; second at 24 years → third at 24+6 = 30 years; second at 20 years → third at 20+10 = 30 years. Quote: "यापूर्वीच्या योजनेनुसार 12/24 वर्षांनंतर". Benefits sanctioned in 2015–2018 on the old pattern are revised from the new eligibility date and differences adjusted.
7. **Fixation:** in the matrix as on 01.01.2016 or the eligibility date; earlier sanction orders are cancelled and fresh orders issued; differential paid. Retirees/deceased covered (text truncated in source — verify).
8. **Reference list** of 29 earlier orders (GAD 1995, 1997; FD 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2007/2008, 2009, 2010 ×4, 2011, 2013, 2014, 2015 ×4, 2016 ×3, 2017; GAD 15.12.2017; FD Notification 30.01.2019; FD Circular 20.02.2019; FD GR 01.03.2019) — full table reproduced in file 10.

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims:** 10-year benefit denied although no promotion was available; denial for not passing departmental exam which was never held; counting of pre-2016 benefits; demand that the ACP level be the promotional post's level and not merely the next cell; stenographers/clerks of courts claiming the benefit from 01.01.2016 with arrears; claim that temporary/ad-hoc service counts.
- **State's usual stand:** benefit is financial, not a promotion, and strictly conditional on promotion-eligibility (exam, ACR, no DE); only regular continuous service counts; three-promotion / S-20 ceilings; transition formula applies; for court staff the sanctioning authority is the High Court / District Judge under Article 229 / Article 235 — Government only provides the scheme and budget; petition without first approaching the DPC/appointing authority is premature.
