## A. Verified content — 2016 Amendment Rules (from the fetched gazette; the fetch tool returns paraphrase, so treat as close summary, not verbatim)

**Rule 2 (definitions) substituted**: "Head of Department", "major penalty" (= penalties in clauses (v) to (ix) of rule 5(1)), "minor penalty" (= clauses (i) to (iv) of rule 5(1)), "Regional Head of Department".

**Rule 5(1) substituted — penalties** (post-2016 list):

*Minor penalties*
- (i) censure (ठपका);
- (ii) withholding of promotion;
- (iii) recovery from pay of the whole or part of any pecuniary loss caused to Government by negligence or breach of orders;
- (iv) withholding of increments of pay (with or without cumulative effect — verify wording).

*Major penalties*
- (v) reduction to a lower stage in the time-scale of pay for a specified period, with directions as to whether increments are postponed / whether it has effect on future increments (verify wording);
- (vi) reduction to a lower time-scale of pay, grade, post or service, with or without restoration directions;
- (vii) compulsory retirement;
- (viii) removal from service — not a disqualification for future Government employment;
- (ix) dismissal from service — ordinarily a disqualification for future Government employment.

*Explanation* amended: certain matters are **not** penalties (e.g., withholding increment for failure to pass departmental exam, non-promotion on merits, reversion of probationer / officiating employee, compulsory retirement under pension rules, termination under contract/temporary rules); 2016 added that **compensation ordered after a sexual-harassment complaint** is not a penalty under these rules.

**Rule 6 substituted (in part)**: Heads of Department may impose minor penalties on Group A officers with grade pay Rs. 6,600 or less (excluding the pre-revised Rs. 10,650–15,850 scale); Divisional Commissioners given penalty powers for EGS staff (Groups A–D).

**Rule 8(7)**: "on within such further time" corrected to "or within such further time". **Rule 9(2)**: "favourable or not to the said Government servant". **Rule 10(2)**: deleted the power to impose penalties under rule 5(1)(v)/(vi) through the minor-penalty procedure. **Rule 18(1)(i)**: deleted the words "(Group A and Group B service)". **Annexure** to the principal rules deleted.

## B. Structure of the principal rules — summary (verify against original)
Rule numbers below are as commonly cited in GAD GRs and Bombay HC / MAT orders; confirm against the printed GAD edition.

| Rule | Subject | Gist |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Application | All State Government servants except AIS, persons under other rules. |
| **4** | **Suspension** | (1) Appointing authority / disciplinary authority / authority empowered by Government may suspend (a) where a disciplinary proceeding is contemplated or pending; (b) where a criminal case is under investigation/trial. (2) **Deemed suspension**: detention in custody exceeding 48 hours; conviction with sentence exceeding 48 hours. (3)/(4) Suspension continues on setting aside of dismissal/removal in appeal/review or by court and further inquiry ordered. (5) Suspension order remains in force until modified/revoked; may be revoked at any time. Later sub-rules / GAD instructions require **periodic review of suspension** (GAD review-committee GRs — **numbers not verified**). |
| **5** | Penalties | As in Part A. |
| 6 | Disciplinary authorities | Governor/Government; appointing authority; authorities in Schedule; Heads of Department (minor penalties). |
| 7 | Authority to institute proceedings | Government or authority empowered may institute; a lower authority may institute even if not competent to impose the penalty. |
| **8** | **Procedure for imposing major penalties** | No major penalty without inquiry; disciplinary authority draws up (i) articles of charge, (ii) statement of imputations, (iii) list of documents, (iv) list of witnesses; charge-sheet delivered; written statement within prescribed time; appointment of Inquiry Officer (8(2)–(5)) and **Presenting Officer (8(5)(b),(c))**; inspection of documents by charged officer (**8(11)**); examination/cross-examination; defence; inquiry report with findings on each article; *ex-parte* inquiry on non-appearance; report forwarded to disciplinary authority. |
| **9** | Action on the inquiry report | Disciplinary authority may remit for further inquiry, disagree with findings recording reasons, furnish copy of report to charged officer for representation; **9(3): consultation with MPSC** where required (see GAD circular 28.01.2025, file 52). |
| **10** | **Procedure for imposing minor penalties** | Notice of the proposed action with imputations; representation to be considered; no oral inquiry needed unless withholding of increments with cumulative effect / for a long period (then inquiry as in Rule 8 — verify); record of proceedings. After 2016, Rule 10 cannot be used to impose rule 5(1)(v)/(vi) penalties. |
| 11 | Communication of orders | Copy of order, inquiry report and findings to the Government servant. |
| 12 | Common proceedings | Two or more employees in one inquiry. |
| 13 | Special procedure in certain cases | Dispensing with inquiry where (i) penalty is on the ground of conduct leading to conviction on a criminal charge; (ii) authority records in writing that it is not reasonably practicable to hold inquiry; (iii) security of State (Article 311(2) proviso). GAD circular 21.02.2015 cites **Rule 13(4)** for closure of inquiry on death of the employee (verify sub-rule). |
| 14–15 | Officers on deputation / lent to other authorities | — |
| 16 | Orders against which no appeal lies | Interlocutory orders etc. |
| 17 | Orders against which appeal lies | Suspension, penalties, orders affecting pay/seniority etc. |
| 18 | Appellate authority | Generally the authority next above the one that passed the order; Governor for orders by Government (18(1)(i) amended in 2016). |
| 19 | Period of limitation for appeals | **45 days** from the date of receipt of the order (extendable for sufficient cause) — verify. |
| 20–23 | Form and contents of appeal; submission; withholding; transmission | Appeal to be in own name, in proper language, without disrespectful language; submitted through the authority that passed the order. |
| 24 | Consideration of appeal | Appellate authority considers whether procedure complied with, findings justified, penalty adequate / excessive; may confirm, enhance (after notice), reduce or set aside, or remit. |
| 25 | Implementation of appellate orders | — |
| 26 | Revision | Governor / Government / Head of Department may revise suo motu or on application within specified period (generally not after six months of the appeal order, and revision only after appeal period — verify). |
| 27 | Review | Governor may review any order on new material / evidence. |
| 28–31 | Service of orders; power to relax time; savings; repeal | — |

**Subsistence allowance** is governed not by these rules but by the MCS (Joining Time, Foreign Service and Payments during Suspension, Dismissal and Removal) Rules, 1981 (file 55).

## How it is used in litigation
- **Typical petitioner claims**: no inquiry / Rule 8 not followed (documents not supplied, no PO, ex-parte inquiry unjustified); IO report not furnished before penalty; disciplinary authority disagreed without reasons; penalty disproportionate; minor-penalty procedure (Rule 10) used for a major penalty (e.g., withholding increments with cumulative effect); suspension prolonged without review / charge-sheet; appellate authority passed non-speaking order; DE after retirement barred (Pension Rule 27); delay in DE beyond GAD time-limits vitiates proceedings; subsistence allowance not paid.
- **State's usual stand**: inquiry held in compliance with Rule 8 and principles of natural justice; charged officer participated and cross-examined; report furnished and representation considered under Rule 9; penalty within the disciplinary authority's discretion and proportionate (judicial review limited — *Wednesbury* / *B.C. Chaturvedi*); GAD time-limits are directory, delay explained by the employee's own adjournments; suspension reviewed periodically and subsistence allowance paid; Rule 27 pension-cut proceedings within the four-year window; MPSC consulted under Rule 9(3). For High Court staff, the disciplinary authority is the Registrar General / Chief Justice under the High Court's own rules — verify delegation before pleading.

## मराठी सारांश
महाराष्ट्र नागरी सेवा (शिस्त व अपील) नियम, १९७९ मध्ये निलंबन (नियम ४), शिक्षा (नियम ५ — २०१६ च्या सुधारणेनंतर किरकोळ शिक्षा (i)–(iv) व मोठ्या शिक्षा (v)–(ix)), मोठ्या शिक्षेसाठी चौकशीची पद्धत (नियम ८), चौकशी अहवालावरील कार्यवाही व लोकसेवा आयोगाचा सल्ला (नियम ९), किरकोळ शिक्षेची पद्धत (नियम १०), अपील (४५ दिवस), पुनरीक्षण व पुनर्विलोकन यांची तरतूद आहे. २०१६ ची सुधारणा अधिसूचना (क्र. वशिअ-१२१३/प्र.क्र.४३/११, दि. १६.०७.२०१६) अधिकृत संकेतस्थळावरून पडताळली आहे; मूळ नियमांचा मजकूर मिळाला नसल्याने त्याचा सारांश छापील प्रतीशी ताडून घ्यावा. विभागीय चौकशीच्या कालमर्यादा, सादरकर्ता अधिकारी, नोडल अधिकारी, बंद लिफाफा व सेवानिवृत्तीनंतरची चौकशी यावरील शासन निर्णय फाईल ५२ मध्ये आहेत.
