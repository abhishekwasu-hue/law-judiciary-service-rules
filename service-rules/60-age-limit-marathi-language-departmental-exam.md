### Key provisions verified (17.03.2018 notification, Marathi Language Dept) — illustrative of standard clauses
- Appointment by nomination through MPSC; age not more than 38 years; relaxation up to 5 years for Government servants and backward-class candidates; degree with Hindi; Rule 5 — training as prescribed; Rule 6 — post-recruitment examination; Rule 7 — Marathi and Hindi language examinations; Rule 8 — Computer Operation certificate from the Directorate of IT (MS-CIT or equivalent).
- Promotion post (Supervisor): from Translators with 5 years' regular service who have **passed the departmental examination**, on seniority subject to fitness.

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims:** age bar applied though relaxation available (backward class / Government servant / Divyang / ex-serviceman / sportsperson / project-affected); Marathi exam exemption claimed on the basis of SSC with Marathi; departmental exam not conducted for years so promotion/ACP denied; demand for exemption from exam at age 50+ (GAD practice of exemption — not verified).
- **State's stand:** age and qualification are judged on the cut-off date in the advertisement/recruitment rules; relaxation only as expressly provided; language exam is a condition of service applicable to all; departmental exam passing is a condition precedent for promotion/ACP and non-holding of exams can be remedied by holding exams, not by waiver; for court staff the High Court prescribes and conducts the departmental exams.
