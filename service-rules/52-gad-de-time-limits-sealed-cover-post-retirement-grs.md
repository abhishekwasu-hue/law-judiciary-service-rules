## 1. GR विशअ-2025/प्र.क्र.35/विचौ-1, dt. 10 June 2025 — Stage-wise time limits for completing DE
**Source**: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s398e6f17209029f4ae6dc9d88ec8eac2c/uploads/2025/07/20250716660371345.pdf
**Subject**: विभागीय चौकशी पूर्ण करण्यासाठी टप्पेनिहाय कालावधी विहित करणे.
**Revises**: GR प्रविचौ-2008/प्र.क्र.11/08/11-अ, dt. 07.04.2008 (earlier time-limit GR).

| Stage | Time limit |
|---|---|
| Preliminary inquiry where irregularity is alleged | 2 months |
| Decision to hold Rule 8 inquiry → issue of charge-sheet | within 1 month of decision; charge-sheet preparation and service overall 3 months |
| Written statement of defence by employee | 15 days (extendable up to 45 days) |
| Decision on reply; appointment of Inquiry Officer and Presenting Officer | 1 month after reply (or 1 month if no reply) |
| Inquiry Officer's proceedings | **maximum 9 months** (extension to be sought with reasons) |
| Disciplinary authority's consideration of report; furnishing copy / disagreement note | 1 month |
| Employee's representation on inquiry report | 15 days |
| Final order — Group C & D | 1 month |
| Group A & B — consideration, GAD/competent-authority approval, reference to MPSC | 2 months |
| Final order after MPSC advice | 1 month |
| **Overall limit, Rule 8 inquiries of Group A/B and joint cases** | **18 months** ("18 महिने इतका कालावधी निश्चित करण्यात येत आहे") |
| Rule 10 (minor penalty) proceedings | 3 months from sanction |

Disciplinary authorities to take care that DE is completed as above ("विभागीय चौकशीची कार्यवाही वरीलप्रमाणे पूर्ण करण्याची दक्षता शिस्तभंगविषयक प्राधिकाऱ्यांनी घ्यावी"); exceeding a stage limit requires recorded reasons.

## 2. GR संकीर्ण-1025/प्र.क्र.49/विचौ-2, dt. 11 July 2025 — Measures for speedy disposal; Nodal Officer
**Source**: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s398e6f17209029f4ae6dc9d88ec8eac2c/uploads/2025/07/202507161238179627.pdf
- Deputy/Joint Secretary handling DE in each Mantralaya department is the **Nodal Officer**; senior officer in field offices.
- Nodal Officer must know Rule 8 of D&A Rules 1979; ensure first hearing starts within prescribed period; take **mandatory written monthly review** from the Vigilance (सतर्कता) Officer; warn and then recommend action against a negligent Vigilance Officer; submit monthly progress report to disciplinary authority; is himself liable for disciplinary action on default.
- **Supersedes**: GR सीडीआर-1691/प्र.क्र.149/91/11-अ dt. 05.01.1993; GR सीडीआर-2000/प्र.क्र.10/2000/11-अ dt. 31.10.2000; GR संकीर्ण-1018/प्र.क्र.30/11-अ dt. 20.06.2018. **Refers to**: GR विदशअ-1324/प्र.क्र.30/विचौ-1 dt. 12.03.2025; circular विदशअ-1325/प्र.क्र.45/विचौ-1 dt. 25.04.2025.

## 3. Circular वशिअ-1325/प्र.क्र.45/विचौ-1, dt. 25 April 2025 — Duties of Presenting Officer
**Source**: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s398e6f17209029f4ae6dc9d88ec8eac2c/uploads/2025/04/202504282027722228.pdf
- PO appointed by disciplinary authority under Rule 8(5)(b)/(c); personal to the post-holder; standard appointment-order format (Annexure 1) and duty guidelines (Annexure 2): pre-hearing analysis, allow inspection of documents (Rule 8(11)), prepare and examine witnesses, cross-examine defence witnesses, written brief after hearing; no unnecessary adjournments; non-cooperation of PO reported by IO → disciplinary authority fixes responsibility and initiates action against PO.
- **Refers to**: GR विशिअ-1214/प्र.क्र.22/11 dt. **22.08.2014** (detailed PO guidelines); GR संकीर्ण-2024/प्र.क्र.57/विचौ-2 dt. **06.11.2024** (IO appointment-order format; checklist of documents to accompany charge papers).

## 7. Circular वनिअ-1315/प्र.क्र.5/11, dt. 21 February 2015 — DE of retired / deceased employees (links to Pension Rule 27)
**Source**: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s398e6f17209029f4ae6dc9d88ec8eac2c/uploads/2023/08/2023080320.pdf
- Where preliminary inquiry discloses substance, DE should start **at least 3 months before retirement**; if it must start at/after retirement, complete within **1 year** ("चौकशीची संपूर्ण कार्यवाही १ वर्षात पूर्ण करणे बंधनकारक राहील").
- DE pending at retirement: complete within **6 months of retirement**; DE begun after retirement: within 6 months of start. Outcomes reflected in CRs of the Dy/Joint Secretary and IOs.
- After final penalty order or after **notice under Rule 72 of MCS (Joining Time, Foreign Service and Payments during Suspension, Dismissal and Removal) Rules, 1981**, the suspension period must be regularised and pay/allowances sanctioned **within 1 month**.
- On death of the employee: DE to be closed forthwith under **Rule 13(4) of D&A Rules** (as cited in the circular); orders within 15 days; delay beyond a month attracts accountability.
- Annual departmental and quarterly GAD review of pending cases.
- **Refers to**: GR सीडीआर-1097/156/प्र.क्र.14/97/अकरा dt. 24.02.1997; GR सीडीआर-1010/प्र.क्र.56/11 dt. 30.10.2010; GR निप्रआ-1112/प्र.क्र.82/11-अ dt. 23.04.2013; GR एसआरव्ही-2014/प्र.क्र.25/2014/12 dt. 04.02.2014.
- GAD category page (robots-blocked, open manually): https://gad.maharashtra.gov.in/document-category/%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B5%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%80-%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A7%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%85%E0%A4%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%80-%E0%A4%B5/

### Pension Rule 27 — summary (verify against original; text not fetched)
MCS (Pension) Rules, 1982, **Rule 27**: Governor's right to withhold or withdraw pension (wholly or partly, permanently or for a period) and to order recovery of pecuniary loss if the pensioner is found guilty of grave misconduct or negligence in a departmental or judicial proceeding; **27(2)(a)**: a departmental proceeding instituted while in service is deemed to continue after retirement as if the pensioner were in service; **27(2)(b)**: a proceeding not instituted while in service may be instituted after retirement only (i) with the sanction of the Governor/Government, (ii) in respect of an event **not more than four years before** institution, (iii) by the authority and in the manner applicable to serving employees. Explanation: proceeding is "instituted" on the date the charge-sheet is issued (or suspension for proceeding, if earlier); judicial proceeding on the date of complaint/police report/plaint. Official page of Pension Rules (Marathi, robots-blocked): https://finance.maharashtra.gov.in/publication/%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%B9%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B7%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%9F%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0-%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%97%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%80-%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%87%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BE-%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF/

## 8. Sealed cover procedure (बंद लिफाफा / मोहोरबंद पाकीट)
**Source actually fetched**: GAD GR **एसआरव्ही-2018/प्र.क्र.159/कार्यासन 12, dt. 01 August 2019** — consolidated promotion procedure — https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s392c5ad73d34a8c18d276a0f0b60ea745/uploads/2025/04/202504251809348302.pdf
- Employees against whom a **DE is pending, a criminal case is sub judice, or a penalty appeal is pending** are not denied consideration; the DPC/Establishment Board assesses them and keeps the recommendation in a **sealed cover**; the committee must record the current status and check whether earlier sealed-cover cases have concluded ("मागील निवडसूचीत मोहोरबंद पाकिटात ठेवलेल्या प्रकरणी विभागीय चौकशी/न्यायप्रविष्ट प्रकरण/शिक्षेचा अंमल पूर्ण झाला आहे किंवा कसे हे तपासून").
- Detailed sealed-cover rules are in **GAD GR एसआरव्ही-2015/प्र.क्र.310/काया-12, dt. 15.12.2017, read with GR dt. 30.08.2018** (listed as reference no. 34 in the 2019 GR) — **text not fetched; obtain from gr.maharashtra.gov.in**.
- Other promotion-related checks in the 2019 GR: five years' CRs, qualifications, departmental and language exams, probation, caste validity, **property returns**, mandatory training; unauthorised absence of 2+ years before eligibility date = ineligible; cases kept "open" where delay is administrative (probation/confirmation orders pending).
- Minimum service for promotion GR (gr.maharashtra.gov.in, title only, not fetched): https://gr.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/Government%20Resolutions/Marathi/201510061439369607.pdf (dt. 06.10.2015).

## How these are used in litigation
- **Petitioner**: DE delayed beyond GAD limits → quash; promotion wrongly withheld though only preliminary inquiry/no charge-sheet at the DPC date (sealed cover applies only after charge-sheet is issued — *Union of India v. K.V. Jankiraman* principle); DE after retirement beyond four years / without Governor's sanction; pension/gratuity withheld without Rule 27 order; suspension not regularised.
- **State**: time-limits are administrative/directory; delay attributable to employee's adjournments / court stay; sealed cover opened and promotion given retrospectively on exoneration per GR; charge-sheet issued before retirement so Rule 27(2)(a) continuation valid; four-year bar computed from charge-sheet date; gratuity withheld under Pension Rules pending DE (Rule 130-series — verify) and interest not payable; nodal/vigilance machinery under 2025 GRs now in place.
