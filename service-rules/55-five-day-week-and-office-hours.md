# Five-day week and office hours for State Government offices — GAD GR 24.02.2020 and later GRs; applicability to courts
# शासकीय कार्यालयांसाठी पाच दिवसांचा आठवडा व कार्यालयीन वेळ — सामान्य प्रशासन विभाग

**Issuing department:** General Administration Department (कार्यालयीन सुधारणा / रेखा-1)
**Status:** In force since 29.02.2020.

## 1. GAD GR dated 24.02.2020 — five-day week w.e.f. 29.02.2020
- **GR number: not verified** (the GR itself could not be fetched from an official domain in this session; search budget exhausted).
- Contents per contemporaneous press report (The Hitavada, 25.02.2020: https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2020/2/25/State-Govt-s-5-day-week-not-applicable-to-hospitals-cops-essential-services.html) — summary (verify against original):
  - Saturdays and Sundays holidays; office hours 9.45 am to 6.15 pm (officers/staff) and 9.30 am to 6.30 pm (peons/Group D); lunch half hour between 1–2 pm; 45 minutes extra per day to compensate.
  - Excluded: Government hospitals/dispensaries, Police, Prisons, water-supply schemes, Fire & Emergency Services, sanitation staff, Government colleges/medical colleges/schools/polytechnics, offices under the Factories Act / Industrial Disputes Act / essential services, dairy schemes, Government printing presses, Vaccine Institute Nagpur, specified WRD workshops and Forest units.
  - **Courts are not mentioned** as excluded or included: the GR governs offices under the State executive; the working days/hours of the High Court and subordinate courts are fixed by the High Court (Bombay High Court Appellate Side / Civil Manual, court calendar). Subordinate courts in Maharashtra continue to work on Saturdays other than the 2nd and 4th Saturdays per the High Court calendar (verify from the current year's calendar on bombayhighcourt.nic.in).

## 2. GAD GR जीएडी-32036/14/2023-जीएडी(रेखा-1) (code 715186), dated 10.03.2026 — flexi-time for women employees in MMR
- Fetched: https://www.mahapolice.gov.in/uploads/27bc41b71d00de4545a2a7f7aba379dd.pdf
- **Verbatim:** > "सोमवार ते शुक्रवार सकाळी 9:45 ते सायंकाळी 6:15 अशी आहे." (confirms the standard five-day office hours)
- "Come early – Go early": women officers/employees in Mumbai Metropolitan Region arriving between 9.15 and 9.45 may leave earlier by the same minutes (max 30); must stay late when work (legislature/court matters) requires; cites 60-minute flexi scheme for Mantralaya staff under GR 31.12.2014.

## How it is used in litigation
- Court staff (non-judicial) sometimes claim parity with the five-day week / compensatory off or overtime for Saturdays; State/High Court stand: court working days are regulated by the High Court under its administrative control (Art. 235) and the court calendar; the GAD GR expressly applies to executive offices and exempts essential services; no legal right to a five-day week.

## मराठी सारांश
- सामान्य प्रशासन विभागाचा दि. 24.02.2020 चा शासन निर्णय: दि. 29.02.2020 पासून शासकीय कार्यालयांना पाच दिवसांचा आठवडा; वेळ सकाळी 9.45 ते सायंकाळी 6.15 (चतुर्थश्रेणी 9.30 ते 6.30); रुग्णालये, पोलीस, कारागृह, अत्यावश्यक सेवा वगळल्या.
- न्यायालयांची कामकाजाची दिवस/वेळ उच्च न्यायालय ठरवते; हा शासन निर्णय न्यायालयांना आपोआप लागू होत नाही.
- दि. 10.03.2026 च्या शा.नि. ने मुंबई महानगर क्षेत्रातील महिला कर्मचाऱ्यांना 30 मिनिटांची लवचिकता.
