## 2. Case details

### 2.1 Article 229 cluster — Gurumoorthy (1971), Dixitulu (1979), SC Employees' Welfare Assn (1989), C.L. Agrawal (1997), Paliwal (1998) ✖
- Combined ratio: (i) appointments and conditions of service of High Court officers/servants are exclusively for the Chief Justice (Art. 229(1)–(2)); (ii) rules touching **salaries, allowances, leave or pensions** need the **Governor's approval** (i.e. State's financial concurrence through the Law & Judiciary / Finance Departments) and are ineffective without it (C.L. Agrawal); (iii) the Governor/State cannot itself frame or alter those rules — approval is of the CJ's rules (Paliwal); (iv) approval must be considered reasonably but the Court will not ordinarily compel a specific approval (SC Employees' Welfare Assn); (v) tribunals have no jurisdiction over HC staff service matters (Dixitulu line; MAT/CAT excluded).
- **How the State uses it**: when High Court staff petition for a scale/allowance that the CJ recommended but the State has not approved — the State's defence is that approval is a constitutional discretion exercised on financial grounds, and the Court cannot fix pay by writ (*Court Stenographers*, 2002); when the CJ's rules were approved, the State is bound to fund them (Art. 229(3) — charged expenditure).
- **How petitioners use it**: once the CJ has framed/recommended, the State's refusal is justiciable if arbitrary; parity with Secretariat/other HC staff; the State cannot sit on approval indefinitely.

### 2.2 State of Maharashtra v. Assn. of Court Stenographers (SC, 09.01.2002) ◐
- Bombay HC had directed a particular pay scale for HC stenographers/PAs/PSs applying equal pay. SC set aside: pay scale for HC employees can only be fixed by rules framed by the CJ under Art. 229(2) with the Governor's approval; the High Court on the judicial side should not fix scales. Quoted lines (LegitQuest): "It is no doubt true that the doctrine of equal pay for equal work is an equitable principle" … "it would be a matter for the learned Chief Justice of the High Court to frame the appropriate rules". The Court also noted that the Government cannot fix HC employees' salary apart from Art. 229 rules.
- **State uses**: in every HC-staff pay parity writ — the forum is the CJ's rule-making + Governor's approval, not Art. 226.

### 2.3 All India Judges' Association line — Shetty Commission to court staff ✖/◐
- AIJA (2002) 4 SCC 247 accepted the Shetty Commission for judicial officers; the Commission's Part on **ministerial staff** (pay scales for Clerks, Stenographers, Bailiffs, Process Servers, Class IV) was left to States/High Courts. The SC order of **07.10.2009** in W.P.(C) 1022/1989 directed implementation for court staff (recorded in *Dhawale*). Maharashtra: L&J GR **20.10.2011** accepted implementation w.e.f. **01.04.2003**; GRs 2017 and **15.09.2018** corrected stenographer grades.
- SNJPC (2020 report; SC judgment 19.05.2023) concerns judicial officers only — the State's affidavit should not concede that SNJPC/2023 judgment mandates staff revisions (file 72).
- **Court Managers (16.05.2025) ✔**: https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/1989/60181/60181_1989_1_1501_61807_Judgement_16-May-2025.pdf — HCs to frame rules on the Assam 2018 model, "with State approval to follow"; regularisation of existing Court Managers subject to suitability test within set timelines (¶44); ¶44(iv): District Court Managers work under "Registrars/Superintendents (Head of the Ministerial Staff) of the concerned courts"; ¶44(v): duties not to overlap with Registrars. Maharashtra had contractual Court Managers (LiveLaw 2020 report of their Bombay HC petition for regularisation/pay ◐) — the State must now show compliance (rules, posts, financial sanction).

### 2.4 Rajendra Ramaji Dhawale (Bom HC, 2026) ◐ and Pralhad Ghule (Bom HC, 2014) ◐
- Dhawale: stenographers of District & Sessions Courts; the State sought to apply the corrective GR 15.09.2018 prospectively; DB held it corrective, effective 01.04.2003; arrears with 6% interest from 07.10.2009; noted repeated State adjournments. **Lesson for drafting**: once a GR accepts an implementation date, later "clarificatory/corrective" GRs cannot shift it; the affidavit must either justify a fresh policy decision with a rational basis or accept the original date and contest only quantum/interest.
- Ghule: Family Court staff at Aurangabad/Nagpur (district-court employees on deputation) cannot be excluded from GR 20.10.2011; exclusion lacked intelligible differentia; relief from date of petition.

## 3. Drafting checklist — court-staff petitions where the State is respondent
1. Identify the establishment: High Court (Art. 229 — CJ's rules; Governor's approval for pay rules); City Civil Court / Small Causes Court / District Courts (Art. 235 — District Judge/Principal Judge appoints under the High Court-approved recruitment rules; pay scales as per State GRs extended by L&J Dept.).
2. State's role: financial approval/concurrence; GRs extending pay commissions and Shetty scales; the State is not the appointing/disciplinary authority — say so, and let the High Court administration file facts.
3. Shetty claims: GR 20.10.2011 (01.04.2003), GR 15.09.2018 (grades), Dhawale (corrective = retrospective), Ghule (no exclusion of similarly placed staff); compute arrears + Tarsem Singh / interest exposure.
4. Pay-fixation by writ: *Court Stenographers* (2002) — not permissible; direct the petitioner to the Art. 229 process.
5. Court Managers: SC 16.05.2025 directions and timelines — status of Maharashtra rules/regularisation.
6. Keep a register of pending SLPs/LPAs in staff matters (Siddaraj-type cut-offs; Ghogre; Goa staff rules W.P. 464/2023).

## 4. मराठी सारांश
- अनु. 229: उच्च न्यायालयाच्या कर्मचाऱ्यांच्या नेमणुका व सेवाशर्ती मुख्य न्यायमूर्तींच्या नियमांनुसार; वेतन/भत्ते/रजा/निवृत्तिवेतनाशी संबंधित नियमांना **राज्यपालांची मान्यता** (= शासनाची वित्तीय सहमती) आवश्यक, तिच्याशिवाय नियम प्रभावी नाहीत (*सी.एल. अग्रवाल*); शासन स्वतः नियम करू शकत नाही (*पालीवाल*).
- *कोर्ट स्टेनोग्राफर्स असोसिएशन* (स.न्या. 2002): उच्च न्यायालय रिट अधिकारात वेतनश्रेणी ठरवू शकत नाही — ती मुख्य न्यायमूर्ती + राज्यपाल मान्यतेची बाब.
- शेट्टी आयोग: न्यायिक अधिकाऱ्यांसाठी स.न्या. (2002); कर्मचाऱ्यांसाठी स.न्या. आदेश 07.10.2009 → महाराष्ट्र शा.नि. 20.10.2011 (01.04.2003 पासून); दुरुस्ती शा.नि. 15.09.2018 ही "सुधारणात्मक" असल्याने तीही 01.04.2003 पासून (*धवले* 2026, 6% व्याज); कुटुंब न्यायालय कर्मचारी वगळता येत नाहीत (*घुले* 2014).
- SNJPC/19.05.2023 निकाल फक्त न्यायिक अधिकाऱ्यांसाठी; कोर्ट मॅनेजर्ससाठी स.न्या. 16.05.2025 — आसाम नियम 2018 नमुना, नियमितीकरण व कालमर्यादा (¶44).
- विभागीय फाईलमधील मुंबई उच्च न्यायालय प्रकरणे (*बांदेकर* 2780/2023, 8224/2022, *बिरादार* 14632/2021, *अहिरे* 15662/2025) ऑनलाइन सापडली नाहीत — प्रमाणित प्रती घेऊन येथे नोंद करावी.
