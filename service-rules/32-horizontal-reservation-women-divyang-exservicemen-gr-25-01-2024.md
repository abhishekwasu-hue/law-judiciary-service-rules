### Key provisions (summary of fetched text; quotes marked)
**Vertical (social) reservation — total 62%, open 38%:** SC 13, ST 7, VJ-A 3, NT-B 2.5, NT-C 3.5, NT-D 2, SBC 2, OBC 19, **EWS 10**.

**Horizontal reservation — total 72%:**

| Category | % | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Women (महिला) | 30 | all posts |
| Ex-servicemen (माजी सैनिक) | 15 | Group C and D only |
| Persons with disabilities (दिव्यांग) | 4 | all posts |
| Sportspersons (खेळाडू) | 5 | all posts |
| Project-affected (प्रकल्पग्रस्त) | 5 | Group C and D only |
| Earthquake-affected (भूकंपग्रस्त) | 2 | Group C and D only |
| Part-time graduates (अंशकालीन पदवीधर) | 10 | Group C and D only |
| Orphans (अनाथ) | 1 | all posts |

**Method:** vertical reservation is computed first; horizontal percentages are then applied to the vacancies and shown in the requisition and advertisement. For most horizontal categories the **compartmentalised (कप्पीकृत)** method applies — reserved posts sit within each social category and the open category. **Divyang 4% and orphans 1% are computed on total vacancies (overall method), shown as separate posts, and the selected candidates are adjusted into their social/open category.** Quote: "दिव्यांग व अनाथ आरक्षण वगळता अन्य समांतर आरक्षण घटकांसाठी कप्पीकृत समांतर आरक्षण धोरण अवलंबण्यात यावे."
**Non-additive rule:** "समांतर आरक्षण हे सामाजिक आरक्षणांतर्गत असून, सामाजिक आरक्षणावर वाढीव म्हणून गणण्यात येऊ नये." Horizontal reservation cannot be shifted from one social category to another ("स्थलांतरीत करता येणार नाही"); if no suitable candidate, the post goes by merit within that social category.
**Three-stage selection** (for compartmentalised categories): (A) open merit list including meritorious reserved candidates; if horizontal quota short, last candidates replaced by horizontal candidates on merit; (B) category-wise merit lists excluding those already selected; (C) horizontal candidates added within each social category.
**Scope:** Government offices, boards, corporations, local bodies, ZPs, aided institutions, universities, cooperative bodies, undertakings. Compliance checked by the Backward Class Cell (मागासवर्ग कक्ष) and the appointing authority. MPSC recruitment applies women, orphan, Divyang, ex-servicemen and sportspersons quotas; recruitment outside MPSC also applies project-affected, earthquake-affected and part-time categories.
The GR relies on W.P. 4067/1998 (Aurangabad Bench) and Anil Kumar Gupta v. State of U.P. (SC).

### Divyang 4% — statutory basis
- Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 (Central Act 49 of 2016), s. 34: not less than 4% in Government establishments (1% each for (a) blindness/low vision, (b) deaf/hard of hearing, (c) locomotor disability incl. cerebral palsy, leprosy cured, dwarfism, acid attack, muscular dystrophy, (d) autism, intellectual disability, specific learning disability, mental illness, and 1% for multiple disabilities from (a)–(d)); s. 33 identification of posts; s. 34(2) carry-forward; reservation in promotion per Central guidance — **Act text not fetched in this research; verify at indiacode.** The Maharashtra GAD GR implementing 4% (commonly cited as GAD GR dated 29.05.2019) was **not located** on an official site — write "GAD GR dated 29.05.2019 (number not verified)".
- Medical board verification before appointment: GAD GR dated 06.05.2004 (cited in GAD circular 11.09.2025 on compassionate appointment).
- Sports quota: verification by Commissioner, Sports & Youth Services, Pune; School Education & Sports Dept GR 30.06.2022 (cited in GAD circular 11.09.2025).

### EWS 10%
- GAD GR dated **12.02.2019** (number not verified; official PDF not fetched) introduced 10% for आर्थिकदृष्ट्या दुर्बल घटक in direct recruitment and education; age/fee relaxation as for SEBC; income limit ₹8 lakh. Confirmed as part of the 62% vertical structure in GR 25.01.2024 above.

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims:** woman/Divyang/ex-serviceman candidate not selected though quota unfilled; horizontal quota treated as additional (over-and-above) or shifted to another social category; Divyang posts not identified; backlog of Divyang vacancies not filled; sportsperson certificate not verified in time.
- **State's / High Court's stand:** horizontal reservation is compartmentalised within each vertical category and non-additive (GR 25.01.2024, para quoted above); Divyang/orphan computed on total vacancies by overall method; a candidate selected on merit is counted against the horizontal quota (Anil Kumar Gupta / Indra Sawhney principle); verification of certificates (sports, disability medical board) is a condition precedent; for court recruitment the High Court follows the State's reservation policy and the roster is validated by the Backward Class Cell.
