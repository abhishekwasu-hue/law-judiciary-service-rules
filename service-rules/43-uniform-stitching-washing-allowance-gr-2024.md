# Uniform stitching & washing allowance for Class IV (Group D) employees — GAD GR 10.10.2024
# चतुर्थश्रेणी कर्मचाऱ्यांच्या गणवेशाचा शिलाई भत्ता व धुलाई भत्ता — सुधारित दर

**GR No.:** गणवेश-2024/प्र.क्र.67/जपुक (29)
**Date:** 10.10.2024
**Issuing department:** General Administration Department (सामान्य प्रशासन विभाग), with Finance Department concurrence (unofficial ref 5557/व्यय-4 dt 30.09.2024)
**Official source fetched:** https://mahapolice.gov.in/uploads/e19db3269d0a4e3298a17e6c6827ae06.pdf (Government of Maharashtra GR copy on Maharashtra Police site)
**Status:** In force from date of issue.

## Key provisions
| Item | Old rate | Revised rate | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shirt + trouser stitching | Rs 250 | **Rs 750 per pair** — verbatim: "रु. 750/- प्रत्येक जोडीसाठी" | once in 2 years |
| Blouse stitching (women) | Rs 50 | **Rs 250 per blouse** — verbatim: "रु. 250/- (प्रत्येक ब्लाऊज साठी)" | once in 2 years |
| Woollen uniform stitching | Rs 350 | **Rs 1,500** — verbatim: "रु. 1500/-" | once in 4 years |
| Washing allowance (धुलाई भत्ता) | Rs 50 p.m. | **Rs 250 p.m.** — verbatim: "रु.250/-" | monthly |

Conditions (summary — verify against original):
- Controlling officers must ensure eligible employees actually wear uniform during office hours.
- Claims to be submitted within the prescribed period and paid as per GR dated 26.03.2008 (the earlier uniform GR).
- **Posts filled through outsourcing are NOT eligible.**
- Departments to verify the number of eligible employees; expenditure from the sanctioned grant of the department.

## Related GRs
- GAD GR dt 26.03.2008 (earlier uniform/stitching rules) — not fetched, number not verified.
- Court-specific: uniform allowance for bailiffs / peons / hamals of subordinate courts is drawn under these GAD rates applied to the L&J establishment; no separate L&J GR located (not verified).

## How it is used in litigation
- Claims by peons/bailiffs/hamals/drivers for washing allowance at revised rates with retrospective effect, or for uniform supply in kind plus allowance; contractual/outsourced staff claiming parity.
- State's stand: allowance is admissible only from the date of the GR, only to regular Group D employees on sanctioned posts who are required to wear uniform; outsourced staff are expressly excluded by the GR; quantum is a policy matter.

## मराठी सारांश
- सामान्य प्रशासन विभागाचा दि. 10.10.2024 चा शासन निर्णय (गणवेश-2024/प्र.क्र.67/जपुक-29).
- शिलाई भत्ता: शर्ट-पँट जोडी रु. 750 (दर 2 वर्षांनी), ब्लाऊज रु. 250, लोकरी गणवेश रु. 1500 (दर 4 वर्षांनी); धुलाई भत्ता दरमहा रु. 250.
- बाह्ययंत्रणेद्वारे भरलेल्या पदांना लागू नाही; शासन निर्णयाच्या दिनांकापासून लागू.
