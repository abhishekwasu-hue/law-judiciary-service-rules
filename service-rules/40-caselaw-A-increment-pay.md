# 40-A  Case-law: Increment, pay fixation, recovery of excess pay, arrears

**Scope**: Supreme Court / Bombay HC decisions the State (Law & Judiciary Dept.) meets in writ petitions of court staff on (i) notional increment for employees retiring on 30 June / the day before increment date, (ii) recovery of excess payment, (iii) limiting arrears to three years, (iv) wrong pay fixation / stepping-up.

**Verification key**: ✔ = official text opened (sci.gov.in / HC site) in this research; ◐ = only secondary source opened (news/digest) — *citation from secondary source — verify*; ✖ = cited from standard reporters / department file, not opened — *verify before pleading*.

## 2. Case details

### 2.1 KPTCL v. C.P. Mundinamani (SC, 11.04.2023) ✔
- **Source opened**: https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2020/8117/8117_2020_4_1503_43425_Judgement_11-Apr-2023.pdf
- **Facts**: Karnataka Power Transmission Corporation employees retired on 30 June/31 May; increment fell due the next day. Karnataka HC (DB) directed one notional increment; SC dismissed KPTCL's appeal.
- **Ratio**: Increment is earned by rendering one year of service with good conduct; it is a reward for the service already rendered, not a benefit for future service. Denying it because the employee retired the next day is arbitrary. The Court expressly agreed with *P. Ayyamperumal* (Madras HC).
- **Verbatim (¶6.5)**: "A government servant is granted the annual increment on the basis of his good conduct while rendering one year service." … "Therefore, the increment is earned for rendering service with good conduct in a year/specified period." … "Merely because, the government servant has retired on the very next day, how can he be denied the annual increment".
- **Verbatim (¶6.7)**: "We are in complete agreement with the view taken by the Madras High Court in the case of P. Ayyamperumal (supra)" … "Any interpretation which would lead to arbitrariness and/or unreasonableness should be avoided."
- **Verbatim (¶7)**: "the Division Bench of the High Court has rightly directed the appellants to grant one annual increment which the original writ petitioners earned on the last day of their service for rendering their services preceding one year from the date of retirement with good behaviour and efficiently." … "the present appeal deserves to be dismissed and is accordingly dismissed."
- **Petitioners use it**: every 30-June retiree (and 31-Dec retiree under the 2019 Rules' two-date system) claims one notional increment for pension; claims arrears with interest; argues Rule 10 of MCS (Revised Pay) Rules 2009 ("six months and above") in fact supports them.
- **State uses / answers it**: (a) relief is for *pensionary* purposes only — notional increment, not pay for a day; (b) *M. Siddaraj* clarification: for non-litigants the benefit runs from 01.05.2023 only; no arrears for earlier period; (c) each claim must be tested on the State's own rules (MCS Pay Rules 1981 Rr. 36/39; FD circular 29.04.2009; GR 26.12.2011 on EOL); (d) delay/laches and Tarsem Singh three-year cap for anything beyond the Siddaraj window; (e) check whether FD has issued a Maharashtra-level implementation/stay instruction (the library file 02 refers to an FD direction of Oct-2026 — verify before relying).

### 2.2 Union of India v. M. Siddaraj — clarification (SC, 06.09.2024) ✔; confirmed 20.02.2025 ◐
- **Source opened**: https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2024/2400/2400_2024_2_23_55453_Order_06-Sep-2024.pdf (M.A. Diary 2400/2024 in C.A. 3933/2023, bench Sanjiv Khanna & Sanjay Kumar JJ).
- **Verbatim directions (06.09.2024)**:
  - (a) "The judgment dated 11.04.2023 will be given effect to in case of third parties from the date of the judgment, that is, the pension by taking into account one increment will be payable on and after 01.05.2023." … "Enhanced pension for the period prior to 31.04.2023 will not be paid." (sic — the order reads "31.04.2023"; evidently 30.04.2023.)
  - (b) "For persons who have filed writ petitions and succeeded, the directions given in the said judgment will operate as res judicata, and accordingly, an enhanced pension by taking one increment would have to be paid."
  - (c) "The direction in (b) will not apply, where the judgment has not attained finality, and cases where an appeal has been preferred, or if filed, is entertained by the appellate court."
  - (d) Retired employees who filed intervention/impleadment in C.A. 3933/2023 or any writ and got a beneficial order — enhanced pension "payable from the month in which the application for intervention/impleadment was filed."
  - Protection: "no person who has already received an enhanced pension including arrears, will be affected by the directions in (a), (c) and (d)."
- **20.02.2025 order (LiveLaw report — verify from sci.gov.in)**: directions (a)–(c) confirmed; (d) modified — persons who had filed OA/WP/intervention get enhanced pension for **three years before the month of filing**; those who filed after 19.05.2023 (date of the *M. Siddaraj* main judgment) are governed by (a); excess already paid not recoverable; grievances first to the authorities, then CAT/HC.
- **Review status**: Union's review against Mundinamani — treated as not re-opened; the Court instead issued the above clarification (confirm on sci.gov.in case status of R.P. in C.A. 2471/2023 — not located in this research).
- **State uses**: this is the State's principal answer to arrears claims — enhanced pension only from 01.05.2023 for non-litigants; three-year window only for those who had litigated; no interest for the period before.
- **Petitioners use**: direction (b)/(d) where they had earlier litigated; argue Maharashtra employees are "third parties" entitled from 01.05.2023 at least.

### 2.3 P. Ayyamperumal (Madras HC, 15.09.2017) ◐
- W.P. 15732/2017, Madras HC (DB); held employee retiring 30.06.2013 entitled to notional increment of 01.07.2013 for pension. SLP(C) 22008/2018 dismissed by SC on 23.07.2018; review dismissed (2019). DoPT had taken the stand (OM dated 03.02.2021 referred in library file 02) that *Ayyamperumal* was in personam — that stand is no longer available after Mundinamani ¶6.7 (quoted above).
- **How used**: petitioners cite it with Mundinamani; State's answer is now limited to the Siddaraj date-cut.

### 2.4 State of Punjab v. Rafiq Masih (White Washer) (SC, 18.12.2014) ◐
- (2015) 4 SCC 334. Official PDF at digiscr.sci.gov.in could not be opened (robots); the five categories were read verbatim as reproduced in *Jagdev Singh* ✔ (para 10 there; para 18 of Rafiq Masih in SCC):
  - "(i) Recovery from employees belonging to Class-III and Class-IV service (or Group 'C' and Group 'D' service).
  - (ii) Recovery from retired employees, or employees who are due to retire within one year, of the order of recovery.
  - (iii) Recovery from employees, when the excess payment has been made for a period in excess of five years, before the order of recovery is issued.
  - (iv) Recovery in cases where an employee has wrongfully been required to discharge duties of a higher post, and has been paid accordingly, even though he should have rightfully been required to work against an inferior post.
  - (v) In any other case, where the Court arrives at the conclusion, that recovery if made from the employee, would be iniquitous or harsh or arbitrary to such an extent, as would far outweigh the equitable balance of the employer's right to recover."
- **Petitioners use**: Group C/D court staff (clerks, bailiffs, peons) rely on category (i) and (ii) to stop recovery of excess pay after re-fixation; retired staff rely on (ii).
- **State uses**: Rafiq Masih applies only where the excess was paid *without the employee's fault/misrepresentation*; it does not bar **prospective re-fixation** of pay to the correct scale (only recovery of the past excess); where an **undertaking** was given at fixation (common in Maharashtra pay-fixation orders under the 2009/2019 Rules), *Jagdev Singh* permits recovery; Group A/B employees and payments under five years remain recoverable (*Chandi Prasad Uniyal*).

### 2.5 High Court of P&H v. Jagdev Singh (SC, 29.07.2016) ✔
- Source: https://api.sci.gov.in/jonew/judis/43829.pdf — C.A. 3500/2006.
- Judicial officer's pay wrongly fixed; he had given an undertaking to refund. Held: Rafiq Masih category (ii) (retiree) does not apply where the employee specifically undertook to refund; recovery upheld.
- **State uses**: whenever the fixation order carried an undertaking (MCS Revised Pay Rules 2009 Rule 7/ 2019 Rules option forms usually contain one).

### 2.6 Union of India v. Tarsem Singh (SC, 13.08.2008) ✔
- Source: https://hcraj.nic.in/sci/2008/3-5151-2008_2008-08-13.pdf (Rajasthan HC mirror of SC judgment), [2008] 12 SCR 104; (2008) 8 SCC 648.
- **Ratio (¶5–6)**: In service matters, delay bars stale claims; but where the claim relates to pension or similar *continuing wrong*, the petition cannot be dismissed on delay — relief is moulded by restricting arrears to **three years prior to the petition**. Exception (¶5): if the claim "involved issues relating to seniority or promotion etc., affecting others, delay would render the claim stale" and reopening would unsettle third-party rights.
- **State uses**: in every pay/pension/increment writ filed years after the cause — ask that arrears be confined to three years before filing (and, for Mundinamani claims, to the Siddaraj window). Also to resist re-opening of old seniority/promotion decisions.
- **Petitioners use**: to defeat the State's delay objection in pension matters ("continuing wrong"). Note *Maya Banerjee* (2026) ◐ — the cap was not applied to a widow's family pension where delay was not her fault.

### 2.7 Pay-fixation / stepping-up cases ✖ (standard reporters; verify)
- **State of M.P. v. Yogendra Shrivastava (2010) 12 SCC 538**: pay scale granted on a mistaken reading does not create a vested right; the State may withdraw/correct it; employees cannot insist on continuation of a mistake. (Open on sci.gov.in judis before quoting.)
- **Union of India v. R. Swaminathan (1997) 7 SCC 690**: conditions for stepping-up — same cadre, same scale, direct result of application of the fixation rule, junior not drawing more because of advance increments/personal pay/ad-hoc promotion.
- **Gurcharan Singh Grewal (2009) 3 SCC 94**: no stepping-up on the basis of a junior's *erroneously* fixed pay.
- **How used**: petitioners seek stepping-up with juniors (frequent in Stenographer/Clerk cadres after Shetty Commission re-fixation); State replies that junior's higher pay flows from a different rule/mistake, Art. 14 cannot perpetuate an illegality (see file I — *Jai Singh*), and corrected fixation is prospective with recovery limited per Rafiq Masih.

### 2.8 Bombay HC — Jyotiram Tupe (06.08.2026) and Balaji Manikrao Biradar (W.P. 14632/2021, Aurangabad, 04.05.2022) ✖
- Per department file / library file 02: Bombay HC granted notional increment to 30-June retirees, reading Rule 10 of MCS (Revised Pay) Rules 2009 ("six months and above") with Mundinamani. Full text not opened in this research. For the State's affidavit: check whether an LPA/SLP was filed, and whether FD has a stay or review direction; plead the Siddaraj cut-off in any event.

## 3. Drafting checklist for the State's reply (increment/pay matters)
1. Date of retirement, date of increment under the applicable Rules (1981 Rr. 36/39; 2009 R.10; 2019 Rr.) and whether petitioner completed the qualifying period.
2. Whether the petitioner litigated before 19.05.2023 / 11.04.2023 (Siddaraj categories (b)/(d)) or is a "third party" (category (a) — from 01.05.2023 only).
3. Date of petition → Tarsem Singh three-year cap for other arrears.
4. Recovery: identify Rafiq Masih category; check undertaking (Jagdev Singh); distinguish re-fixation (permissible) from recovery (restricted).
5. Stepping-up: verify Swaminathan conditions; junior's pay source.

## 4. मराठी सारांश
- *मुंडिनामणी* (स.न्या. 11.04.2023): सेवानिवृत्तीच्या दुसऱ्या दिवशी देय होणारी वेतनवाढ पूर्ण वर्षाच्या सेवेने "कमावलेली" असते; ती निवृत्तिवेतनासाठी काल्पनिक (notional) स्वरूपात द्यावी.
- *एम. सिद्दराज* स्पष्टीकरण (06.09.2024 / 20.02.2025): ज्यांनी स्वतः याचिका दाखल केली नव्हती त्यांना लाभ फक्त 01.05.2023 पासून; आधीची थकबाकी नाही; आधी जिंकलेल्या याचिकाकर्त्यांना रेस-ज्युडिकाटा संरक्षण; आधी दिलेली रक्कम वसूल करू नये.
- *रफीक मसीह*: वर्ग-3/4 कर्मचारी, निवृत्त/एक वर्षात निवृत्त होणारे, 5 वर्षांपेक्षा जुनी जादा अदायगी — चूक कर्मचाऱ्याची नसल्यास वसुली नाही; पण हमीपत्र दिले असल्यास (*जगदेव सिंग*) वसुली शक्य; वेतन पुनर्निश्चिती भविष्यलक्षी करता येते.
- *तरसेम सिंग*: निवृत्तिवेतन/वेतनाचा दावा "सततचा अन्याय" — विलंबाने फेटाळू नये, पण थकबाकी याचिकेच्या आधीच्या 3 वर्षांपुरतीच; ज्येष्ठता/पदोन्नतीचे जुने दावे मात्र शिळे ठरतात.
- शासनाच्या उत्तरात: नियमानुसार वेतनवाढीची तारीख, सिद्दराज कट-ऑफ, 3 वर्षांची मर्यादा, हमीपत्र आणि स्टेपिंग-अपच्या अटी (स्वामिनाथन) हे मुद्दे प्रत्येक प्रकरणात तपासावेत.
