# Shetty Commission (First National Judicial Pay Commission) and Second National Judicial Pay Commission (SNJPC) — bearing on subordinate court staff; Supreme Court directions in All India Judges Association
## शेट्टी आयोग (प्रथम राष्ट्रीय न्यायिक वेतन आयोग) व द्वितीय राष्ट्रीय न्यायिक वेतन आयोग — दुय्यम न्यायालयीन कर्मचाऱ्यांवरील परिणाम; सर्वोच्च न्यायालयाचे निर्देश (अखिल भारतीय न्यायाधीश संघटना)

### Key points verified from the Supreme Court material
- SNJPC's remit = judicial officers; the Supreme Court's 2020 and 2023 orders contain no direction on non-judicial staff. A State affidavit should therefore **not** assert that SNJPC/the 2023 judgment mandates revision of staff pay; staff claims rest on the Shetty Commission recommendations as accepted/implemented by the State and the High Court, and on general 6th/7th CPC GRs.
- Retirement age of judicial officers remains 60 (19.05.2023 judgment) — distinct from staff retirement age (file 80).
- The Supreme Court treated Maharashtra's "New Pension Scheme" as inapplicable to judicial officers (Bombay HC judgment reported on livelaw; official copy not fetched) — not applicable to staff.

### How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner's claims (court staff):** Shetty Commission pay scales / higher scales recommended for stenographers, bailiffs, clerks; parity with High Court staff; arrears with interest; application of SNJPC 2.81 multiplier to staff by analogy.
- **State's stand:** Shetty Commission/SNJPC recommendations bind only to the extent accepted by the Supreme Court and concern judicial officers; staff scales are fixed by the State on Pay Commission recommendations (6th CPC — FD Notification 2009; 7th CPC — FD Notification वेपुर-2019/प्र.क्र.1/सेवा-9 dated 30.01.2019) and extended to court staff; any special recommendation for staff requires equation of posts and Governor's approval (for HC staff, Art. 229(2) proviso); financial implications are a legitimate consideration; arrears limited by delay/laches.
