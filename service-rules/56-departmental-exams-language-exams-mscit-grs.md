## 2. Departmental examinations (विभागीय परीक्षा) — NOT verified

## 3. Marathi and Hindi language examinations (मराठी / हिंदी भाषा परीक्षा) — partly verified
- Governing instruments: **Maharashtra Marathi Language Examination Rules** and **Hindi Language Examination Rules** (GAD; administered by Language Directorate / भाषा संचालनालय — https://marathibhasha.maharashtra.gov.in (Directorate page exists)). Employees must pass the lower/higher standard Marathi and Hindi examinations within the prescribed period after appointment; increments may be withheld until passed.
- **Exemptions** (summary — **GR numbers not verified**): exemption from the Marathi examination for employees who have passed SSC/HSC/degree with Marathi as a subject or medium; exemption from the Hindi examination for those who passed SSC with Hindi (or equivalent) as a subject; exemption on attaining the age of **50 years** (or on completing a specified service / nearing retirement); exemption for Group D employees. Verify against the Language Examination Rules and the GAD कार्यासन-17 GRs.
- Promotion GR dt. 01.08.2019 (verified) treats non-passing of language examinations as a disqualification for promotion unless exempted.

## How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner**: increments/promotion withheld for non-passing of departmental / language / MS-CIT exam although petitioner is exempted by age (45/50) or by educational qualification; exam not conducted by the Department so condition impossible of performance; GR applied retrospectively; equivalence of petitioner's computer qualification wrongly rejected.
- **State**: passing the exam is a condition of the recruitment rules / GR; exemption clause not attracted (age/qualification not met on the relevant date); withholding increment for failure to pass is **not a penalty** (Explanation to D&A Rule 5) and no DE is required; equivalence governed strictly by the IT (GAD) GRs of 04.02.2013/2018; promotion denied under GR 01.08.2019 for unfulfilled eligibility; the High Court's recruitment/exam rules (adopting GAD GRs) bind its staff.

## मराठी सारांश
संगणक अर्हता (MS-CIT किंवा समकक्ष) सा.प्र.वि. शासन निर्णय क्र. प्रशिक्षण-२०००/प्र.क्र.६१/२००१/३९, दि. १९.०३.२००३ (नियुक्तीपासून २ वर्षांत प्राप्त करणे) व माहिती तंत्रज्ञान (सा.प्र.वि.) शासन निर्णय क्र. मातंस-२०१२/प्र.क्र.२७७/३९, दि. ०४.०२.२०१३ (समकक्ष अर्हतांची यादी; पुढे दि. ०८.०१.२०१८ व १६.०७.२०१८ ने सुधारित) यांनुसार बंधनकारक आहे — हे क्रमांक अधिकृत कागदपत्रांतून पडताळले आहेत; वय/सेवानिवृत्तीवरील सूटीची अट पडताळता आली नाही. विभागीय परीक्षा व मराठी/हिंदी भाषा परीक्षांतून सूट (वयाची ४५/५० वर्षे, शैक्षणिक अर्हता) देणाऱ्या शासन निर्णयांचे क्रमांक अधिकृत पानांवरून मिळाले नाहीत (“not verified”); ते सा.प्र.वि. कार्यासन १२/१७ च्या पानांवरून घ्यावेत. दि. ०१.०८.२०१९ च्या पदोन्नती शासन निर्णयानुसार या परीक्षा उत्तीर्ण नसल्यास पदोन्नतीस अपात्रता येते.
