# Service break (सेवाखंड), condonation (सेवाखंड क्षमापन) and counting of ad-hoc / temporary / prior service
# सेवाखंड, सेवाखंड क्षमापन व तदर्थ / अस्थायी / पूर्वसेवा गणना

**Status:** Governed by MCS (Pension) Rules 1982 (qualifying-service chapter), MCS (Pay) Rules 1981 (increments) and FD/GAD GRs. Only one instrument could be fetched from an official source in this session (FD GR 30.05.2024).

## 1. FD GR रानिप्र-2024/प्र.क्र.35/सेवा-4, dated 30.05.2024 — joining prior service to the present post for gratuity (NPS employees)
- Fetched: https://mahapolice.gov.in/uploads/e03d8bd64ed679e7957a7d3168b02769.pdf
- Four cumulative conditions (verbatim where fetched): (अ) earlier appointment > "नियमित स्वरुपाची वैध मार्गाने झालेली असावी"; (ब) probation completed; (क) applied through proper channel with prior permission; (ड) **break between the two appointments not more than joining time on transfer** — this is the operative "no service break" condition. FD approval on administrative department's proposal. Full note in file 46.

## 2. MCS (Pension) Rules 1982 — summary (rule numbers to be verified; text not extracted)
- Qualifying service begins from the date of taking charge of a post in a pensionable establishment on substantive/temporary/officiating basis; service as apprentice/daily-wage/casual generally does not count; temporary/officiating service followed by confirmation counts.
- **Interruptions (सेवाखंड):** an interruption in service entails forfeiture of past service except in specified cases (authorised leave, suspension followed by reinstatement, abolition of post, etc.); the competent authority (Government/Head of Department as delegated) may **condone** interruptions on conditions — typically that the interruption was not due to resignation/dismissal/removal, that the period of interruption does not exceed a specified limit, and that the service before and after is of a specified minimum length; the condoned period itself is not counted as qualifying service. (Rules on "Interruption in service" and "Condonation of interruption" — verify rule numbers, approx. Rules 48–49, in the 1982 Rules: https://vjti.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/MCS-Pension-Rules-1982-modifed-up-to-31st-july-1992.pdf — binary not extractable here.)
- Resignation to take up another Government post with proper permission is treated as technical resignation and past service counts (FD GR 30.05.2024 reflects the same logic for NPS gratuity).

## 3. Ad-hoc / contract service and increments
- Ad-hoc service followed by regular selection without break counts for increments/seniority only where the recruitment rules or a specific GR so provide; GAD GRs on regularisation of ad-hoc appointments (e.g. 1990s "तदर्थ सेवा नियमित करणे") — **not fetched; not verified**.

## How it is used in litigation
- Petitioners (court clerks/peons appointed first on ad-hoc/daily-wage basis, or who resigned from one court establishment and joined another): seek counting of earlier service for pension, increments, seniority and time-bound promotion; condonation of a short break; treatment of resignation as technical.
- State's stand: earlier engagement was not against a sanctioned post / not through recruitment rules, hence not qualifying; break exceeded joining time and no prior permission was taken, hence conditions of GR 30.05.2024 / Pension Rules not met; condonation is discretionary and prospective; counting for seniority is governed by the Seniority Rules 2021 (file 37 by another agent) and cannot be claimed through pension rules.

## मराठी सारांश
- सेवाखंड असल्यास पूर्वीची सेवा निवृत्तिवेतनासाठी ग्राह्य धरली जात नाही; सक्षम प्राधिकारी अटींवर सेवाखंड क्षमापित करू शकतो (म.ना.से. निवृत्तिवेतन नियम 1982 — नियम क्रमांक पडताळणे आवश्यक).
- NPS कर्मचाऱ्यांसाठी पूर्वसेवा उपदानासाठी जोडण्याच्या चार अटी — वित्त विभाग शा.नि. दि. 30.05.2024 (नियमित नियुक्ती, परिविक्षा पूर्ण, पूर्वपरवानगी, बदलीच्या पदग्रहण अवधीपेक्षा जास्त सेवाखंड नसणे).
- तदर्थ/रोजंदारी सेवा नियमितीकरणाचे सामान्य प्रशासन विभागाचे शासन निर्णय या सत्रात पडताळता आले नाहीत.
