Key content (summary of the verified text; short quotes):
- Background recited: Madras HC *P. Ayyamperumal v. UoI*, W.P. 15732/2017, order **15.09.2017** (retired 30.06.2013; notional increment "फक्त निवृत्तिवेतनविषयक लाभासाठी अनुज्ञेय"); SC dismissed SLP (Diary 22283/2018) on **23.07.2018**; GoI letters/OMs dated 18.10.2019, 03.02.2021, 24.06.2021, 01.07.2021; SC orders 05.04.2021, 01.08.2022, **11.04.2023** (*KPTCL v. C.P. Mundinamani*), 19.05.2023; Allahabad HC 23.12.2021.
- Operative basis: **Bombay HC, Aurangabad Bench, order dated 16.02.2023 in W.P. No. 1881/2023 and connected petitions**, directing the Chief Secretary / Principal Secretary (Finance) to (a) have all departments **invite applications** from eligible retirees; (b) limit arrears to **three years prior to the application** or from the actual retirement date, whichever is less, and not reject any application meeting the judgment's parameters; (c) alternatively identify eligible cases suo motu and grant benefit within **120 days**; (d) recalculate "pension, gratuity, earned leave, commutation of pension benefits etc." (para 9 of the order, as quoted in the GR).
- **Eligibility**: State Government employees who **retired on 30 June** (and those "सेवानिवृत्त होणाऱ्या" — retiring in future) and who rendered **qualifying service (अर्हताकारी सेवा) for the 12 months ending 30 June**, thereby *earning* the annual increment due on 1 July.
- **Procedure**: administrative departments → heads of departments → eligible retirees apply to the concerned office → office scrutinises from its own records and sanctions **revised pension**.
- **Arrears**: three years preceding the application, or from retirement date if later.
- The GR does **not** mention Rule 10 of the 2009/2019 Rules or the Pension Rules by number, does not add a conduct/penalty condition, and does not state a cut-off (e.g., 01.01.2006).

## 3. How it is used in litigation (Maharashtra, court staff)
- **Petitioner**: retired 30 June (or 31 Dec); completed the increment year; relies on *Mundinamani* (SC 11.04.2023), Aurangabad Bench 16.02.2023 (W.P. 1881/2023), FD GRs 28.06.2023 / 19.12.2025, *Balaji Biradar* and *Jyotiram Tupe* (six-months reading of Rule 10 of 2009 Rules); seeks recalculation of **pension, gratuity, leave encashment, commutation**, arrears with interest, and no three-year cap (Aurangabad order allowed three years from application).
- **State** (affidavit lines actually available): (i) the entitlement flows only from the FD circular of 28.06.2023 / GR 19.12.2025 and is **confined to pension fixation**; gratuity/leave encashment are computed on pay *actually drawn* on the last day of service (Rule 111 "emoluments" = pay received immediately before retirement); (ii) arrears limited to **three years before application** per the Aurangabad order adopted in the circular; (iii) 1 January increment benefit only from **01.01.2016** (2019 Rules), not for 2006–2015 (single 1 July date under 2009 Rules); (iv) qualifying service for the full year with satisfactory work and good conduct is a condition; (v) GoI pattern: SC interim order 06.09.2024 — third parties only from 01.05.2023 and pension only; review petition outcome governs; (vi) **six-months reading** of Rule 10 of 2009 Rules is under review (FD letter 05.10.2026) — plead pendency and seek leave to abide by outcome; (vii) FD letter 12.10.2026 holds implementation in abeyance — plead that the petition be decided after FD's final decision; (viii) no interest where petitioner approached late (*Tupe*). For High Court staff, the GR applies through the High Court's own order; check whether the Registry has issued consequential instructions under Art. 229.

## मराठी सारांश
३० जून रोजी निवृत्त होणाऱ्यांना १ जुलैची काल्पनिक वेतनवाढ धरून निवृत्तिवेतन निश्चित करण्याचा वित्त विभागाचा शासन परिपत्रक क्र. वेतन-२०२३/प्र.क्र.१३/सेवा-३ दि. २८.०६.२०२३ (औरंगाबाद खंडपीठ, रिट याचिका १८८१/२०२३, दि. १६.०२.२०२३ च्या आदेशावर आधारित; अर्जाच्या तीन वर्षे आधीपर्यंतच थकबाकी) आणि ३१ डिसेंबर निवृत्तांना १ जानेवारीची वेतनवाढ देणारा शासन निर्णय दि. १९.१२.२०२५ (सुधारित वेतन नियम २०१९ नियम १०; दि. ०१.०१.२०१६ पासून; केंद्राच्या दि. २०.०५.२०२५ च्या ज्ञापनाच्या धर्तीवर) हे प्रमुख आदेश. केंद्र शासनाचे कार्मिक विभागाचे ज्ञापन दि. १४.१०.२०२४ (सर्वोच्च न्यायालय, मुंदिनामणी प्रकरण व दि. ०६.०९.२०२४ चा अंतरिम आदेश – त्रयस्थांना दि. ०१.०५.२०२३ पासून, फक्त निवृत्तिवेतनासाठी) सत्यापित आहे. उच्च न्यायालयाने २००९ च्या नियम १० मधील "सहा महिने" निकषावर वेतनवाढ मान्य केल्याने वित्त विभागाने ऑक्टोबर २०२६ मध्ये पुनर्विलोकन याचिका दाखल करण्याचे व अंमलबजावणी स्थगित ठेवण्याचे पत्र काढल्याचे वृत्त आहे (पत्र क्रमांक सत्यापित नाहीत).
