## Rule-by-rule summary (verify against original)
- **Rule 1 – Short title & commencement**: "Maharashtra Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2009"; deemed in force from **1 January 2006**.
- **Rule 2 – Application**: all persons under the rule-making control of the Governor **except** (a) part-time employees, (b) persons on consolidated pay, (c) contract appointees unless contract provides otherwise, (d) persons paid from contingencies, (e) work-charged establishment (specified), (f) daily-rated employees, (g) persons retired on or before 31.12.2005 and re-employed, (h) any other class excluded by the Government.
- **Rule 3 – Definitions**: "basic pay" (pay in pay band + grade pay), "existing basic pay", "existing emoluments" (basic pay + dearness pay + DA at 24% as on 01.01.2006), "existing scale", "grade pay", "pay band", "pay in the pay band", "revised emoluments", "revised pay structure", "Schedule".
- **Rule 4 – Scale of pay of posts**: pay band and grade pay of every post as in the Schedule (S-1 to S-33 series in Maharashtra).
- **Rule 5 – Drawal of pay in revised structure**: every employee draws pay in the revised structure from 01.01.2006, with an **option** to continue in the existing scale until the date of next increment or until vacating the post; no option for persons appointed on/after 01.01.2006.
- **Rule 6 – Exercise of option**: in writing in **Annexure IV** within one month of publication; deemed option for revised structure from 01.01.2006 if not exercised; option once exercised is **final**.
- **Rule 7 – Fixation of initial pay in the revised structure**: existing basic pay as on 01.01.2006 × **1.86**, rounded to next multiple of 10 = pay in pay band (not below the minimum of the band); **grade pay** of the existing scale added. Provisos/Notes: bunching relief (one increment for every two stages bunched), **personal pay** where existing emoluments exceed revised emoluments, **stepping up** of senior's pay with reference to junior, fixation during suspension/leave, officiating pay not below substantive pay.
- **Rule 8 – Fixation of pay of direct recruits appointed on or after 01.01.2006**: entry pay in the pay band as per **Annexure III** + grade pay.
- **Rule 9 – Rate of increment**: **3% of (pay in pay band + grade pay)**, rounded off to the next multiple of 10, added to pay in the pay band.
- **Rule 10 – Date of next increment** (the key rule): "There will be a **uniform date of annual increment, viz. 1st July** of every year. Employees completing **six months and above** in the revised pay structure as on 1st July will be eligible to be granted the increment." Provisos: employee at the maximum of the pay band moves to the next higher pay band after one year at the maximum (no progression beyond PB-4). [Note: in the 2009 Rules there is a single increment date; the Bombay HC in *Jyotiram Tupe* (06.08.2026) read "six months" as the qualifying condition entitling a 30-June retiree to a notional increment — see file 05.]
- **Rule 11 – Fixation of pay of employees switching over later** (on date of next increment after 01.01.2006): fixed on basic pay + dearness pay + DA as on 01.01.2006 at pre-revised rates.
- **Rule 12 – Re-appointment to a post held before 01.01.2006**: benefit as if the post were held on 01.01.2006 (proviso to Rule 11 of the 1981 Rules applied).
- **Rule 13 – Fixation of pay on promotion on or after 01.01.2006**: **one increment @3%** of (pay in band + grade pay of the lower post), rounded to next 10, added to pay in band; then the **grade pay of the promotion post**; if the resultant pay in band is below the minimum of the higher band, stepped up to that minimum. **Option** to fix from date of promotion or from the date of next increment (1 July) (as under Rule 11 of the 1981 Rules).
- **Rule 14 – Time-bound promotion / ACP beneficiaries**: grade pay admissible per availability of further promotion; where no further promotion is available, additional grade pay of Rs 200–500 depending on existing grade pay (verify figures in Schedule).
- **Rule 15 – Arrears**: arrears for 01.01.2006–31.03.2009 credited to GPF in five equal annual instalments; first instalment adjusts the advance sanctioned by FD GR dated **06.10.2008**.
- **Rule 16 – Overriding effect**: "the provisions of the Maharashtra Civil Services (Pay) Rules, 1981 shall not, save as otherwise provided in these rules, apply to cases where pay is regulated under these rules, to the extent they are inconsistent with these rules."
- **Rule 17 – Power to relax**: Governor may relax any rule where its operation causes "undue hardship in any particular case", by order, to deal with the case in a just and equitable manner.
- **Rule 18 – Interpretation**: doubts referred to the Government in the Finance Department, whose decision "will be final".
- **Schedule / Annexures**: Schedule (department-wise posts with pay band & grade pay); Annexure I–II (pay bands, grade pays); Annexure III (entry pay for direct recruits); Annexure IV (form of option); Annexure V (fixation statement).

## How it is used in litigation
- **Petitioner claims**: (a) **notional increment on 1 July for 30-June retirees (2006–2015)** relying on Rule 10 "six months" + *KPTCL v. C.P. Mundinamani* (SC, 11.04.2023) and Bombay HC decisions (*Balaji Manikrao Biradar*, 2022; *Jyotiram Tupe*, 2026); (b) wrong fixation under Rule 7 (bunching, 1.86 factor), denial of stepping-up; (c) pay on promotion under Rule 13 and option; (d) wrong grade pay in the Schedule / parity with central posts; (e) increment denied due to EOL — challenge to GR 26.12.2011.
- **State's usual stand**: Rule 10 fixes 1 July as the date on which an increment *accrues*; an employee who retires on 30 June is **not in service** on 1 July and so no increment "becomes due" (FR-17 analogy; *Union of India v. Dhanlaxmi*-type reasoning and DoPT OM 03.02.2021 that *Ayyamperumal* was in personam); increments remain subject to Rules 36/39 of the 1981 Rules (FD circular 29.04.2009); EOL not counting for increment postpones the increment (GR 26.12.2011); Rule 18 makes FD the final interpreter; relief if any is confined to pension only and from 01.05.2023 onwards for third parties per SC interim order 06.09.2024, and now subject to the FD stay/review direction of October 2026 (see file 05). For court staff, the High Court adopted the 2009 Rules by its own notification/GR of the Law & Judiciary Department — the applicability date and option formalities are pleaded from that notification.

## मराठी सारांश
वित्त विभागाच्या दि. २२.०४.२००९ च्या अधिसूचनेने (क्र. वेपुर-१२०९/प्र.क्र.२७/सेवा-९) सहाव्या वेतन आयोगानुसार सुधारित वेतन नियम २००९ दि. ०१.०१.२००६ पासून लागू झाले. नियम ७ नुसार मूळ वेतन × १.८६ + ग्रेड पे; नियम ९ नुसार ३% वेतनवाढ; **नियम १० नुसार सर्वांना एकच वेतनवाढ दिनांक १ जुलै, सहा महिने सेवा पूर्ण असल्यास**; नियम १३ पदोन्नतीवरील वेतननिश्चिती; नियम १६ – १९८१ च्या वेतन नियमांवर अधिभावी. दि. २९.०४.२००९ चे परिपत्रक वेतनवाढ १९८१ च्या नियम ३६ व ३९ च्या अधीन ठेवते; दि. २६.१२.२०११ चा शासन निर्णय असाधारण रजेमुळे वेतनवाढ पुढे ढकलण्याबाबत आहे (हे दोन्ही अधिकृत संकेतस्थळावरून मिळवता आले नाहीत – पडताळणी आवश्यक). ३० जून रोजी निवृत्त झालेल्यांच्या काल्पनिक वेतनवाढीच्या याचिकांमध्ये हा नियम १० केंद्रस्थानी असतो.
