# Maharashtra General Provident Fund Rules, 1998 — key provisions
# महाराष्ट्र सर्वसाधारण भविष्य निर्वाह निधी नियम, 1998 — प्रमुख तरतुदी

**Issuing department:** Finance Department (Rules notified 1998; corrected up to 14.02.2017 per AG(A&E)-I Mumbai)
**Official sources:** AG(A&E)-I Maharashtra, Mumbai GPF page — https://cag.gov.in/ae/mumbai/en/page-ae-mumbai-gpf-calculation (fetched; gives Rule 12 and interest table); Rules PDF https://cag.gov.in/themes/a_e/uploads/pdfs/mumbai/MGPF_Rules1.pdf (listed; binary could not be text-extracted — verify rule text there).
**Status:** In force for employees appointed before 01.11.2005 (NPS employees are outside GPF).

## Key provisions (from AG Mumbai page — summary/verbatim as marked)
- **Rule 8(1)(b):** maximum subscription — not more than the subscriber's emoluments; excess carries no interest.
- **Rule 11(2), 11(3):** amounts forwarded directly by the subscriber (counted from 1st of month of receipt subject to the 5th/15th cut-off).
- **Rule 12 (interest):** minimum 4 % if the notified rate falls below 4 %; interest credited on last day of the year on opening balance less withdrawals plus deposits from their dates; recoveries from pay count from 1st of the month of recovery; interest on amounts payable under Rules 28–30 runs to end of the month of payment or six months after it became payable, whichever is less (Accounts Officer may extend to one year; beyond that senior officers); temporary posts — six months after termination; retirement — the six months exclude the month after retirement; overdrawals charged at 2.5 % above normal rate; subscriber may decline interest in writing.
- **Rules 25, 26, 28, 29, 30:** payments / final withdrawal (headings only; text not extracted).
- **Rule 32:** transfer of balance from another provident fund.
- Interest rates (table on AG page): 12 % (1986-87 to 1999-00) … 8 % (2003-04 to 2010-11) … 7.9 % (07/2019–03/2020), 7.1 % (04/2020 onwards per later notifications — current rate 7.1 % as reflected in GIS GR 31.01.2025, file 49).

## Related
- FD GRs notifying yearly GPF interest (not fetched); GAD/FD GRs on advances/withdrawals delegation (not fetched); NPS procedure GR 30.12.2024 (file 46).

## How it is used in litigation
- Claims for interest on delayed GPF final payment beyond six months (Rule 12(4) framework) and for missing credits; State/AG stand: delay attributable to non-submission of forms, missing credits to be reconciled from DDO schedules; interest beyond the rule period needs sanction of the competent authority.

## मराठी सारांश
- महाराष्ट्र सर्वसाधारण भविष्य निर्वाह निधी नियम 1998 (14.02.2017 पर्यंत सुधारित) — नियम 8 (वर्गणी मर्यादा), नियम 12 (व्याज: किमान 4%; विलंबाने देयक असल्यास कमाल 6 महिने, लेखा अधिकारी 1 वर्षापर्यंत वाढवू शकतात; जादा उचल 2.5% अधिक दराने), नियम 25–30 (अंतिम प्रदान), नियम 32 (हस्तांतरण).
- सध्याचा व्याजदर 7.1%. 01.11.2005 नंतरच्या (NPS) कर्मचाऱ्यांना लागू नाही.
