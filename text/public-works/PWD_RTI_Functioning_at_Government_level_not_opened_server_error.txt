1. Functioning of Public Works Department at Government level.

Two Secretaries [Secretary (Roads) and Secretary (Works)] are the high
level Administrative Officers who works under the control of Hon.
Minister and Minister of State for Public Works Department at
Government level. Different policy decisions, technical resolutions,
budget of Public Works Department, constructions of State Highways
and other roads, Government buildings, maintenance of the same etc. are
dealt with at Government level.
1.1 Budget :
Road Development Plan for 20 years is prepared by the Government
taking into consideration needs of the roads in the State. For the
implementation of the plan, the works of Roads, Bridges and Buildings
are included in the budget. Progress of the works is reviewed regularly in
accordance with the availability of funds. In addition to Government
funds, the necessary required funds are also raised with the help of
NABARD, HUDCO and through BOT depending upon scope of works.
1.2 Approval to road maintenance and repairs programme.
The annual programme for maintenance and repairs of different roads is
prepared  at field level. The funds are made available after sanctioning
the programme at Government level. The priority is given to the
demands of people’s representatives and urgent needs  of users.
Guidelines in this respect by Government.
1.3 Functions regarding Legislative Assembly and Legislative Council.
Three sessions (March, July and December) of Legislative Assembly and
Legislative Council are  held every year. Question and answer budget,
supplementary demand, resolutions etc. are dealt with at Government
level in these sessions.
1.4 Transfers of Officers
Class-I and Class II Officers are  transferred according to the
Maharashtra Government, G.A.D. Circular dated 12.05.2006 as per rule-
2005.
1.5 Promotions of Officers
Officers are promoted according to the vacancies of post
and the seniority list published from time to time for different cadres.

2.1 Road Development Plan (20 Years Plan)

The previous Road Development Plans were not only limited to
the available  funds for construction but for road development also.
Therefore while preparing the 1981-2001 Road Development plans, it was
envisaged of idea of master plan which would consider the expectations of
common peoples of rural area without limitations of finance.  The prime
objective of midterm review of 1981-2001 Road Development Plan was the
financial development of rural area of the country and the development of
villages and small towns. This would help to stop the inflow of the people
from villages  to the small or big cities. The importance is given to the
improvement of living standard of villagions. To achieve this target, all
villages will be connected by all weather Roads. Government is emphasizing
on common need and expectations of rural area.
Important objectives of 1981-01 Road Development Plan (Reviewed
Plan) are as under-
A) Network of National Highways comparising 100 kms wide area.
B) Construction of Express Highways for high intensity zone for fast
communication.
C) Connecting District head quarters, sub divisional head quarters,
major industrial centers, commercial centers, tourist places, major
agricultural business centers  and ports etc. by State Highways.
D) Connecting all cities to N.H. ways or S.H. ways by road network
Connecting all villages having population  1500 & more by
M.D.R. network.
E) Villages having population between 1000 to 1500  be connected by
other district roads.
F) At least one all weather road to every village.
G)Construction of ‘Sakavs’ to villages situated in hilly and
    inaccessible areas where agricultural and other facilities are located
    beyond river / creek.

Road Development Plan 2001-2021

Government of India has published Road Development Vision-2021
in November 2001. According to the guidelines mentioned Road
Development Plan. 2001-2021 is being prepared.

The main targets as per vision-2021 are as below –
1.
Express Ways
All expressways are to be included in Road Development Plan
2001-2021 which were not included in 1981-2001. Four-laning of the
high density National Highway corridors to be completed by 2021.
2.
National Highways
Important state Highways to be proposed for upgradation as
National Highways.
3.
State Highways
Connectivity major ports, industrial complexes, important
growth nodes, pilgrimage and tourist centres. Two laning to roads
connecting District Head uarters and minimum Intermediate lane
roads to Taluka places connecting District Head Quarters. Two laning
to be completed by 2021. All District Head Quarters should have ring
roads around city and Taluka places should have bypass outside town.
4.
Major District Roads
Major District Road should pass through villages having
population more than 1500 & important market places. Major District
Roads having very heavy traffic should either be two lane or four
lane.
5.
Other District Roads
The other District Roads should pass through the villages
having population 1000 to 1500.
6.
Village Roads
Non plan roads which are developed and sanctioned should be
included as plan village roads.
7.
Village Connectivity
100% villages to be connected having population more than
500 as per census 2001 and 50% villages to be connected
having population 200 to 500. The villages having population
upto 500 should have at least one all weather road and to be
connected by 2010 under Pradhan Mantri Gram Sadak Yojna
(PMGSY).

2.3 The works of Roads and Bridges  undertaken through
Privatisation
   [B.O.T.]
Maharashtra Government has prepared  Road Development
Plan for the period of 20 years and implement the project of road
construction and improvement. After every 20 years, the new road
development plan  is prepared. It is very difficult to achieve the target of the
road development plan due to paucity of funds. The development process
does not motivated unless the industrial centers, business centers in
Maharashtra are connected by the roads. Roads with sufficient transport
capacity are to be built to connect  district head quarters to capital cities and
district head quarters to other district head quarter. In view of the above
Maharashtra Government formed “Maharashtra State Road Development
Corporation (MSRDC)” to implement the projects of improvement to state
roads.
2.3.1 The works under privatisation are executed on the principle of build
operate and transfer. The entrepreneur is  constructing roads at his own cost.
After completion of work, the entrepreneur is allowed to recover the total
amount by the way of toll collection.
2.3.2 The following points are considered while proposing a project
through privatisation.
1. The Government  vide resolution No. Khakshesa/2002/Praka 279/Raste-9
dt. 9.1.2003 has given a guide line for the types of project which can be
implemented through privatization , New construction of Roads and
bridge, improvement of existing road for widening to 4 lane,
improvement of 2 lane B.T. road for minimum 25 kms. length of road,
Bypasses, Railway over bridges are the predominant type of works which
can be taken up through  privatization.
2. Schedule ‘A’ of the above Govt. Resolution includes the list of  vehicles
exempted from paying toll tax and norms of concession in toll tax.
3. The rate of toll tax are separate for the project cost up to 20 crore and
project cost between 20 to 75 crores. The toll rates far the period up to
31st March 2052 are given in schedule B of the above circular. The toll
tax policy is decided separately for the project costing more than 75
crore.
4. The Government has issued the guide lines for the preparation of
feasibility report for the project to be undertaken through B.O.T. vide
circular No. Khakshesa /2002/Praka/279(III)/Raste –9  dt. 9.1.2003.

2.4 Road Maintenance and Repairs

2.4.1 Public Work Departments Manual chapter No.3, Para No.13 classifies
the works of P.W. Department in 2 categories.
1.   Original Works –
Defined as purely new construction (Road and Building) or Addition
or alteration to the existing  structure, similarly complete repairs to newly
purchased, acquired  structure which was   left previously but now necessary
to use again. (P.W. Department manual Para 131 (1))
2. Maintenance and Repairs :
Definition includes the construction work for the maintenance and repairs
of the building and roads in general use to keep the structure properly.
2.4.2 To maintain and Repair the existing roads to observe that there should
not be any interruption to the traffic is  the main purpose  of the road repairs.
To maintain the roads properly is the prime duty of the P.W. Department. To
keep the traffic plying smooth on the National Highways, State Highways,
Major District Roads, is the responsibility  of the P.W. Department.
Similarly Zilla Parishad is fully responsible for Other District Roads and
Village Roads.

The expenditure for the maintenance and Repairs of National
Highways is being borne by Central Government, whereas the State
Government is allotting the funds required to maintain and repair of the
State Highways Major District Roads, Other District Road, Village Road
(PW Manual Para 131 (2))
Maintenance and Repairs of National Highways.
2.4.3  P.W. Department maintain and repair the National Highways in
Maharashtra state. The length of National Highway in Maharashtra is @
4000 kms. For the repairs of National Highways the funds are allotted by
MORT & H New Delhi and accordingly repairs are being carried out by
P.W. Department.

Major items involved in maintenance and repairs  of  National
Highways include
A) Necessary pre mansoon activities to be carried out to avoid pot holes on
the surface of road (Providing road site gutters, dressing site, shoulders,
seal coat etc.)
B) To fill the pot holes by emulsion and metal during mansoon and by
asphalt and metal after mansoon.
C) Removing bushes on the side shoulders and curves filling side shoulders
by murum.
D) Painting of K.M. / 200 mt. / guard stone / pillers / sign boards etc.

E) Other necessary work as per requirement.
The funds allotment for maintenance and repairs of National Highways
changes as per prevalent market rates, index etc.
2.4.4 MORT& H has issued revised guidelines for periodic renewals and
        surface quality improvements work  on National Highways.
       Accordingly stretches of National Highways are to be selected. Details
       are listed below.
1) Criteria for surface quality improvement works
i)
Length of which existing layer is not incomplete or it not divided

OR
ii)
Two  lane road carrying traffic of 1500 CVD or more and
roughness index is more than 3500 mm per kilometer.
iii)
Single lane road geometrically in order having traffic intensity
more than 100 CVD and roughness index more than 3500mm/km.
OR
iv)
Such length which was strengthened 5 years back but renewal not
Done and due to increased traffic intensity indications of bad
surface are there.
      v)      Such length  should not be less than 10 kms.
2) Criteria for periodical  renewals
i)
Such length of the road where traffic intensity is between 450 to 1500
CVD and minor repairs are necessary and  where need of renewal is
justifiable.
ii)
Such length of the road which has been strengthened in prvious
programme and condition of surface in quite good comparatively and
by renewal its surface  will become durable and quality of surface
will become fine and proposed renewal will last for minimum 3 years.
iii)
The proposed renewal length be @ 10 km as far as possible or the
length of integrated estimate for stretches of the length will be 10
kms.

2.6.District Schedule of Rates

2.6.1 Construction and maintenance Repairs of roads and buildings is done
in P.W. Department. The funds are made available by Government for these
works. For the construction of original work or maintenance and repairs of
existing structures the plans and estimates are prepared and got sanctioned
by competent authority. For preparation of the estimate, survey is taken, the
scope is decided and accordingly items and cost is worked out. For working
out estimated cost it is necessary to fix the rate of each item. The DSR
Committee is form for Nashik Region. The committee comprises of all the
Superintending Engineer in the region and all the Executive Engineer at
district head quarters. Zonal Superintending Engineer is the Chairman of this
committee and other are members.
2.6.2 DSR is prepared  every year. Generally after the end of financial year
the meeting of the committee is held for preparation of DSR. It is completed
in 3 months and the implementation of DSR starts from 1st of July every
year. If there is delay in the finalization of DSR, then extension to last year’s
DSR is granted. In the DSR generally the rates of following items are
finalized.
1) The completed rate of items as per details  and as per category of work.
2) Labour rate and material rate of the completed item.
3) Independent labour rates.
4) Transportation rates per km. for construction material.
5) Rates of hire of machinery.
2.6.3 Following activity is done while preparing DSR.
1) The market rates of construction material are gathered by the Executive
Engineer of district head quarter.
2) The trend of the bidder for the tenders in the last year is observed.
3) Transportation rates are finalised after collecting the market rates of
petrol, Diesel and  transportation of construction material.
4) The rates of items  are finalised considering the market rates  and the
trend of the bidders. In the last year tenders.
5) If a new item is to be included in the DSR, then detail discussion
regarding rate analysis is held in the meting and then rate is finalised.
6) The sufficient no. of copies of DSR are made available in P.W.
Department , as the same DSR is useful for the works undertaken by
Municipality, Corporation and Private civil engineers.

2.15 Auction of fruits on road side trees

Number of  trees are planted along to road side  and Govt.
buildings premises. Trees are consisting of Mango, Tamarind, jack
fruit etc. The fruits are auctioned every year to obtain the revenue.

Following procedure is adopted for auction
1. Notice of auction is published in news papers, Grampanchayats  of
nearby villages, Sub Division Officers etc.
2. The place and time of auction is decided in such way that villagers
from nearby villages can reach easily.
3. Notice is published well in advance.
4. Terms and Conditions of auction are read before the auction.
5. All the participants has to sign attendance sheet. Nobody can
participate without  signature or thumb impression.
6. All the doubts regarding terms and conditions are resolved.
7. Security deposit is taken from all the participants. This deposit is
returned to unsuccessful participants after the auction is over.
8. If the higher bid received is not proper and justifiable Executive
Engineer can reject the auction and recall.
9. Person with maximum offer has to pay full amount immediately. If
a person fails to do so his deposit is forfeited and re auction is
conducted.

3.4 Fixation of Rent for Buildings

3.4.1 Government of Maharashtra has given instructions regarding
issuing the rent certificate for the private building which are accommodated
for government use vide Public Works Department marathi circular No.
RNT/1084/890/Karasan/Milkat vyavasthapan – 3 dated 23rd April 1984.

Before fixation of rent, the user department should obtain necessary
certificate for non availability of Govt. accommodation from concerned
Executive Engineer P.W.D.
3.4.2 Then the user departments  should submit the rent proposal in
prescribed form.
1. Form 1 and  2 ,  Form 1 will be submitted by the owner of building &
Form 2 will submitted by the concern department.
2. Approved plan of the building in original or attested certified copy.
3. Certificate from concern department regarding the available area in the
building is sufficient.
4. 7/12 abstract , Form 8.
5. Consent of the owner of the building.
6. Details of staff, officers and their post sanctioned by Govt. of
Maharashtra along with sanction letter.
7. The purchase or  sale index in the  area in which building is situated from
the concern Deputy Registrar Class I or Deputy Registrars (Mahasul)
8. The recent receipt of the taxes paid to the Municipal Corporation/
Grampanchayat authority.
9. Completion certificate
10. Agreement between owner of building and user department on stamp
paper costing Rs. Twenty.
3.4.3   After receiving the  proposal along with above forms and other
papers, the Executive Engineer will call for report of the Deputy Engineer
concern. Deputy Engineer will inspect the site and submit report to the
Executive Engineer. The report will scrutinized in the office of the
Executive Engineer and rent / revised rent certificate will be issued.
3.4.4 Procedure for rent fixation.
1. Valuation of the building  on the basis of type of construction i.e. plinth,
flooring, walls, roof, doors / windows, year of construction of the
buildings, the depreciation and maintenance cost etc. is carried out. In
this 6% interest and 2% maintenance cost  is considered with total
amount.
2. After this valuation of the land used for the building is obtained from
concern Deputy Registrar of Revenue department on the basis of

=== पृ. 73 (textlayer) ===
73
prevailing rates of sale and purchase rates. On this amount 6% interest
considered.
3. For fixing the rent of the proposed building expenditure likely to be
incurred on water supply, drainage arrangement, electrification is
considered and on this total likely expenditure 6% interest  and 4.5%
maintenance cost is considered.
4. Sum of above 1 to 3 points is the annual rent of the proposed building.
Though the rent is fixed as per the steps 1 to 4  maintained above. The
Executive Engineer has to take care about the conditions mentioned in
Government circular date 16.2.1987 before finalizing the rent, the
conditions are as listed below.
1. The Executive Engineer should process the rent case proposals
only received with due recommendation from concern head of the
department and office in charge, and no proposal received from
private institutes and owners should be entertained.
2. While issuing rent certificate it should be clearly mentioned that
the rate is inclusive of all taxes or otherwise.
3. While submitting the proposals to executive Engineer for rent
fixation / revision of rent of the building or land where rent act is
inforce the user department should clearly mentioned in the
proposal about the enforcement of rent act because it is the
responsibility of the concern administrative department who is
hiring building to take cognigence  of the rent act and  obtain the
protection applicable as per this act.
4. While issuing the rent certificate it is necessary to mention the

3.5
Facilities for physically handicapped
3.5.1 Guidelines providing facilities to physically handicapped persons in
residential and office buildings are issued vide Govt. circular No. Bdg-
1290/CR-66/Buildings-1
dated
31.3.1990
and
BDG-1098/CR-
298/Buildings-2 dated 31.5.1999.
3.5.2 While constructing office and residential buildings following special
facilities are to be provided (All special facilities shall be as per Indian
Standard Institute code for “Recommendation for building and facilities for
the Physical Handicap ”) No.IA 4963:1968.
A) Residential Buildings :
Of the total tenements, at least 1% to 2% tenements on ground floors
shall be provided with following special facilities for physically
handicapped persons.
1) Ramp with handrail from ground to plinth level shall be provided with
the care that slope of the ramp shall be maximum 1:12 ( If ramp cannot
be provided because of non availability of sufficient land then in such
case instead of ramp wheel chair lift should be provided.)
2) Flooring of rough shahabad or rough Tandur shall be provided. Polished
surface shall be strictly avoided.
3) In toilets European type W.C. pan shall be provided with flush controlled
at lower level and grab arms. Doors width shall be enough for easy
movement of the wheel chair.
4) Special types of revolving doors and grab arms shall be provided with the
care that door width shall be enough for easy movement of wheel chair.
5) Threshold to door shall not be provided.
6) Electrical switches shall be provided at such a lower height that they can
be operated easily by sitting in the wheel chair.
7) For the guidance of handicapped persons information boards giving
details of location of facilities shall be provided.
B) OFFICE BUILDINGS
In addition to the facilities provided in residential building following
additional facilities  shall be provided in office building on ground floor.
1. In case of multi story building lift of sufficient size to accommodate
the wheel chair shall be provided.
2. In lift information in “Brail mode” shall be given.
3. At least one separate toilet for handicapped ladies and gents  with
special facilities shall be provided at ground floor. In this toilet special
facilities like European type W.C. pan with grab arms at lower level
and door of sufficient size for easy movement of wheel chair shall be
provided.

3.6 Inspection of Cinema/ Video halls

Cinema halls / Video hall located at big villages, taluka and district
places are main source of entertainment for informity Government of
Maharashtra has framed rules and regulation with guidance for construction,
operation and maintenance. These rules are turn as Maharasta Cinema Talkie
rules 1966. All temporary and permanent structures showing cinemas are
treated as talkies / theatre. These rules are updated time to time  to suit the
requirement.
3.6.1 Theatres are classified in following categories.
1) Permanent

2) Quasi-Permanent
3) Touring

3) Drive-in-Theatre
Type (1) Permanent Theatres –
Theatres  showing moving pictures and constructed with stones,
bricks, earth, lime or other non flammable material.
Type (2) Quasi permanent –
Theatres built with iron angle, iron bullies etc. or like non flammable
material.
Type (3) Touring Theatres –

Theatres in which stage, machinery and its structure is movable from
one place to other.
Type (4) Drive in theaters  --

Theatres which is open and viewers can se picture while sitting in the
vehicles. The number of vehicles are limited to 1000.
3.6.2 No objection certificate and permission is granted by District
Collector / Superintendent of Police / Commissioner of Police. Validity of
permission may vary from 3 month to 2 years. Before construction of theatre
the owner has to obtain permission for construction from competent
authority. For obtaining such permission owner has to submit application
along with plans, lay out of land  a detailed plan of cinema  theatre and also
information required for civil construction. Plan, elevation and sections
along land plan, stairs, open space, roof, door, windows, fire fighting
provision, toilets, parking, water supply / drainage system, internal roads,
electrification etc. details are to be submitted along with application to
revenue department.
3.6.3 For comments of civil structures like stability, type of construction etc.
application is forwarded to the Public Works authorities (Executive
Engineer)

3.7 Maintenance and Repairs of Buildings

3.7.1 The main  aim of P.W. Department is to maintain , repair and keep in
good condition  government public building. Time to time maintenance and
repairs of buildings so as to increase  buildings life and increase in utility
period.
Government building are classified into two main parts.
1. Residential Buildings  2. Non residential buildings
Classification of building as per financial norms of repairs and maintenance
is as per below.
A-group – Administrative building
B- group – Residential building
C-group – Hospital building.
3.7.2 As per Maharashtra P.W.D. para 377 Superintending Engineer has to
keep the record of Government building in his circle. Concern Executive
Engineer has also to keep the record of Government building in his division.
In this register details regarding such as year of construction, plinth area,
name of concern user department, period of use, details about major
specified repairs carried out etc. is necessary. Record of all buildings
constructed by government, purchased or taken in possession by
government, should be kept uptodate.
3.7.3 Type of building repairs

Following are the 3 types of repair for maintenance of buildings
• Specified repairs (SDR)
• Special repairs (SLR)
• Current repairs (CR)
(A) Specified repairs (SDR) :
Under this repairs programme items for structural, strengthening of
building to increase the life and for safety purpose of building are mainly
included. Vide Government P.W.D.Circular No.BPL-1290/CR-595/Bldg
dated 10.4.90
SDR – (1) Replacement of broken old roof tiles, Renovation of  roof by
providing  A.C.  cement sheets.
SDR –(2) Replacement of rotten doors, windows and their renovation.
SDR-(3)  To prevent the leakage, repairing the water supply and drainage
line.
SDR – (4) To maintain the structure of building by providing gunitting,
grouting and rust preventive method.
SDR-(5) Renovation of outdated electric wiring.
SDR –(6) Replacement of outdated electric motor, pump set, lift etc.

work of drainage system to drain out the water surrounding the building.
SDR-(10) Reconstruction of old and crack brick pillars and walls.
SDR-(11) Replacement of roof in rainy season.
SDR-(12) Renovation of damaged flooring

4.3 Registration of Spare Part Suppliers and Workshops

mechanical wing. Repairs of machinery are commenced after obtaining
technical sanction to repair estimate from competent authority. Spare parts
are procured locally by calling quotations as and when required without
maintaining stock with the deptt. There is variety of machinery make,
models with the deptt, hence spare parts are procured locally to avoid
blocking of funds.

or with departmental staff. But, some times repairs are carried out from
private workshops where departmental workshop is not fully equipped or
special tools are not available or sufficient skilled staff is not available. The
private workshops are registered as per Govt.circular No. STD-1094/28
Desk-Mech. dated 16th July 1996.
4.3.3
Generally in the month of June and December offers for such
registration are invited from interested parties by giving notification in news
paper. Prescribed blank application forms are available with division at
prescribed cost. Interested parties are applying to division with required
documents such as shop act registration, sales tax registration, income tax
clearance, details of shops and business etc.

In case of private workshop, the documents such as shop act
registration, indemnity bond, list of special tools, equipment and skilled
workers are also attached with the application.

signatures of head quarter Executive Engineer and committee member are
submitted to region office. After scrutiny by Assistant Chief Engineer

is submitted to Superintending Engineer (Mech.) Konkan Bhavan , Navi
Mumbai for registration.  Superintending Engineer (Mech.) after scrutiny
convey to receive registration fee from applicant. After receiving report of
receipt of registration fee Superintending Engineer (Mech.) issues
registration certificate.
