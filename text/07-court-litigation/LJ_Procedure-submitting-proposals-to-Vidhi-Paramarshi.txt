Rule 8: Matters on which opinion of Remembrancer
of Legal Affairs may
not be sought:-
Generally the opinion of the Remembrancer of Legal Affairs may not be
sought on the following matters:
(1) Points concerning mainly the questions of facts or policy matters;
(2) Cases of hypothetical nature;
(3) Ordinary departmental procedure or administrative matters; and
(4) Scrutiny of letters and resolutions which do not involve any legal point.
Considering the obvious advantages of following a standardized pattern it is
expected that, hereafter, all references forwarded to this department are in strict
compliance with the provisions mentioned above.
