Introduction -

The State Government implemented the Defined Contribution Pension Scheme on
the lines of Government of India, for the employees who are recruited on or after 1st
November, 2005 vide Government Resolution referred to at Sr. No.1. Further the
Government also decided to join the National Pension Scheme of Government of India.

2. The State Government has already created the State Record Keeping Agency (SRKA)
vide Government Resolution referred to at Sr. No.2. This Agency has been implementing the
scheme in the State, pending formation of the Central Record Keeping Agency (CRKA) by
the Government of India.
3. It is under consideration of Government to suppress the orders issued accordingly
vide Government Resolution Finance Department No. CPS 2012/96/Ser-4, dated
21.08.2014. referred to above at Sr.No.3.
Resolution

Government of India w.r.t. the employees recruited on or after 1st November,2005 in the
State Government, Zilla Parishads, Recognized and Aided Educational Institutions,
Agricultural/Non-Agricultural Universities and _ affiliated Non-Government Colleges,
