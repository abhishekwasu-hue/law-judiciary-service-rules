CHAPTER I.
INTRODUCTORY •.
1. The work in the Secretariat is divided into 12 departments and the
subjects allotted to each Department are mentioned in the Rules of Business.
Each Department is placed under a Seoretary, who is assisted by Deputy
Secretaries, Under/Assistant Secretaries and a number of branches. The
number of branches varies from department to department. Each branch
is managed by one Superintendent, one or more Senior and Junior Assistants
and two or more General Duty Clerks. The work of noting and drafting is
done by Senior and Junior Assistants and also by Superintendents. The
General Duty Clerks in a branch attend to the work of keeping movement
of papers, tracing previous references, keeping papers on which some action
is to be taken after a certain period duly arranged, and compiling properly
papers on. which final action is taken.
