authorities should take particular care to see that Government orders conveying the need
of making recruitment through the Employment Exchanges are followed scrupulously and
even where in view of urgency they are forced to make appointments directly without
reference to the Employment Exchanges they should ensure that the persons sc appointed
are not continued in service for more than three months and that they are replaced by

upto a period of three months straightway without making any effort to obtain candidates
from the Employment Exchanges for temporary vacancies which are likely to last upto
three months. It is, therefore, specifically brought to the notice of all the appointing
