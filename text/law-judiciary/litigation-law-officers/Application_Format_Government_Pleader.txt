Notice

Government of Maharashtra, Law and Judiciary Department, Mantralaya, Mumbai
invites applications in prescribed form, from eligible candidates for the post of
“Government Pleader” at High Court of Judicature of Bombay, Appellate Side ( Writ Cell),
Mumbai.
2.
The above appointment will be purely on temporary basis and terminable at any time
without assigning any reason and subject to the provisions of order XXVII of the Code of
Civil Procedure, 1908, (V of 1908), Section 24 (1) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973
(II of 1974) and Rule 11, 12, 13 & 30 of the Maharashtra Law Officers (Appointment,
Conditions of Service and Remuneration) Rules, 1984.
3.
Eligibility for appointment :-
1) Applicant must be a Citizen of India.
2) Applicant must hold a degree in Law from any recognized university and be enrolled as an
Advocate with the Bar Council of Maharashtra and Goa.
3) Applicant must not be more than 58 years of age on the date of advertisement.
4) The Upper age limit of 58 years shall be relaxed upto 2 (two) years in case of the applicants
who had worked on or before the date of advertisement as the Government Pleader; or
Government Pleader and Public Prosecutor; or Additional Government Pleader and
Additional Public Prosecutor in the High Court.
5) Applicant must have atleast 10 (ten) years of practice as an Advocate ordinarily in the High
Court, on the date of advertisement, certified by the Registrar of High Court or as the case
may be, Prothonotary & Senior Master, that he ordinarily practices in the High Court.
4.
Procedure to Apply:-
4.1
The application duly filled in must be addressed and sent to the Registry, Law and
Judiciary Department, Room No. 345, 3rd Floor, Annex Building, Mantralaya, Mumbai-
400032.

4.2
Application shall reach at the given address in office hours on or before 09th
February, 2024.
4.3
On the envelope “Application for ……..(post name) and ……(side for which
applied)” shall be clearly mentioned. It should be noted that application sent at any other
place or by any other mode shall not be accepted.
4.4
The proforma of Application is available on the official website of the Government
(www.maharashtra.gov.in) and the official website of the Law and Judiciary Department
(lj.maharashtra.gov.in)
5.
The last Date for submission of application is 09th February, 2024 in office hours
(upto 06.15 P.M.). The applications received after the last date as well as incomplete,
illegible, not in prescribed proforma and without necessary attachments will not be
considered.
6.
About enclosures to be attached to documents, note given below the prescribed format
be strictly followed.
7.
The Government of Maharashtra in Law and Judiciary Department reserves the right
to shortlist the number of eligible candidates for interview after scrutiny of the applications.
8.
Canvassing or pressurizing in any manner by any candidate shall entail
disqualification.
