Constitution of India.

 No. LOR-2021/C.R.251/D-XIV.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309
of the Constitution of India, the Governor of Maharashtra hereby makes the following rules further
to amend the Maharashtra Law Officers (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Remuneration)
Rules, 1984,  namely :—
1.  These rules may be called the Maharashtra Law Officers (Appointment, Conditions of Service
and Remuneration) (Amendment) Rules, 2022.
2.  In rule 2 of the Maharashtra Law Officers (Appointment, Conditions of Service and
Remuneration) Rules, 1984 (hereinafter referred as “the Principal Rules”),—
(i)  after clause (a), the following clause shall be inserted, namely :—
“(a-i)	 “Chief Presenting Officer” means an advocate appointed to that post under these
rules by the Government in Law and Judiciary Department in relation to the Maharashtra
Administrative Tribunal, Mumbai and its benches at Nagpur and Aurangabad, to conduct
Original Application, Miscellaneous Application or any other Application filed under the
Administrative Tribunals Act, 1985 (13 of 1985) for and on behalf of the State or its officers
and includes Presenting Officer ; ” ;
(ii)  for clause (l), the following clause shall be substituted, namely :—
“(l)  “Law Officer” means an advocate appointed by the Government in the Law and
Judiciary Department, under these rules, to conduct cases on behalf of the State or its officers
before any Court or the Maharashtra Revenue Tribunal or Maharashtra Administrative
Tribunal and includes an Advocate-General, a Government Pleader, Public Prosecutor and
Chief Presenting Officer ; ” ;
3.  In rule 3 of the Principal Rules, after sub-rule (j), the following sub-rule shall be added,
namely :—
“(k)  for the Maharashtra Administrative Tribunal, the Chief Presenting Officer and the
Presenting Officer for the Maharashtra Administrative Tribunal at Mumbai, Nagpur and
Aurangabad.”.
4.  In rule 4 of the principal Rules, after sub-rule (g), the following rule shall be added,
namely :—
“(h) the Presenting Officer for the Maharashtra Administrative Tribunal at Mumbai, Nagpur
and Aurangabad shall be subordinate to the Chief Presenting Officer for the Maharashtra
Administrative Tribunal at Mumbai, Nagpur and Aurangabad, respectively.”.
5.  In Chapter III of the principal Rules, for the heading, the following heading shall be
substituted, namely :—
“QUALIFICATIONS AND APPOINTMENT OF THE GOVERNMENT PLEADERS, PUBLIC
PROSECUTORS AND CHIEF PRESENTING OFFICERS.”.
