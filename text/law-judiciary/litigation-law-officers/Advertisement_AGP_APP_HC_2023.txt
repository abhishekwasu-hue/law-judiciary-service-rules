NOTICE

Government of Maharashtra, Law and Judiciary Department, Mantralaya, Mumbai
invites applications in the prescribed form, from eligible candidates for further appointment
to posts of “Assistant Government Pleader and Additional Public Prosecutor” at High Court
of Judicature at Bombay, Bench at Aurangabad, Aurangabad.
2.
The above appointments will be purely on temporary basis and terminable at any
time without assigning any reason and subject to the provisions of order XXVII of the Code of
Civil Procedure, 1908, (V of 1908), Section 24 (1) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (II
of 1974) and Rule 11, 12, 13 & 30 of the Maharashtra Law Officers (Appointment, Conditions
of Service and Remuneration) Rules, 1984.
3.
Eligibility:-
1) Applicant must be a Citizen of India.
2) Applicant must hold a degree in Law from any recognized university and be
enrolled as an Advocate with the Bar Council of Maharashtra and Goa.
3) Applicant must not be more than 55 years of age on the date of advertisement.
4) The Upper age limit of 55 years shall be relaxed upto 5 (five) years in case of the
applicants who had worked on or before the date of advertisement as the Assistant
Government Pleaders in the High Court.
5) Applicant must have at least 7 (seven) years of practice as an Advocate ordinarily
in the High Court, on the date of advertisement.
4.
Procedure to Apply:-
4.1
The application duly filled in must be addressed to the Joint Secretary, Law
and Judiciary Department, Old High Court Building, Adalat Road, AURANGABAD – 431005.
4.2
Application shall reach at the given address in office hours on or before 15th
December, 2023.
