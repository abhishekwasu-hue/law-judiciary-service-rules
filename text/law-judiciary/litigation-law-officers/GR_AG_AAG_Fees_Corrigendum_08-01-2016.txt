Note-1: The rates of the fees under cluses 1,2 and 3 for the Conduct of Civil and Criminal Cases
Of  the Original and Appellete Side of the High Court of judicature at Bombay and its Benches at
Nagpur and Aurangabad, shall be payable to be the Advocate General and Associate Advocate General
subject to the condition that, if two  or more cases are conducted during a day, not more than Rs.60,000/- and
Rs.50,000/- per day, respectively , irrespective of the number of cases conducted by them during that day,shall be
admissible.
         Note-2: In the case of group matters where the matters are decided by comman judgement then only
one set of fees shall be payable.

2.
Apart from the revised rates mentioned above in the para-1 of this Government
Resolution, the terms and conditions prescribed in Maharashtra Law Officers (Appointment, Conditions
of Service and Remuneration) Rule,1984 and the Rules for the Conduct of Legal Affairs of
Government,1984,shall be applicable.

3.
The Expenditure involved on account of revision of fee shall be debited to the budget
head “Demand No. J-1, 2014 – Administration of Justice (voted), (00)114-Leagal Advisor and Counseles
(00) (01-Leagal Advisor and Counsele, City Officer (2014 029  9), (00) (02) Mofussil Officer (2014 030
5), (28) Profession and Special Services.” and shall be met from the sanctioned grants.

4.
This Resolution is issued with the concurrence of the Finance Department, vide its
unofficial reference number 6/Exp.6 dated 4.1.2015.

5.
The revised rates of fees shall be applicable with effect from date of issue of this order.
