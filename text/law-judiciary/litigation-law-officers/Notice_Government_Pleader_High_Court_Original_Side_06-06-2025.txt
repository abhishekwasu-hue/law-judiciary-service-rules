NOTICE

Government of Maharashtra, Law and Judiciary Department, Mantralaya, Mumbai
invites applications in the prescribed form, from eligible candidates for the post of
“Government Pleader” at High Court of Judicature of Bombay, Original Side, Mumbai.
2.
The above appointment will be purely on temporary basis and terminable at any
time without assigning any reason and subject to the provisions of order XXVII of the Code
of Civil Procedure, 1908, (V of 1908), Section 18 (1) of the Bhartiya Nagarik Surksha
Sanhita,2023 and Rule 11, 12, 13 & 30 of the Maharashtra Law Officers (Appointment,
Conditions of Service and Remuneration) Rules, 1984.
3.
Eligibility:-
1) Applicant must be a Citizen of India.
2) Applicant must hold a degree in Law from any recognized university and be
enrolled as an Advocate with the Bar Council of Maharashtra and Goa.
3) Applicant must not be more than 58 years of age on the date of advertisement.
4) The Upper age limit of 58 years shall be relaxed upto 2 (two) years in case of the
applicants who had worked on or before the date of advertisement as the Government
Pleaders; or Government Pleaders and Public Prosecutors; or Additional Government
Pleaders and Additional Public Prosecutors in the High Court.
5) Applicant must have at least 10 (ten) years of practice as an Advocate ordinarily in
the High Court, on the date of advertisement, certified by the Registrar of the High Court or,
as the case may be, Prothonotary & Senior Master, that he ordinarily practices in the High
Court.
4.
Procedure to Apply:-
4.1
The application duly filled in must be addressed to The Registry, Law and
Judiciary Department, Mantralaya, Room No. 345, 3rd Floor, Annex Building, Mantralaya,
Mumbai-400032

Application shall reach at the given address in office hours on or before 27th
June, 2025.
4.3
On the envelope “Application for ……..(post name for which applied)” shall be
clearly mentioned. It should be noted that application sent at any other place or by any
other mode and shall not be accepted.
