NOTIFICATION
CONSTITUTION OF INDIA. न

No.Misc.5915/(27)/D-14.—In exercise of the powers conferred by the proviso to article 309
read with article 165 of the Constitution of India, the Governor of Maharashtra is hereby pleased
to make the rules further to amend the Maharashtra Law Officers (Appointment, Conditions of
Service and Remuneration) Rules, 1984, as follows, namely :—

1. These rules may be called the Maharashtra Law Officers (Appointment, Conditions of
Service and Remuneration) (Second Amendment) Rules, 2015.

2. In rule 11 of the Maharashtra Law Officers (Appointment, Conditions of Service and
Remuneration) Rules, 1984, in sub-rule (1), in clause (c), the following proviso shall be inserted,
namely :—

“Provided that, the upper age limit of fifty-five years shall be relaxed upto five years in case
of the persons who are appointed on or before the date of commencement of the Maharashtra Law
Officers (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Remuneration) (First Amendment) Rules, 2015,
as the Government Pleaders or Public Prosecutors.”.

By order and in the name of the Governor of Maharashtra,
M. A. SAYEED,
