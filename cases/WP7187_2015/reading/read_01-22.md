## पृ. 8
Affidavit (cont.), para 5 (cont.) and para 6.

"the date of passing of the Government Resolution. I say and submit that the recommendation about the restructuring/ reconstruction (as per the recommendations of the Commission) involves the up-gradation of posts by abolition of cadre of Steno-cum-Clerk and creation of new posts of remaining three Grades of Stenographers for three levels of courts.
6. I say and submit that, the creation of new posts and abolition of posts can only be prospective and not retrospective. The employee is to be appointed or promoted to the post or promotional post only if such post exists on that day and he is otherwise eligible for the same. Only after creation of the additional posts of three Grades of Stenographers, the employee will be promoted. I say and submit that the posts, even if, were/are created retrospectively, then also the employee cannot be promoted to the posts retrospectively. Thus, creation of the additional posts three Grades of Stenographers with effect from 01.04.2003 is not useful / beneficial to the employees as they will not be promoted retrospectively. I say and submit that the recommendation of revised pay scale to all the then existing stenographer were made available with effect from 01.04.2003."

## पृ. 9
Affidavit (cont.), printed page 5 — paras 7, 8.

"7. I say and submit that, the eligibility for the posts of Steno-cum-Clerk and Stenographer is different.
