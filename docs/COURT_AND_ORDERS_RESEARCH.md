## (c) Steps the files show
1. A GP/AGP letter is received; it names the petition and the hearing date and asks for "जबाबदार अधिकारी" plus "परिच्छेदनिहाय अभिप्राय/शपथपत्र" (o13).
2. Desk-6 note: the background with page refs.
3. Desk-6 asks for information from the HC / District Court; when it is late, "स्मरणपत्र" (C R 28-2025, 34-2026).
4. The affidavit draft is placed on the file (पृ.41-45). It goes up the senior chain to प्र.स. व वि.प.
5. When the policy belongs to another department, the draft goes to that department for approval (GAD, ACS-Services). The authorised officer is "सह सचिव, विधि व न्याय विभाग, औरंगाबाद". In 249/2023 GAD filed the affidavit (dt. 05.09.2023; 168).
6. Desk-6 sends facts or the draft to the GP/AGP: letter to GP 15.05.2023 (o13); "शपथपत्राचे प्रारुप … सहायक सरकारी वकिल यांना पाठविण्यात आले" (016).
7. SLP / review petition: the decision goes through the "ई" शाखा (विधि शाखा) to सरकारी वकील, SC (letter 06.11.2023, reminder 30.07.2024; 167). Desk-9 also handles some court items and asks Desk-6 (150).

## B. Key provisions (verbatim)
- **S1 Rule 58(3)** (PDF p.70 / book 63): the administrative department "shall prepare or obtain parawise report on all the points raised in the Writ Petition and affidavit."
- **S1 Rule 58(4)** (PDF p.70-71 / book 63-64): the department "shall forward the complete case papers, as far as possible. within two weeks from the date of receipt by it or in an[y] case before the expiry of the date mentioned in the notice" to the GP. A copy goes to the RLA, or to the JS/DS of L&J at Nagpur/Aurangabad, with: "(a) Recommendations whether the Writ Petition should or should not be opposed; (b) self-contained note making references to rules, orders and precedent[s]; (c) such additional information as is likely to help the Government Pleader".
  - Note (i): "the Rule is returnable … within a period which is not less than 14 days after the service".
  - Note (ii): "the parawise report should not contain cryptic answers such as 'not admitted' or 'denied' but should contain elaborate factual position and should be invariably accompanied by copies of relevant notifications, rules and orders" (OCR).
- **S1 Rule 58(2)** (PDF p.70): the officer impleaded in his official capacity forwards "a copy of petition, a statement of facts … and his recommendations as to whether the Writ Petition should be opposed or not". He also sends copies to the GP.
- **S1 Rule 58(6)** (PDF p.71-72 / book 64-65):
  - (a) "The Government Pleader … shall then draft a counter affidavit".
  - (b) The department "shall depute a competent officer, conversant with the subject matter".
  - (c) The GP sends the draft "for verifying the correctness of the facts".
  - (d) The department sends it to the RLA/JS/DS, "who in turn shall send it duly approved".
  - (e) "The draft counter affidavit shall then be sworn in by the competent officer authorised to do so by the administrative department".
  - (f) Proviso (ii): where the validity of a statute is in issue, the draft goes to the RLA for approval.
- **S1 Rule 59(1)(c)** (PDF p.72): before a concession or undertaking, the GP takes an adjournment and refers the matter to the department.
- **S1 Rule 60** (PDF p.74): the GP communicates the decision and applies for a certified copy "on the very day of the decision".
- **S1 Rule 64(2)-(3)** (PDF p.79): SLP or appeal against the State. "parawise remarks" go to the GP, with copies to the Government Advocate (SC) and the RLA. The counter affidavit "shall be sworn in by the competent officer of the administrative department".
- **S1 Rule 32** (PDF p.36-40): defence of suits. Replies are given "to each paragraph of the plaint"; "parawise report should not be cryptic".
- **S10 / S1 Rule 7(1)(c)**: a reference to the RLA is made by an "officer not below the rank of a Deputy Secretary or the concerned Desk Officer". **Rule 7(1)(g)**: the department first reaches its own conclusion.
- **S2** (p.1): "within a period of one month of receipt of notice/summons from the Court or … intimation from the Government Pleader, provide instructions and information to the Government Pleader … so as to avoid delay in filing of affidavit-in Reply". The heads of departments are responsible for this.
- **S3 para (2)** (p.1): on receipt of a notice, "संबंधित सह/उप सचिव किंवा कार्यासन अधिकारी (कक्ष अधिकारी/अवर सचिव) … योग्य ते परिच्छेदनिहाय अभिप्राय संबंधित सादरकर्ता अधिकाऱ्यास पाठवावेत व त्यांच्याकडून शपथपत्राचे प्रारुप तयार करुन ते सक्षम प्राधिकाऱ्याच्या मान्यतेने विहित मुदतीत किंवा सुनावणीच्या आधी दाखल" (legacy font, normalised).
  - para (3): if there is not enough time, seek more time through the CPO/PO.
  - para (7): an adverse order goes with the PO's opinion "विधि व न्याय विभागाकडे, विधीविषयक कामकाज चालविण्याचे नियम, 1984 अन्वये".
  - para (10): a separate register of court cases in every कार्यासन; action against the officer for delay.
- **S4 para 2**: "while preparing parawise comments/affidavits-in-reply, the concerned officer shall either consult Law Officer, if posted in that Department, or the concerned Presenting Officer".
- **S5 para 2(a)-(c)**: the Chief Secretary's affidavit is drafted by the GP/PO. It reaches L&J for vetting "at least nine days before the date of filing". L&J sends it to the CS "seven days before". Then fair copies are made and the CS signs and affirms.
- **S6 para 2, 5** (OCR): when several departments are respondents, they file one "एकत्रित म्हणणे, लेखी कथन, प्रतिज्ञापत्र" after a consolidated "परिच्छेदनिहाय उत्तर" agreed by all. In complex constitutional cases the draft is filed "सक्षम अधिकाऱ्याच्या सहमतीने आणि आवश्यकता असल्यास मुख्य सचिवांच्या मान्यतेने".
- **S7 (1996)** (OCR): "instructions for filing affidavit be given by the officers of the level of Deputy Secretary". Departments must prepare "a consolidated parawise remarks" and send them to the PO/GP.
  - S7 (1993): the MAT objected that "affidavits-in-Reply are filed … by the officers who are not duly authorised".
- **S11**: Finance/GAD opinions are not to be named in an affidavit. The affidavit says "असे शासनाचे धोरण आहे".
- **S8 SOP**: for an adverse order, comply, or seek extension of time, or appeal through L&J "along with all relevant documents, affidavits, replies … and the opinion of the Government Pleader". Contempt notices go to the Nodal/Law Officer immediately (para II). Where the department cannot comply, it files "a detail affidavit" (para IV).
- **S9 Annex B**: 3.4.1 Nodal Officer "not below the rank of Under Secretary". 3.4.2(2) he "shall prepare the draft pleadings". 3.2.2(3) the committee enquires into delays in "para-wise remarks, affidavits-in-reply".

- **S12**: in PILs with time-bound directions, a Class-II officer writes "a letter every fortnight" to the GP.
- **S13**: file affidavits in time.
- **S14**: RLA and AG advice is confidential.

## 3. Who signs
- Authentication: NPM 52(ख) p.21 (MMOP 11.3 p.79 says the same): "राज्य शासनाच्या प्रत्येक आदेशावर किंवा लेखावर सचिव, अपर सचिव, सह-सचिव, उप-सचिव, अवर सचिव किंवा सहायक सचिव किंवा ज्याला त्या बाबतीत विशेषकरून अधिकार प्रदान करण्यात येईल असा दुसरा एखादा अधिकारी सही करील व अशी सही ही अशा आदेशांचे किंवा लेखाचे योग्य अधिप्रमाणन समजण्यात येईल." ## 4. Gazette parts (observed mastheads only)
- Ordinary Gazette every Thursday; Extraordinary any day (NPM 102(५); MMOP 10.1(4)). The press must be told the Part and Section, and the number of spare copies.
- भाग चार-अ = "महाराष्ट्र शासनाने केंद्रीय अधिनियमांन्वये तयार केलेले … नियम व आदेश". Art.309 rules (GAD) were published here.
- भाग चार-ब = "…महाराष्ट्र अधिनियमांन्वये तयार केलेले (भाग एक, एक-अ आणि एक-ल यांमध्ये प्रसिद्ध केलेले नियम व आदेश यांव्यतिरिक्त) नियम व आदेश".
- भाग आठ = "महाराष्ट्र विधानमंडळाचे अधिनियम व राज्यपालांनी प्रख्यापित केलेले अध्यादेश व केलेले विनियम आणि विधि व न्याय विभागाकडून आलेली विधेयके (इंग्रजी अनुवाद)".

## 5. Bilingual rule
- OL Act 1964 s.5(c) (H p.13): "Marathi shall … be the language to be used — … (c) in all orders, rules, regulations and bye-laws issued by the State Government …: Provided that, the State Government may use the English language for the purposes of clauses (a) to (c), if it is satisfied that it is necessary so to do under special circumstances or for technical reasons." s.4(2)(c) covers "resolutions … orders". MMOP 10.6 requires the Act to be "scrupulously implemented".
- Practice (I): the Marathi rules are followed in the same Gazette issue by the English "NOTIFICATION … By order and in the name of the Governor of Maharashtra". Translations of Acts are published under Art.348(3) in Part VIII "under the authority of the Governor" (H p.24).
