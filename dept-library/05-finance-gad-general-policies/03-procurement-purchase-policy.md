## 3.2 Related official document read (adjacent topic)
### Project contingency expenditure policy (PWD)
- Title (EN): Policy on project contingency expenses
- Title (MR): प्रकल्प आकस्मिक खर्चाचे शासन धोरण निश्चित करण्याबाबत
- GR no. (OCR-cleaned): संकीर्ण 2025/प्र.क्र.04/नियोजन-3 ; Date: 28 March 2025 ; Dept: Public Works Department
- URL [FETCHED]: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ea80421fc06a2ed5c7adc1018e0026df/uploads/2025/04/20250408886043888.pdf
- Key provisions (summary, verify against original): contingency follows Rule 145 of PWD Code; only project-related items; no new works without competent approval; illustrative eligible items include lawyer fees for court cases within fee limits; generally up to 3% of project cost (per GR of 16.02.2024); sanction limits: Executive Engineer up to Rs 25,000 or 10% of contingency; Superintending Engineer Rs 25,001 to 2,00,000 or 25%; Chief Engineer above Rs 2,00,000; procurement policy rules to be followed.
- Litigation relevance: Shows PWD-side payment of lawyer fees from project contingency; not a general Finance Dept rule. Applies to PWD projects only.
- Marathi gist: प्रकल्प आकस्मिक खर्च फक्त प्रकल्पाशी संबंधित बाबींसाठी; न्यायालयीन प्रकरणातील वकील शुल्क मर्यादेत अनुज्ञेय; मंजुरी अधिकार अभियंता स्तरानुसार. हा सा.बां. विभागासाठीचा निर्णय आहे.
