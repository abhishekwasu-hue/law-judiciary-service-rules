# 04. FRBM Act 2005, fiscal documents, economy / austerity measures

## 4.1 Maharashtra Fiscal Responsibility and Budgetary Management Act, 2005
- Title (EN): The Maharashtra Fiscal Responsibility and Budgetary Management Act, 2005 (Maharashtra Act No. 16 of 2005; enacted 20 April 2005 per India Code)
- Title (MR): महाराष्ट्र राजकोषीय उत्तरदायित्व व अर्थसंकल्पीय व्यवस्थापन अधिनियम, 2005
- Issuing dept: Finance Department
- Official URLs: India Code page https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/19920 [FETCHED: metadata and section list only; no PDF link shown]; Finance Dept PDF (Marathi/English listing, "FRBM Act") [LISTING] https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s349d4b2faeb4b7b9e745775793141e2b2/uploads/2025/01/2025012816555521.pdf
- Status: in force; amended (Act amendment 29.12.2006 and rules amendment 29.02.2008 are listed by the Finance Dept) — details **not verified** (PDFs not opened).
- Structure (verified from India Code): ss.1-2 title/definitions; s.3 Fiscal Policy Statement to be laid before Legislature; s.4 disclosures; s.5 fiscal management principles; s.6 measures to enforce compliance; s.7 rules; s.8 rules laid before Legislature; s.9 good-faith protection; s.10 civil court jurisdiction barred; s.11 other laws not barred; s.12 removal of difficulties. Chapters I-V (Preliminary; Budget Transparency; Fiscal Management Principles; Enforcement; Miscellaneous).
- Specific numeric targets (deficit/debt percentages): **not verified**.
- Litigation relevance: Low directly. s.10 bars civil court jurisdiction over the Act's matters (per section heading). Relevant as background when Finance refuses concurrence citing fiscal discipline.
- Marathi gist: राजकोषीय तूट/कर्ज मर्यादा, पारदर्शकता व अर्थसंकल्पीय शिस्त यासाठीचा कायदा; मध्यम मुदतीचे राजकोषीय धोरण विधिमंडळात ठेवणे बंधनकारक. s.10 नुसार दिवाणी न्यायालयाचे अधिकारक्षेत्र वर्जित (कलम शीर्षकानुसार; मूळ कायदा पाहावा).
