## 8.2 Maharashtra Government Rules of Business (कार्यनियमावली)
- Official GAD listing [LISTING]: "महाराष्ट्र शासन कार्यनियमावली पहिली अनुसूची (दिनांक ३० ऑगस्ट २०१८ पर्यंत सुधारल्याप्रमाणे)" (First Schedule: allocation of subjects to departments) https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s398e6f17209029f4ae6dc9d88ec8eac2c/uploads/2023/06/2023060573.pdf
- GAD Rules page: https://gad.maharashtra.gov.in/e0-a4-a8-e0-a4-bf-e0-a4-af-e0-a4-ae/ ; the Rules of Business main text and Instructions are **not** listed on it.
- 2026 development (SECONDARY source only, not acceptable as authority): a news/education site (Drishti IAS, 20 Aug 2026) reports that "Maharashtra Government Rules of Business, 2026" were notified replacing the 1975 rules, with CM override power (Rule 13(5)), circulation decisions, Chief Secretary advisory role, 22 categories to be submitted to the Governor. No notification number or GAD reference was given and **no official copy was found**. Treat as **not verified** until the GAD notification is obtained. (Another search result mentioned a "Fourth Amendment Rules 2026" on a compliance blog: also not verified.)
- Litigation relevance: Rules of Business govern which department/Secretary decides, consultation with Law & Judiciary and Finance Departments (mandatory consultation entries), and who instructs the Advocate General; verify against the current (2026) rules.
- Marathi gist: कार्यनियमावली 1975 बदलून 2026 ची नवीन नियमावली लागू झाल्याचे वृत्त आहे, पण अधिकृत अधिसूचना पडताळली नाही; वापरण्यापूर्वी सा.प्र.वि.कडून मूळ अधिसूचना घ्यावी.
