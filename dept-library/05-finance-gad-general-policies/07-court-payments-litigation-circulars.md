## 7.2 Example of department-level circular on timely handling of court cases (Soil & Water Conservation Dept)
- Title (MR): न्यायालयीन प्रकरणात विहित कालमर्यादेत कार्यवाही करण्याबाबत
- No./date: न्यायाप्र-2025/प्र.क्र.477/जल-4 ; 21 August 2025 ; Soil and Water Conservation Department (not Finance/GAD; shows the typical pattern)
- URL [FETCHED]: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s301a0683665f38d8e5e567b3b15ca98bf/uploads/2025/11/20251128968616801.pdf
- Key provisions (summary, verify against original): collect writ petition papers from Govt Pleader office within 2 days; authorise at least a sub-divisional officer to represent; draft affidavit with Govt Pleader, obtain approvals and file within time; officer must attend hearings (prior permission from senior if not); only an officer of at least Sub-Divisional Officer rank may file an affidavit; obtain judgments promptly; after an adverse ruling obtain Govt Pleader's written opinion on review/appeal quickly; implement within time; district officers responsible so that no contempt petition is filed; regional review of cases every 15 days; disciplinary action for lapses.
- Verbatim (as extracted): "मा. न्यायालयात किमान उपविभागीय जलसंधारण अधिकारी यांनीच शपथपत्र दाखल करावे." (OCR text may vary)
- Litigation relevance: Useful model of departmental standing instructions on affidavits, appearance, contempt prevention. The file number prefix "न्यायाप्र" (court case) matches the reference style cited in your instruction.
- Marathi gist: न्यायालयीन प्रकरणांत 2 दिवसांत कागदपत्रे घेणे, शासकीय अभिवक्त्यासोबत शपथपत्राचा मसुदा करून वेळेत दाखल करणे, सुनावणीस उपस्थित राहणे, प्रतिकूल निकालानंतर अभिवक्त्याचे मत घेणे, अवमान याचिका टाळणे, दर 15 दिवसांनी आढावा आणि विलंबास शिस्तभंग.
