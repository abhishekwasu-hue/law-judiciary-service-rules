## 1.1 Delegation of Financial Powers: "वित्तीय अधिकार नियम पुस्तिका 1978" (Financial Powers Rules Handbook 1978)
- Title (EN): Maharashtra Delegation of Financial Powers Rules / Financial Powers Rule Book, 1978 (official site title: "Financial Authority Rules Handbook 1978")
- Title (MR): वित्तीय अधिकार नियम पुस्तिका 1978
- Issuing dept: Finance Department (वित्त विभाग), Govt. of Maharashtra
- GR/notification no. and date of the 1978 rules: **not verified** (the PDF content could not be opened; CDN file not on fetch allowlist)
- Official page: https://finance.maharashtra.gov.in/publication/%e0%a4%b5%e0%a4%bf%e0%a4%a4%e0%a5%8d%e0%a4%a4%e0%a5%80%e0%a4%af-%e0%a4%85%e0%a4%a7%e0%a4%bf%e0%a4%95%e0%a4%be%e0%a4%b0-%e0%a4%a8%e0%a4%bf%e0%a4%af%e0%a4%ae-%e0%a4%aa%e0%a5%81%e0%a4%b8%e0%a5%8d/  (page last updated 19.06.2025 per site)
- Official PDF (6 MB, Marathi) [LISTING]: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s349d4b2faeb4b7b9e745775793141e2b2/uploads/2025/04/202504152065519229.pdf
- Status: in force as the consolidated handbook on the Finance Dept website (upload path suggests April 2025); latest revision of individual sanction limits: **not verified**.
- Key provisions (summary: background knowledge, verify against original; NOT read from the PDF):
  1. The handbook fixes which authority (Government/Secretary, Head of Department, Head of Regional Office, DDO) may sanction which kind of expenditure and up to what monetary limit.
  2. Powers are arranged in Parts/sub-divisions by subject (establishment, contingencies, purchase, repairs, write-off, etc.). Administrative departments exercise powers subject to Finance Dept consultation where the limit is exceeded or the matter is not covered.
  3. The handbook is amended by Finance Dept GRs; always check the latest amending GR before relying on a rupee limit.
  4. Rule of thumb for notes: cite the exact Part / sub-division / serial no. of the handbook for any sanction authority, and the amending GR date.
- Litigation relevance: Legal expenses (fees of Special Government Pleader, counsel, court fees, payment of costs/compensation ordered by a court) and write-off or payment of decretal amounts must be traced to a delegated limit; beyond it, Finance concurrence is needed. Notes forwarding Court-ordered payments should quote the sanctioning authority and limit.
- Marathi gist (सारांश, मूळ GR शी पडताळा): वित्तीय अधिकार नियम पुस्तिका 1978 नुसार शासन, विभागप्रमुख, कार्यालयप्रमुख व आहरण-संवितरण अधिकारी यांना कोणत्या बाबीवर किती खर्च मंजूर करण्याचे अधिकार आहेत ते निश्चित केले आहे. मर्यादा ओलांडल्यास वित्त विभागाची सहमती आवश्यक असते. नोटमध्ये नियम/भाग/अनुक्रमांक व सुधारणा शासन निर्णयाचा दिनांक नमूद करावा.

## 1.4 Practical drafting note (background, not verified)
Before approaching Finance Dept for concurrence, the note should state: (a) rule/DFPR serial under which the power is claimed; (b) why it exceeds the delegation; (c) court order/direction with date; (d) budget head and availability of provision.
