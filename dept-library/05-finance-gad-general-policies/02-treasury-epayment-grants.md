## 2.1 Virtual Personal Deposit Account (VPDA) system for grants-in-aid
- Title (EN): Introduction of Virtual Personal Deposit Account (VPDA) system and procedure for quick disbursement and control of grants-in-aid, and management of unspent balances with DDOs and non-government implementing agencies
- Title (MR): सहायक अनुदानाच्या जलद संवितरण व संनियंत्रणासाठी आहरण व संवितरण अधिकारी (आभासी वैयक्तिक ठेव लेखा – VPDA) ...
- GR no. (as extracted from OCR, may contain errors): संकीर्ण 2023/प्र.क्र.110/2023/कोषा प्रशा-5; Date: 14 February 2024; Dept: Finance Department
- Official URL [FETCHED]: https://gr.maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/Government%20Resolutions/English/202402141720506705...pdf
- Status: in force (no supersession seen); pilot then roll-out to all DDOs/agencies.
- Key provisions (summary of fetched text, verify against original):
  1. Applies to grants-in-aid drawn from the Consolidated Fund under object heads 27, 31, 33, 35, 50, 53 and others notified by Finance.
  2. Funds moved by book adjustment (nil bill) into the administrator's VPDA; payments to beneficiaries go through e-payment.
  3. System (VPDAS) to be built by NIC Pune and integrated with BEAMS, Bill Portal, TreasuryNet and Arthwahini.
  4. DDO is VPDA Administrator for government offices; administrators are notified by GR, authorised by Mahalekhapal (L&A) and given a code by the Directorate of Accounts and Treasuries.
  5. Grants must be spent within one year of drawal; utilisation certificates within the same period (clause 17.1).
  6. Unspent amounts must promptly be returned to the Consolidated Fund by challan (clause 19.1).
  7. Daily cash book, monthly reconciliation with treasury; subject to internal and statutory audit.
- Quoted (as OCR text): "एक वर्षाची कालमर्यादा"; "तत्परतेने राज्याच्या एकत्रित निधीमध्ये तात्काळ भरणा करणे अनिवार्य राहील" (OCR-garbled original; check the PDF).
- Litigation relevance: Grants to court-linked bodies (e.g., Legal Services Authority, Judicial Academy) and unspent-grant disputes; also explains how funds for court-ordered payments flow when a body is a grantee.
- Marathi gist: सहायक अनुदान आता VPDA मार्फत थेट लाभार्थ्यांना ई-पेमेंटने द्यायचे; अनुदान एका वर्षात खर्च करणे व उपयोगिता प्रमाणपत्र देणे बंधनकारक; न वापरलेली रक्कम तात्काळ शासन खात्यात जमा करणे आवश्यक.
