# 05. Creation / abolition of posts, aakritibandh (revised staffing pattern), Finance concurrence

## 5.1 Revised aakritibandh of GAD (Khuddh) establishment
- Title (EN): Revised staffing pattern (आकृतिबंध) of posts on the establishment of General Administration Department (Khuddh) (covers Parliamentary Affairs Dept and related posts)
- Title (MR): सामान्य प्रशासन विभाग (खुद्द) च्या आस्थापनेवरील पदांचा सुधारित आकृतिबंध
- GR no. (as extracted): आढावा-1616/प्र.क्र.213/19अ ; date as extracted: 17 January 2023 (the CDN path is 2024/05, so upload was May 2024; the extracted date should be checked) ; Dept: General Administration Department
- URL [FETCHED]: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s398e6f17209029f4ae6dc9d88ec8eac2c/uploads/2024/05/202405101237735203.pdf
- Status: in force as per fetch; any later modification not verified.
- Key provisions (summary of fetched text, verify against original):
  1. Approves 610 regular posts (Table A) and 142 posts to be filled through outsourcing (Table B); body text also gives a total of 753 including one revived Deputy Director post under Planning Dept (figures not reconciled in the GR).
  2. Baseline: 14 May 2004 structure, used because the 2012 Mantralaya fire destroyed establishment records.
  3. 99 posts (11 drivers, 88 multi-purpose workers) declared dead; posts equal to serving staff are kept as supernumerary and abolished as staff leave; outsourcing fills needs within the sanctioned limit.
  4. 50 posts in Table D are abolished/cancelled; seven posts transferred from the IT Directorate.
  5. Recruitment permitted under Finance Dept GRs dated 30 September and 31 October 2022 (details of those GRs not verified).
  6. Issued after Sub-Committee (22 June 2022) and High-Level Committee of Secretaries (16 September 2022) approval, with Finance Dept concurrence.
  7. Future changes need approval of senior officers named in the GR.
- Litigation relevance: Illustrates the standard path for any post-related proposal from a court establishment: aakritibandh approval, committee of Secretaries, then Finance concurrence; supernumerary posts and outsourcing as the substitute for abolished posts.
- Marathi gist: पदांचा आकृतिबंध उपसमिती व सचिव समितीच्या मान्यतेनंतर वित्त विभागाच्या सहमतीने निश्चित होतो. निरुपयोगी पदे व्यपगत/अधिसंख्य ठरवून कंत्राटी (आउटसोर्सिंग) पद्धतीने गरज भागवली जाते.
