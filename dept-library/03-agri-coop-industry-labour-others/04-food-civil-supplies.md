## 1. National Food Security Act, 2013 — implementation in Maharashtra (राष्ट्रीय अन्न सुरक्षा कायदा, २०१३)
- **Issuing dept:** Food, Civil Supplies & Consumer Protection (Act is central).
- **GR refs given on the page (numbers not given):** AAY identification — GR 17-07-2013; Priority Household (PHH) — GR 17-12-2013; district-wise target revision — GR 18-02-2022 **[OPENED page]**.
- **Official URL:** https://mahafood.gov.in/en/national-food-security-act-2013/ ; allocation orders https://mahafood.gov.in/en/document-category/national-food-security-act-2013/
- **Status:** In force; free foodgrains from 01-01-2024 to 31-12-2028 under GoI notification dated 04-12-2023 (as stated on the page).
- **Key provisions (summary — verify against original):**
  1. Implemented in Maharashtra since 01-02-2014; about 62.3% of population eligible (76.32% rural, 45.34% urban); target 700.17 lakh beneficiaries (25,05,300 AAY families; 5.92 crore PHH persons).
  2. AAY households: 35 kg foodgrain per card per month; PHH: 5 kg per person per month.
  3. PHH includes BPL households not in AAY and APL (saffron) card-holders within annual family income Rs 44,000 (rural) / Rs 59,000 (urban) (per page; limits' current validity **not verified**).
  4. Prices were Rs 3 / 2 / 1 per kg (rice/wheat/coarse grain) until made free from 1-01-2024.
- **Why it matters for litigation:** Writs for ration-card cancellation/denial, exclusion errors, eligibility income limits, fair-price-shop licence actions.
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** महाराष्ट्रात १ फेब्रुवारी २०१४ पासून अन्न सुरक्षा कायदा लागू; अंत्योदय कुटुंबास दरमहा ३५ किलो व प्राधान्य कुटुंबातील प्रति व्यक्ती ५ किलो धान्य. प्राधान्य गटासाठी उत्पन्न मर्यादा (ग्रामीण रु. ४४,०००/शहरी रु. ५९,०००) — सध्याची वैधता पडताळावी.

## 3. Ration cards — categories, e-ration card (GRs of 21-02-2023 and 16-05-2023)
- **Official URL:** https://mahafood.gov.in/en/ration-cards/ **[OPENED]**
- **Key provisions (page summary — verify):** categories AAY, PHH, APL Farmer, Non-Priority Household (NPH) (RCMS counts of January 2024 shown: total 2,24,52,050 cards); e-ration cards issued now, with prescribed fee for some categories under GR dated 21-02-2023; GR dated 16-05-2023 extended online e-ration card access to NPH, AAY, PHH and the APL-farmer state scheme in suicide-affected districts; earlier three colour-coded cards under 05-05-1999 scheme.
