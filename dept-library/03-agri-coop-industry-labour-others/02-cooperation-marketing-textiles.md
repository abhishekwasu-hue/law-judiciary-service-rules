## 1. Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 (महाराष्ट्र सहकारी संस्था अधिनियम, १९६०)
- **Act No.:** Mah. XXIV of 1961 (the India Code listing also shows a "40 of 1961" typographical variant — use XXIV of 1961). Dept: Cooperation, Marketing & Textiles.
- **Official URLs:** India Code PDF (as amended to 6 June 2025) https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15836/3/maharashtra.pdf **[OPENED, first 100,000 of 109,646 chars]**; Dept portal copy https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s392c5ad73d34a8c18d276a0f0b60ea745/uploads/2025/04/202504241793532905.pdf [SEEN]; Registrar's site https://sahakarayukta.maharashtra.gov.in/SITE/PDF/Rules_Acts_Bylaws/Maharashtra_Co-operative_Societies_Act_1960.pdf [SEEN]; handle page https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/15836
- **Status:** In force, amended (latest text seen: up to 6-06-2025). A Jan Vishwas decriminalisation draft (Jan 2026) is pending — see item 5.
- **Key provisions (section numbers as they appear in the text I read — summary — verify against original):**
  1. s.9 — registration of society and by-laws (generally within two months; deemed-registration provision).
  2. ss.22–24 — admission to membership (NOT s.9).
  3. s.73 — Committee, powers and functions (heading only seen); s.78A — supersession of Committee / removal of member; s.79 returns; s.79A Government directions in public interest.
  4. s.83 — Registrar's inquiry; ss.85–86 costs of inquiry and recovery; s.88 — damages/assessment against delinquent promoters, officers.
  5. s.91 — settlement of "disputes" (touching constitution, management, business of a society) by Co-operative Court; s.91A Co-operative Courts; s.93 transfer/suspension of proceedings; s.96 decision; s.97 appeal (to Maharashtra State Co-operative Appellate Court, s.149); s.98 recovery of awards; s.101 recovery of certain sums as arrears of land revenue.
  6. s.102–103 winding up/liquidator; s.110 disposal of surplus assets; s.152 appeals; s.154 revision by State Government/Registrar.
- **Why it matters for litigation:** Core law for societies' employees (credit societies, sugar factories, DCC banks, housing societies): service disputes of society employees are "disputes" under s.91 (Co-operative Court) vs Industrial/Labour Court jurisdiction; supersession (s.78A) and inquiry writs; recovery certificates (s.101).
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** राज्यातील सहकारी संस्थांची नोंदणी, व्यवस्थापन, लेखापरीक्षण, चौकशी व वाद-निवारण यासाठीचा मूलभूत कायदा. कलम ९१ अन्वये संस्थेच्या कामकाजाशी संबंधित वाद सहकारी न्यायालयात; कलम ७८अ अन्वये समिती बरखास्ती; कलम १५२/१५४ अन्वये अपील/पुनरीक्षण.

## 4. Draft amendments to MCS Act under "Maharashtra Jan Vishwas" Bill (21-01-2026)
- **Issuing dept:** Cooperation (draft notice inviting objections till 06-02-2026).
- **Official URLs:** Draft https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s392c5ad73d34a8c18d276a0f0b60ea745/uploads/2026/01/2026012285555908.pdf **[OPENED]**; invitation notice https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s392c5ad73d34a8c18d276a0f0b60ea745/uploads/2026/01/20260122116804359.pdf [SEEN]
- **Status:** **Draft / proposed** — enactment status not verified.
- **Key provisions (summary — verify against original):** s.146 replaced (many offences deemed BNS offences, e.g. BNS s.318 for deception via s.145(2); some retain imprisonment, others reduced, e.g. election corrupt practice fine up to Rs 5,000); s.147 replaced with monetary penalties (e.g. up to Rs 20,000 for improper accounts; Rs 100/day capped Rs 10,000 for housing societies not supplying documents); new ss.147A/147B — adjudication by officer not below Assistant Registrar, appeal to officer not below Joint Registrar within 60 days, disposal in six months; s.73CA(1)(viii) disqualification trigger changed to conviction under s.146.
- **Why it matters:** If enacted, shifts prosecutions to departmental adjudication; affects disqualification of committee members.
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** जनविश्वास विधेयकांतर्गत सहकार कायद्यातील कलम १४६/१४७ सुधारणा प्रस्तावित — काही गुन्ह्यांऐवजी आर्थिक दंड, सहायक निबंधकांकडे निर्णय व सह-निबंधकांकडे अपील. हा मसुदा असून अंतिम स्थिती पडताळावी.
