# PART A — MARATHI LANGUAGE (मराठी भाषा विभाग) — official-language compliance

## A1. Maharashtra Official Languages Act, 1964 (महाराष्ट्र राजभाषा अधिनियम, १९६४)
- **Act No.:** Mah. V of 1965 (11-01-1965); amended 1996 (Mah. 2), 2015 (Mah. 36) and 2021 (Mah. 14) per the Law & Judiciary Dept copy (text as on 4-02-2022) **[OPENED]**. (India Code handle shows act-year 1965 vs title 1964.)
- **Official URLs:** https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s36a4cbdaedcbda0fa8ddc7ea32073c475/uploads/2025/01/202501311876730648.pdf ; https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s36a4cbdaedcbda0fa8ddc7ea32073c475/uploads/2025/02/202502091579818192.pdf ; India Code https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/15763 (also lists Official Languages Rules 1966 dated 30-04-1966 and Amendment Act 2021 dated 16-07-2021 — PDFs not opened).
- **Status:** In force as amended in 2021.
- **Key provisions (summary — verify against original):**
  1. s.1A — Marathi is the official language of the State.
  2. s.3 — English may continue in the State Legislature along with Hindi and Marathi.
  3. s.4 — Marathi is the language for official purposes under Art. 345 (exceptions by rules; Hindi permitted for some); the 2021 amendment lists official purposes incl. public correspondence, notices, forms, signboards, seals, electronic services.
  4. s.5 — Marathi to be used in Bills/amendments, State Acts and Ordinances, and orders, rules, regulations and bye-laws; English allowed where special circumstances or technical reasons so require.
  5. ss.5A–5I (2021) — Marathi Language Officers, State and District Marathi Language Committees, proactive publication, Government directions; s.5I — on non-implementation the Disciplinary Authority may initiate disciplinary action under MCS (Discipline & Appeal) Rules 1979 and report it to the committees. No criminal penalty.
  6. s.6 — rule-making (rules laid before Legislature for 30 days). The text I read contains no section on courts/High Court language.
- **Why it matters for litigation:** Basis for demanding Marathi in official correspondence, and for disciplinary exposure of officials; language of courts is governed separately (Constitution Art. 348 and State orders — **not verified here**). Proposals in Marathi from district courts: Marathi usage in subordinate courts is supported by GR of 01-06-2005 (inspection of courts for Marathi use; cited in the Language Directorate's RTI booklet, **not opened**).
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** मराठी ही राज्याची राजभाषा (कलम १अ); शासकीय कामकाज, कायदे/आदेश मराठीतून (कलम ४, ५). २०२१ च्या सुधारणेने मराठी भाषा अधिकारी व राज्य/जिल्हा मराठी भाषा समित्या; अंमलबजावणी न केल्यास शिस्तभंग कारवाईची तरतूद.

## A2. Marathi Language Policy 2023 (मराठी भाषा धोरण २०२३)
- **Issuing dept:** Marathi Language Dept; drafted by Language Advisory Committee chaired by Laxmikant Deshmukh.
- **GR No./Date:** the document's own resolution number/date was illegible in the text read — **not verified**.
- **Official URL:** https://directorate.marathi.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/language-policy.pdf **[OPENED]**
- **Status:** Policy document hosted by the Language Directorate (uploaded 2025/07); adoption date not verified.
- **Key provisions (summary — verify against original):** Marathi as language of knowledge and employment; Marathi in administration; full use of Marathi in taluka and district courts and a proposal to seek official-language status for Marathi in the High Court; banks to transact in Marathi; Marathi-medium higher education incl. law, medicine, engineering; a Marathi university; localisation in computing; a Marathi Language Development Authority (modelled on Karnataka's); Devanagari script retained; 25-year horizon to 2047 with a five-year action plan.
- **Why it matters:** Foundation for the courts' language proposals (Marathi in the High Court; Marathi translation of judgments/ court records) that may reach Law & Judiciary.
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** राज्याच्या मराठी भाषा धोरणात प्रशासन, न्यायालये (तालुका/जिल्हा न्यायालयात पूर्ण वापर; उच्च न्यायालयात मराठीस दर्जाची मागणी), बँका, शिक्षण व तंत्रज्ञानात मराठी वापराच्या शिफारशी; २०४७ पर्यंतचा आराखडा.
