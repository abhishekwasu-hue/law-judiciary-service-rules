## 3. PMKSY — Per Drop More Crop (Micro-irrigation / drip & sprinkler subsidy)
- **Issuing dept:** Agriculture Dept.
- **GR No./Date:** reference printed in OCR as "0618/प्र.क्र.191/18/14-अे" (prefix garbled), dated 19-06-2018 **[OPENED]** (2018-19 annual action plan).
- **Official URL:** https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in/Farmer/PDF/50.pdf
- **Status:** 2018-19 plan GR; scheme continues but later-year rates **not verified**. Also see a Soil & Water Conservation Dept GR on PMKSY-WDC 2.0 (25-06-2025, कें पुयो-2025/प्र.क्र.74/जल-8) [OPENED] — a watershed component, DDO designation only: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s301a0683665f38d8e5e567b3b15ca98bf/uploads/2025/01/202508041471640523.pdf
- **Key provisions (summary — verify against original):**
  1. 2018-19 plan Rs 78,941 lakh (Centre Rs 48,000 lakh, State Rs 30,941 lakh); Centre:State sharing 60:40.
  2. Subsidy 55% for small/marginal farmers and 45% for others.
  3. Ceiling 5 ha per beneficiary; area given micro-irrigation subsidy in previous 10 years is ineligible.
  4. Applies in 34 districts (not Mumbai / Mumbai Suburban).
  5. At least 50% to small/marginal, 30% to women, balance SC/ST earmarks.
- **Why it matters:** Recovery of subsidy / blacklisting of dealers; disputes on ineligible 10-year repeat benefit.
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** प्रधानमंत्री कृषी सिंचन योजनेतील "प्रति थेंब अधिक पीक" (ठिबक/तुषार) घटकासाठी २०१८-१९ चा आराखडा रु. ७८,९४१ लाख; अल्प/अत्यल्प भूधारकांना ५५% व इतरांना ४५% अनुदान; कमाल ५ हेक्टर मर्यादा. नंतरच्या वर्षांचे दर मूळ GR मध्ये पडताळावे.

## 4. Bhausaheb Fundkar Falbag Lagwad Yojana (भाऊसाहेब फुंडकर फळबाग लागवड योजना) — orchard / santra
- **Issuing dept:** Agriculture Dept.
- **GR:** guidelines cite GR dated 06-07-2018 (economic norms); GR number not stated in guideline text **[OPENED]**.
- **Official URL:** https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in/Farmer/PDF/Scheme-BhausahebPhundkarFalbaagLagvadYojana.pdf
- **Status:** Operational per MahaDBT scheme page (current-year status not verified).
- **Key provisions (summary — verify against original):**
  1. Covers multi-year fruit crops incl. **orange (santra, incl. Indo-Israel technology), sweet lime (mosambi), Kagdi lime**, mango, pomegranate, guava, cashew, coconut, etc.
  2. Subsidy over 3 years in 50:30:20 ratio (illustration in guideline: mango 10x10 m max Rs 53,561/ha).
  3. Land limits: Konkan 0.10–10 ha; other regions 0.20–6 ha.
  4. Survival norm: 80% in year 1, 90% in year 2; drip irrigation mandatory and to be retained 7 years; soil testing compulsory for citrus.
  5. Planting season 1 May–30 Nov; plant within 75 days of pre-sanction; lottery if applications exceed target; women quota 30%.
  6. Violations -> recovery of subsidy and legal action.
- **Why it matters:** Recovery/penalty disputes where survival norm not met; eligibility of tenant/joint-holder farmers (land-title questions).
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** संत्रा, मोसंबी, आंबा, डाळिंब इत्यादी फळपिकांच्या लागवडीसाठी तीन वर्षांत (५०:३०:२०) अनुदान देणारी योजना. पहिल्या वर्षी ८०% व दुसऱ्या वर्षी ९०% झाडे जगणे बंधनकारक; ठिबक सिंचन ७ वर्षे ठेवणे आवश्यक; नियमभंगास अनुदान वसुली.

## 7. Maharashtra Agricultural Produce Marketing (Development and Regulation) Act, 1963 (APMC Act) (महाराष्ट्र कृषि उत्पन्न पणन (विकास व विनियमन) अधिनियम, १९६३)
- **Act No.:** Mah. XX of 1964 (assent 5 May 1964 per India Code). Administered by Cooperation, Marketing & Textiles Dept (Marketing) — placed here per requested scope.
- **Official URL (India Code, text current to 25-03-2026, latest amendment Mah. 52 of 2025 in force 13-10-2025):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15835/1/apmc_2026_updated.pdf **[OPENED, partial: first 100,000 of 112,285 characters]** ; handle page https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/15835
- **Status:** In force, as amended.
- **Key provisions (summary — verify against original):**
  1. s.5 — market areas, principal and sub-markets notified by Director.
  2. s.6 — licence needed to trade/act as commission agent etc. in a market area; retail sales and agriculturist's own-produce sales exempt; each committee must reserve space for direct sale by farmers to consumers.
  3. s.12 — Market Committee is a body corporate, deemed local authority; Director's prior permission for property transactions above limits.
  4. s.13 — constitution: 15 agriculturist members elected via agricultural credit/multipurpose co-op societies and village panchayats; 2 traders/commission agents; 1 hamal/weighman; Deputy Registrar non-voting member. s.14 — 5-year term.
  5. Chapter I-B (s.5D) — direct marketing, private markets, farmer-consumer markets licensed by Director; appeal to State Government within 30 days.
  6. s.31 (fees/adat), s.32 (borrowing), s.44 (amalgamation/division) — headings only seen.
- **Why it matters:** Market-fee/licence disputes, Market Committee staff service matters (committee employees), election/supersession writs, appeals to Government under the Act.
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** कृषि उत्पन्न बाजार समित्यांची स्थापना, परवाने, निवडणुका व शुल्काचे नियमन करणारा कायदा (१९६३ चा अधिनियम, १९६४ चा महा. २०). कलम ५डी अंतर्गत थेट पणन/खाजगी बाजार परवाने; आदेशाविरुद्ध ३० दिवसांत शासनाकडे अपील.
