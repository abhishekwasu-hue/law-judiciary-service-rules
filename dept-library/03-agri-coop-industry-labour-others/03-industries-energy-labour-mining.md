## A. INDUSTRIAL POLICIES

### 1. Maharashtra Industries, Investment and Services Policy 2025 (महाराष्ट्र उद्योग, गुंतवणूक व सेवा धोरण २०२५)
- **GR No./Date:** शासन निर्णय क्र. मऔधो-2023/प्र.क्र.187/उद्योग-2, dated 31-12-2025 (Cabinet approval 11-12-2025) **[OPENED]**. Dept: Industries, Energy, Labour & Mining.
- **Official URL:** https://industry.maharashtra.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-12/202512311332497910.pdf
- **Status:** In force — 5 years or until replaced; annual review.
- **Key provisions:**
  1. Replaces the 2019 policy/package scheme; Industries Dept renamed Industries, Investment & Services Dept; Directorate of Industries -> Commissionerate of Industries; separate MSME and Services commissionerates to be created.
  2. "Invest Maharashtra" Section-8 company (Rs 3,000 crore budget provision); about Rs 1,18,275 crore approved for incentives.
  3. Unit classes: micro up to Rs 2.5 cr, small Rs 2.5–25 cr, medium Rs 25–125 cr (plant & machinery); LSI above Rs 125 cr FCI; mega/ultra-mega by zone.
  4. Zone-based incentive caps (percent of fixed capital investment); stamp duty waiver, SGST-based promotion subsidy, electricity duty exemption, 50% EPF reimbursement (5 years, Group D and below areas).
  5. **Labour provisions:** central inspection system; pre-registration inspection/renewal under Shops & Establishments Act 2017 removed; single consolidated online return; restrictions on night shifts for women removed.
  6. Government has final authority on interpretation; English text prevails for detailed interpretation of Marathi provisions.
- **Why it matters for litigation:** Incentive-eligibility and subsidy-denial writs; interplay of labour relaxations with statutory duties; employees of incentivised units.
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** दि. ३१-१२-२०२५ चे नवे औद्योगिक धोरण (५ वर्षे): उद्योग विभागाचे नामांतर, इन्व्हेस्ट महाराष्ट्र कंपनी, विभागनिहाय प्रोत्साहने; कामगार तरतुदींत केंद्रीय तपासणी प्रणाली, एकत्रित वार्षिक विवरण व महिलांच्या रात्रपाळीवरील निर्बंध शिथिल.

### 3. Maharashtra State Export Promotion Policy 2023

## B. LABOUR LAW — CODES AND STATE ACTS

### 5. The four Labour Codes — in force from 21-11-2025 (Wages 2019, IR 2020, Social Security 2020, OSH 2020)
- **Issuing authority:** Government of India, Ministry of Labour & Employment; State rules/notifications by Maharashtra Labour Dept.
- **Official URLs:** PIB release https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2192463&reg=3&lang=2 **[OPENED]**; PIB backgrounder https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251121701501.pdf [SEEN]; Labour Ministry FAQs (as on 16-03-2026) https://www.labour.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/03/a4ccf4c6d97c4f1f36a6d83f8c64213d.pdf [SEEN]; Industrial Relations Code 2020 (No. 35 of 2020) on Maharashtra Labour site https://labour.maharashtra.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-04/the-industrial-relations-code-2020-no-35-of-2020.pdf **[OPENED]**; Maharashtra "Implementation of Labour Codes" (gazette, 7788 GI/2025) https://labour.maharashtra.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-11/implementation-of-labour-codes.pdf [SEEN; site blocks fetch — contents not verified]; Maharashtra Code on Wages Rules 2025 (India Code) https://www.indiacode.nic.in/ViewFileUploaded?path=AC_MH_166_1466_00045_00045_1755493418369%2Frulesindividualfile%2F&file=code_on_wages_rules_2025_english.pdf [SEEN; not opened]; Code on Wages 2019 = Act No. 29 of 2019 (8-08-2019) per India Code https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/21456
- **Status:** Codes in force from 21-11-2025 (PIB). The PIB note says relevant provisions of existing Acts, rules and notifications continue during transition. State rule finalisation status: not verified.
- **Key provisions (summary — verify against original):**
  1. Subsume 29 central labour laws into four Codes (PIB).
  2. IR Code — industrial dispute definition s.2(q) includes individual dismissal/retrenchment disputes; strike s.2(zk) includes concerted casual leave by 50% or more workers; standing orders apply at 300+ workers (s.28); two-member Industrial Tribunals (judicial + administrative member, s.44); conciliation report within 45 days (s.53); pending ID Act cases transfer to Tribunals (s.51); registered trade unions deemed registered (s.9(4)).
  3. Fixed-term employees: gratuity after one year (PIB); single registration/licence/return; Inspector-cum-Facilitator model.
  4. Contract labour regulation is subsumed in the OSH Code (central CLRA 1970 repealed) — **not verified from an opened official source**.
- **Why it matters:** Pending ID Act/ BIR/ MRTU matters; forum change (Tribunals); Maharashtra's own labour statutes remain unless repugnant/ repealed — check each.
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** २१ नोव्हेंबर २०२५ पासून चार कामगार संहिता (वेतन, औद्योगिक संबंध, सामाजिक सुरक्षा, व्यावसायिक सुरक्षा) लागू; २९ केंद्रीय कायदे समाविष्ट. औद्योगिक संबंध संहितेत दोन सदस्यीय न्यायाधिकरण, ३०० कामगारांवरील आस्थापनांना स्थायी आदेश. राज्य नियमांची स्थिती पडताळावी.

### 6. Maharashtra Shops and Establishments (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 2017
- **Act No.:** Mah. Act No. 61 of 2017 (enactment date 7-09-2017 per India Code).
- **Official URL:** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19710/1/shops_and_establishments.pdf **[OPENED]**; handle https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/19710
- **Status:** In force (labour-code interplay: not verified; 2025 policy removes pre-registration inspection/renewal requirements).
- **Key provisions:** Applies mainly to 10+ workers (s.1(3)); s.7 intimation and s.36A Marathi name board apply to under-10 establishments. s.6 registration within 60 days, certificate valid up to 10 years; s.12 max 9 hrs/day, 48 hrs/week; s.14 spread-over 10.5 hrs; s.15 overtime at twice rate, cap 125 hrs per 3 months; s.13 women's hours 7:00–21:30, night work only with consent and safeguards; s.16 weekly rest 24 hrs; s.18 casual leave 8 days, earned leave 1 per 20 days (accumulation to 45); 8 paid festival holidays; s.36A Marathi (Devanagari) name boards, Marathi lettering first and not smaller; penalties s.29 (up to Rs 1 lakh + Rs 2,000/day), s.30 (injury/death: up to 6 months and Rs 2–5 lakh), s.32 complaint within 3 months by Facilitator. The text I read has no termination-notice / appeal provisions.
- **Why it matters:** Wage/overtime/leave claims of private-sector staff; Marathi signboard enforcement linkage with Marathi language policy (file 05); closure/ retrenchment questions now under IR Code.
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** १०+ कामगारांच्या दुकाने/आस्थापनांसाठी नोंदणी, कामाचे तास (९/४८), अधिक वेळेचा दुप्पट दर, महिलांसाठी रात्रपाळी संमतीने व सुरक्षेसह, रजा तरतुदी; कलम ३६अ नुसार देवनागरी मराठी नामफलक आवश्यक.

### 7. Maharashtra Recognition of Trade Unions and Prevention of Unfair Labour Practices Act, 1971 (MRTU & PULP)
- **Act No.:** Mah. I of 1972 (text on India Code as on 31-12-2025 per page).
- **Official URLs:** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15922/1/recognition_of_trade.pdf **[OPENED]**; also https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15922/5/the_maharashtra_recognitio.pdf [SEEN]; Labour Dept copy https://mahakamgar.maharashtra.gov.in/Upload/PDF/the-mahashtra-recognition-act-1971.pdf [SEEN]; Law & Judiciary copy https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s36a4cbdaedcbda0fa8ddc7ea32073c475/uploads/2025/02/20250210992122160.pdf [SEEN]
- **Status:** In force; post-Code transition: not verified.
- **Key provisions:** ss.26–27 unfair labour practices (Schedules II, III, IV); Industrial Court decides ULP complaints except Sch. IV item 1 (Labour Court) (s.5(d), s.7); complaint within 90 days (s.28); court aims to decide within 6 months; s.28(7) bars challenge in civil/criminal court; s.30 remedies — cease and desist, reinstatement, back wages, compensation; recognition: 50+ employees, 30% membership for six months (ss.10–11), one recognised union per undertaking; appeals against Labour Court criminal orders to Industrial Court within 30 days (s.42).
- **Why it matters:** Primary state forum for unfair labour practice (badli/casual/ temporary for years, victimisation); writs from Industrial/Labour Court orders (Art. 226/227).
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** कामगार संघटना मान्यता व अनुचित कामगार प्रथा (ULP) प्रतिबंध कायदा. ULP तक्रार ९० दिवसांत औद्योगिक न्यायालयात; वर्षानुवर्षे बदली/हंगामी ठेवणे अनुचित प्रथा (अनुसूची IV); ५०+ कामगारांच्या उपक्रमात ३०% सदस्यत्वावर मान्यता.

### 9. Maharashtra Mathadi, Hamal and Other Manual Workers (Regulation of Employment and Welfare) Act, 1969
- **Act No.:** Mah. XXX of 1969 (as printed in the page header "1969 : Mah. XXX" of the India Code PDF, seen in search result; confirm against the PDF before citing).
- **Official URLs:** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15841/1/hamal_and_other_manual.pdf **[OPENED; text as on 6-10-2025, amended through Mah. 25 of 2025]**; Law & Judiciary copy https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s36a4cbdaedcbda0fa8ddc7ea32073c475/uploads/2025/02/20250210867448100.pdf [SEEN]
- **Status:** In force.
- **Key provisions:** applies to scheduled employments (loading/unloading in markets, docks, railway yards, etc.); "unprotected worker" aged 18–65 (s.2(11)); Mathadi Boards (s.6) with equal employer/worker members; schemes can fix wages, guaranteed wages, leave, maternity benefit; Board may determine dues after hearing and recover as land revenue (s.13); offences tried by Labour Court, appeal to Industrial Court in 30 days (ss.17, 17C); fine up to Rs 5 lakh + Rs 5,000/day (s.27).
- **Why it matters:** Hamal/mathadi levy and wage disputes; Mathadi Board employee matters; APMC market labour.
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** माथाडी, हमाल व इतर बिगर-संरक्षित कामगारांचे नियमन व कल्याण; माथाडी मंडळे, योजना, वेतन, लेव्ही वसुली (जमीन महसुलाप्रमाणे). गुन्ह्यांची सुनावणी कामगार न्यायालयात; अपील ३० दिवसांत औद्योगिक न्यायालयात.

### 10. Maharashtra Labour Welfare Fund Act, 1953
- **Act No.:** Bombay Act XL of 1953 ("1953 : XL" on India Code PDF); text as on 6-05-2024.
- **Official URL:** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19974/1/the_maharashtra_labour_welfare_fund_act.pdf **[OPENED]**
- **Status:** In force.
- **Key provisions:** half-yearly contributions for employees on roll on 30 June/31 Dec (employee Rs 25, employer three times; State may raise employee rate once every 3 years by up to 30%); payment by 15 July/15 January; interest 1.5% per month first 3 months then 2%; unpaid accumulations (unpaid wages/gratuity for 3 years) deposited with Board and treated as abandoned property; claims within 4 years; covers factories, motor transport undertakings, shops/establishments with five or more persons.
- **Why it matters:** Unpaid wages/gratuity claims after 3 years; employer default interest.
- **मराठी गोषवारा:** महाराष्ट्र कामगार कल्याण निधीसाठी सहामाही अंशदान (कामगार रु. २५, मालकाचा तिप्पट); तीन वर्षे न दिलेले वेतन/उपदान मंडळाकडे जमा; सहामाही मुदती १५ जुलै/१५ जानेवारी.
