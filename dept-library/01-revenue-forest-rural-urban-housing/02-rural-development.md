## 1. Maharashtra Zilla Parishads and Panchayat Samitis Act, 1961 — महाराष्ट्र जिल्हा परिषदा व पंचायत समित्या अधिनियम, १९६१
- **Type / No.:** Maharashtra Act V of 1962 (the PRS archive file is "1962MH5"); India Code record lists the Act with its service rules.
- **Issuing dept:** Rural Development Dept.
- **Official URL (PDF):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19697/1/zila.pdf (alt: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19697/1/maharashtra_zilla_parishads.pdf)
- **Status:** In force; recent change: ZP Jalgaon's official page lists a **2026 Ordinance amending the Act to repeal the appeal provision** (posted 05/01/2026) and matching amendments to the **ZP Rules, 1962 / Panchayat Samiti Rules, 1962** (repeal of appeal provisions; classification/alphabetical ordering of candidates) [V-OFF-LIST]; India Code also notes an April 2026 amendment on nominated members of ZPs/PSs. Contents of the PDFs not read; GR/ordinance numbers not verified.
- **Official URLs for the 2026 items (listing only):**
  - Ordinance, repeal of appeal provision: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30aa08441cd4526b972bb3451d9f8e4ea/uploads/2026/01/20260123996923147.pdf
  - ZP Rules 1962 / PS Rules 1962 — repeal of appeal provisions: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30aa08441cd4526b972bb3451d9f8e4ea/uploads/2026/01/202601232016932876.pdf
  - ZP Rules / PS Rules — classification & alphabetical order of candidates: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s30aa08441cd4526b972bb3451d9f8e4ea/uploads/2026/01/202601231701855131.pdf
- **Key provisions (summary — verify):** three-tier structure (ZP–Panchayat Samiti–Gram Panchayat via the VP Act); CEO and department heads; standing/subject committees; ZP Chief Executive Officer is State's officer (ZP Satara page lists CEO-related rules 94–100); staffing in "district services" with State control; powers to dissolve/supersede; State Election Commission role (2022 amendment bills moved SEC powers to Govt — contents not read).
- **Litigation relevance:** Writs on ZP member disqualification, ZP/PS elections, ZP employee service (via the Act's rules), teachers' transfers and recoveries; election-reservation (OBC) matters.
- **मराठी सारांश:** जिल्हा परिषद व पंचायत समिती यांची रचना, अधिकार व कर्मचारी नियंत्रण यांचा मूळ कायदा. २०२६ मध्ये अपील तरतूद रद्द करणारा अध्यादेश/नियम सुधारणा (जळगाव जि.प. संकेतस्थळ). मजकूर वाचलेला नाही.

## 2. Maharashtra Zilla Parishads District Services Rules (Entry into Service / Service Admission) — जिल्हा परिषद जिल्हा सेवा (सेवा प्रवेश) नियम
- **Type / No.:** Series of statutory rules under the ZP Act; the India Code record for the Act lists these amendments (title/date as shown there; rule numbers not shown): Entry into Service (Amendment) Rules — 19-08-2005, 05-12-2009, 10-06-2014; **07-03-2024** (Service Admission (Amendment) Rules — Junior Assistant); **13-06-2024** (Entry into Service (Amendment) Rules); **12-11-2025** (Maharashtra District Council District Services (Amendment) Rules); also **12-01-2024** Maharashtra Development Services (Organization, Classification and Recruitment) (Amendment) Rules, 2024. [V-IC page: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/19697]
- **Official URL:** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/19697 (record page; individual PDF links not shown).
- **Status:** In force per India Code listing (Oct 2026 listing).
- **Key provisions (summary — verify):** eligibility, recruitment method, reservation and selection for Group C/D posts in ZPs; promotion channels; ZP-level recruitment procedure; Maharashtra Development Services rules govern Group A/B development-service posts (BDO etc.).
- **Litigation relevance:** Core for ZP employee writs (recruitment, eligibility, seniority, promotion quota). The Law & Judiciary section often receives ZP service proposals via these rules.
- **मराठी सारांश:** जिल्हा परिषद कर्मचाऱ्यांच्या भरतीचे व सेवा प्रवेशाचे नियम (२००५ ते २०२५ च्या सुधारणांसह). भरती, पात्रता, पदोन्नती व आरक्षण याबाबतच्या वादांत आधारभूत.

- **Type / No.:** Bombay Act III of 1959 (PRS archive "1959MH3"); India Code PDF.
- **Official URL (PDF):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19645/1/village.pdf
- **Status:** In force; amended (e.g., Notification — insertion of s.54-1A, 25 June 2014, listed on Raj Bhavan Maharashtra page, no PDF URL seen [V-OFF-LIST]).

## 5. Maharashtra Employment Guarantee Act, 1977; MGNREGA; Maharashtra Rural Employment Guarantee Scheme, 2006 — महाराष्ट्र रोजगार हमी अधिनियम, १९७७ व महात्मा गांधी राष्ट्रीय ग्रामीण रोजगार हमी योजना
- **Type / No.:** Maharashtra Act XX of 1978 (20 of 1978), enacted 3 Oct 1978 [V-IC]. State Scheme: "Maharashtra State Employment Guarantee Scheme, 2006" (applies from 26 Jan 2006; text read on ZP Ahmednagar site — notification number/date NOT stated there).
- **Official URL (PDF):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/22342/1/the_maharashtra_employment_guarantee_act%2c_1977_-_final.pdf ; scheme text page: https://nagarzp.gov.in/html_docs/MREGS.htm
- **Status:** Act in force. **Caveat:** whether the central MGNREGA 2005 has since been replaced/amended (I have a recollection of a December 2025 central law replacing MGNREGA — the "VB-G RAM G" Act — but could NOT verify it because the search quota ended; treat as unverified). Check the current central statute before citing.
- **Key provisions of the 2006 State Scheme (summary — verify):** 100 days' unskilled manual work per rural household; 90:10 centre–state sharing up to 100 days; job card valid 5 years; work within 15 days of application else unemployment allowance (25% for 30 days, then 50%); wages within 15 days; no contractors; work within 5 km (10% extra otherwise); priority to water conservation; worksite facilities and ex-gratia Rs 50,000 on death; sanction limits BPO Rs 5 lakh / Collector Rs 50 lakh / Divisional Commissioner Rs 75 lakh; social audit by gram sabhas.
- **Litigation relevance:** Writs on non-payment of wages/material dues, EGS worker employment claims, contractor liabilities, Gram Panchayat accountability.
- **मराठी सारांश:** ग्रामीण कुटुंबास १०० दिवस अकुशल रोजगाराची हमी, १५ दिवसांत काम न मिळाल्यास बेरोजगारी भत्ता, १५ दिवसांत मजुरी, ग्रामसभेतर्फे सामाजिक अंकेक्षण. केंद्रीय कायद्यातील ताज्या बदलांची पडताळणी आवश्यक.
