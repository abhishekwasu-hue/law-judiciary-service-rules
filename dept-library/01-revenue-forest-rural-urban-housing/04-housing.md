## 1. Maharashtra Rent Control Act, 1999 — महाराष्ट्र भाडे नियंत्रण अधिनियम, १९९९
- **Type / No.:** Maharashtra Act XVIII of 2000 (cited "Mah. XVIII of 2000"), titled 1999 [V-IC; page shows notifications up to 29-05-2025].
- **Issuing dept:** Housing Dept (State statute).
- **Official URL (PDF):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15817/1/the_maharashtra_rent_control_act_1999.pdf
- **Status:** In force (replaced the Bombay Rents, Hotel and Lodging House Rates Control Act, 1947).
- **Key provisions (summary — verify section numbers):** standard rent and permitted increases; statutory protection against eviction with listed grounds for recovery of possession; licensee deemed tenant (s.15 line of cases — verify); exclusion of Govt/local-authority premises and certain high-rent/bank/PSU lettings (s.3 — verify); jurisdiction of Small Causes/Civil courts; revision (a recent HC decision on revisional authority under **s.44** is on a judgments mirror — not read); Supreme Court has decided Rent Act cases in Jan 2024 and May 2019 (judgments seen on sci.gov.in: https://main.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2015/32224/32224_2015_5_1501_49939_Judgement_30-Jan-2024.pdf ; https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2018/42914/42914_2018_12_1502_14483_Judgement_08-May-2019.pdf — case names not read).
- **Litigation relevance:** Thousands of landlord–tenant writs/revisions; Govt premises and State's own leases (government as landlord/tenant) fall under exclusions — check applicability in any proposal involving Govt premises.
- **मराठी सारांश:** भाडेकरू-घरमालक संबंध, प्रमाणित भाडे, बेदखलीची कारणे, परवानाधारक भाडेकरू मानला जाण्याची तरतूद आणि शासकीय जागांना सवलत यांचा कायदा. उच्च न्यायालयातील भाडे-वादांचा आधार.

## 2. Maharashtra Housing and Area Development Act, 1976 (MHADA Act) — महाराष्ट्र गृहनिर्माण व क्षेत्रविकास अधिनियम, १९७६
- **Type / No.:** Maharashtra Act XXVIII of 1977 (28 of 1977), enacted 9 May 1977 [V-IC].
- **Official URL (PDF):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/16704/1/maharashtra_housing_and.pdf ; MHADA subordinate instruments (official): Disposal of Land Regulations 1982 — https://mhada.gov.in/sites/default/files/Disposal-of-Land-Regulations-1982.pdf ; MHAD (Disposal of Land) Rules 1981 — https://www.mhada.gov.in/sites/default/files/5-The-MHAD-%28-Disposal-of-Land%29-Rules-1981.pdf ; DCR extract — https://mhada.gov.in/sites/default/files/DCR_1.pdf (contents not read).
- **Status:** In force; 2026 Amendment Bill 63 of 2026 listed on Legislature/PRS site — content not read.
- **Key provisions (summary — verify):** constitution of MHADA and Boards (Mumbai Building Repair & Reconstruction Board — MBRRB); **Chapter VIII**: repair and reconstruction of dilapidated cessed buildings (about 13,091 cessed buildings in Mumbai Island City per MHADA page [V-OFF]); **s.79A** — owner first, tenants second, MHADA acquisition third (redevelopment of cessed buildings); **s.91A** — NOC/acquisition of properties where owner/developer abandons redevelopment (compensation to owner: higher of 25% of Ready Reckoner land rate or 15% of built-up sale area; tenants rehabilitated free; MBRRB issued notices to 91 owners/developers, 14 proposals to Govt, 7 approved — per MHADA page, undated); s.77(a-1) for incomplete projects; Regulation 33(7) of DCPR 2034 — redevelopment with consent of 51% tenants/occupants in exchange for additional FSI.
  - MHADA pages: https://mhada.gov.in/sites/default/files/79A%20information.pdf ; https://mhada.gov.in/sites/default/files/New%20Section%2091A%20information.pdf (official PDFs, excerpts read; no dates/numbers).
- **Litigation relevance:** Cessed-building tenants' and owners' writs, developer default, acquisition under 79A/91A, lottery/allotment disputes.
- **मराठी सारांश:** म्हाडाची रचना, जीर्ण उपकरप्राप्त इमारतींची दुरुस्ती-पुनर्बांधणी (प्रकरण ८), कलम ७९अ (मालक→भाडेकरू→म्हाडा), कलम ९१अ (अपूर्ण पुनर्विकासात संपादन). मुंबईतील पुनर्विकास याचिकांत महत्त्वाचा.

## 3. Maharashtra Slum Areas (Improvement, Clearance and Redevelopment) Act, 1971 + Slum Rules 2019 (SRA) — महाराष्ट्र झोपडपट्टी क्षेत्र (सुधार, निर्मूलन व पुनर्विकास) अधिनियम, १९७१
- **Type / No.:** Maharashtra Act XXVIII of 1971 (28 of 1971), enacted 11 Aug 1971 [V-IC]. Amendment: Maharashtra Act 38 of 2018 w.e.f. 26.04.2017 and Slum Rules 2019 (per a publisher listing — [SEC], not verified).
- **Official URL (PDF):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15805/1/the_maharashtra_slum.pdf
- **Status:** In force. The Supreme Court judgment of 15 Dec 2023 on slum redevelopment (diary 29112/2021; https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2021/29112/29112_2021_14_1501_49102_Judgement_15-Dec-2023.pdf — case name/holding **not read**) is reported by news to have led Bombay HC to constitute a special bench to audit SRA law — [SEC], verify.
- **Key provisions (summary — verify):** declaration of slum areas; Slum Rehabilitation Authority (Chapter I-A); eligibility by photo-pass cut-off date (1.1.2000 / 2011 extension — verify); consent of ≥70% eligible occupants for rehabilitation schemes; competent authority; DCPR 33(10) incentives; the 25.03.2026 encroachment GR routes municipal-area colonies to MHADA/SRA (Revenue file item 3, clause 13).
- **Litigation relevance:** Eligibility, consent, developer-default and eviction writs; SC/HC scrutiny of SRA schemes.
- **मराठी सारांश:** झोपडपट्टी घोषणा, झोपडपट्टी पुनर्वसन प्राधिकरण (SRA), ७०% पात्र झोपडीधारकांची संमती, पात्रता निकष व विकासकांवरील कारवाई — हा कायदा. सर्वोच्च/उच्च न्यायालयाची छाननी सुरू (वृत्त).

## 4. Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (Central) and Maharashtra RERA Rules/Regulations, 2017 — स्थावर संपदा (विनियमन व विकास) अधिनियम, २०१६ व महाराष्ट्र नियम, २०१७
- **Type / No.:** Central Act 16 of 2016 (number from a secondary listing, India Code record page did not state number in the fetched text; "2016" confirmed) ; Maharashtra Real Estate (Regulation and Development) (Registration of real estate projects, Registration of real estate agents, rates of interest and disclosures on website) Rules, 2017 — notification date/no. **not verified**; MahaRERA General Regulations 2017 (listed by Casemine) [SEC].
- **Official URL:** India Code record https://indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/17773?locale=en (PDF link not seen). MahaRERA portal (maharera.maharashtra.gov.in) documents not opened.
- **Status:** In force; MahaRERA issues frequent orders/circulars (e.g., QR code on advertisements, Order 30 dated 03.06.2022, Circular 42 on SROs — titles seen only on a WIRC-ICAI mirror, not verified).
- **Key provisions (summary — verify):** mandatory registration of projects/agents; escrow of 70% of receipts; refund with interest/compensation (s.18); complaints before MahaRERA, appeals to Maharashtra Real Estate Appellate Tribunal; interplay with MOFA (RERA prevails for registered projects).
- **Litigation relevance:** Writs against MahaRERA orders, recovery warrants (Collector's role), redevelopment-project registration; Govt bodies (MHADA/SRA) as promoters.
- **मराठी सारांश:** गृहप्रकल्प नोंदणी, ७०% रक्कम स्वतंत्र खात्यात, परतावा व भरपाई, तक्रारींसाठी महारेरा व अपील प्राधिकरण — यांचा कायदा. महाराष्ट्र नियम २०१७; अधिसूचना क्रमांक पडताळलेला नाही.

## 5. Maharashtra Ownership Flats (Regulation of the promotion of construction, sale, management and transfer) Act, 1963 (MOFA) — महाराष्ट्र सदनिका मालकी (बांधकाम, विक्री, व्यवस्थापन व हस्तांतरण यांचे नियमन) अधिनियम, १९६३
- **Type / No.:** Maharashtra Act XLV of 1963 (45 of 1963), 16 Dec 1963 [V-IC].
- **Official URL (PDF):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15816/1/maharashtra_ownership_flats.pdf
- **Status:** In force for projects outside RERA coverage (partly overlapping with RERA).
- **Key provisions (summary — verify):** promoter's duties, registered agreement for sale, formation of co-operative society/company and conveyance to it (s.10, s.11), penalties.
- **Litigation relevance:** Conveyance/deemed conveyance and promoter-default disputes.
- **मराठी सारांश:** विकासकाची कर्तव्ये, नोंदणीकृत करारनामा, सोसायटी स्थापना व मानीव अभिहस्तांतरण यांचा कायदा.

- **Type / No.:** Maharashtra Act XV of 1971 (15 of 1971), 19 Feb 1971 [V-IC].
- **Official URL:** record page https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/15810?locale=en (PDF link not seen).
- **Status:** In force.

## 7. Maharashtra Co-operative Societies Act, 1960 (housing societies) — महाराष्ट्र सहकारी संस्था अधिनियम, १९६०
- **Type / No.:** Maharashtra Act XXIV of 1961 (PRS archive "1961MH24"; the India Code page shows mixed citations "40 of 1961"/"24 of 1961" — I use 24 of 1961, **verify**).
- **Issuing dept:** Co-operation, Marketing & Textiles Dept (housing societies' redevelopment/deemed conveyance interacts with Housing Dept).
- **Official URL (PDF):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15836/3/maharashtra.pdf
- **Status:** In force; amendments 2018–2026 (Ordinance 2024 etc. on PRS — not read).
- **Key provisions (summary — verify):** registration, management committee, Registrar's powers, **disputes → Co-operative Court (s.91)**, appeals to Co-operative Appellate Court, revision by State Govt; model bye-laws; redevelopment by housing societies (any Housing/Co-operation Dept GR on society redevelopment: not located, not verified).
- **Litigation relevance:** Society elections/management, redevelopment resolutions, deemed conveyance.
- **मराठी सारांश:** सहकारी गृहनिर्माण संस्थांची नोंदणी, व्यवस्थापन, तंटे (कलम ९१), सहकारी न्यायालय/अपील यांचा कायदा.
