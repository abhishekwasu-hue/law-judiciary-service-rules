## 1. Maharashtra Land Revenue Code, 1966 — महाराष्ट्र जमीन महसूल अधिनियम, १९६६
- **Type / No.:** Act — Maharashtra Act XLI of 1966 (41 of 1966) [Act no. seen on a secondary listing; short title/PDF on India Code]. Date of assent/commencement: not verified.
- **Issuing dept:** Revenue & Forest Dept (statute of State Legislature).
- **Official URL (PDF):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15974/1/the_maharashtra_land_revenue_code.pdf (alt: https://indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15974/3/MH1966.pdf)
- **Status:** In force, amended many times (a 2025 Amendment Bill No. 100 of 2025 appears on PRS/Legislature sites — content not read).
- **Key provisions (summary — verify against original):**
  - Land revenue administration; hierarchy of Talathi–Circle Officer–Tahsildar–Collector–Divisional Commissioner–State Govt.
  - Occupancy classes (Bhogwatdar Class-I / Class-II) and restrictions on transfer of Class-II land (the 2026 GR below builds on this).
  - Govt land vests in State; unauthorised occupation → penalty/summary eviction (the 25.03.2026 GR cites **s.53** for eviction notice).
  - Conversion/NA use: s.42 and 42A–42D (NA permission dispensed where land is in a sanctioned plan; instruments listed on the Chandrapur district page: s.42-B/42-C notification 15.04.2017; R&F circulars 23.05.2023, 13.03.2024, 29.01.2025 — see item 10).
  - Revenue appeals and revision (commonly s.247 appeals, s.257 State revision — verify section numbers).
  - Mamlatdars' Courts Act linkage and s.143 (R&F instruction of Nov 2025 on executing orders — see item 11).
- **Litigation relevance:** Foundational statute for Art. 226/227 writs on mutation entries, NA/conversion, encroachment/eviction, Class-II land transfer, revenue appeals (Revenue Tribunal / MRT). Staff matters (Talathi/Tahsildar service) fall under separate service rules.
- **मराठी सारांश:** हा जमीन महसुलाचा मूलभूत कायदा आहे. शासकीय जमिनीची मालकी, भोगवटादार वर्ग-१/२, अकृषिक परवानगी (कलम ४२ व ४२अ-ड), अतिक्रमण/निष्कासन (कलम ५३) तसेच महसुली अपील व पुनरीक्षण यांचे नियमन करतो. उच्च न्यायालयातील जमीन-विषयक रिट याचिकांत सर्वाधिक उद्धृत.

## 3. GR — Regularisation of residential encroachments on Government land up to 01.01.2011 — शासकीय जमिनीवरील दि. ०१/०१/२०११ पर्यंतची निवासी अतिक्रमणे नियमित करणे
- **Type / No.:** GR No. जमीन-2026/प्र.क्र.20/जमीन-1, dated **25 March 2026** (file no. R&FD-18063(63)/7/2026-MRF(DESKJ1)) — **[V-COPY]**: read from a news-channel-hosted PDF; official copy on gr.maharashtra.gov.in NOT located. Treat number/date as "as printed in the copy; verify".
- **Issuing dept:** Revenue & Forest Dept (जमीन-१ branch).
- **Official URL:** not found. Copy read (non-official): https://images.tv9marathi.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Government-GR-on-encrochement.pdf — *not added to pdf-list.tsv because it is not official*.
- **Status:** Current per the copy; application window stated as up to 31.12.2026.
- **Key provisions (summary — verify against original):**
  - Only existing, purely residential encroachments made on/before 01.01.2011 on Govt land (under Land Revenue Code); Mumbai City/Suburban excluded; individuals only; Indian citizens; land should be zoned residential (RP/DP/UDCPR).
  - One family–one benefit in the State (affidavit). Regularised as Bhogwatdar Class-II, non-transferable for 5 years, then convertible to Class-I.
  - Free up to 500 sq ft; 500–1500 sq ft excess at 10% of ready-reckoner (25% for commercial portion); >1500 sq ft not regularised.
  - Excluded: rivers/nallas/roads/forest/CRZ/landslide areas, reserved public-purpose land, land under court dispute (subject to outcome). Gairan land: landless labourers/SC/ST not individually regularised there — land may go to local body for PMAY housing.
  - Process: application via Gramsevak/ward officer/Chief Officer; committees (Tahsildar rural; SDO for B/C councils; Collector for corporations/A-class); public objections 15 days; decision within 90 days; rejection → eviction notice (s.53) within 30 days; quarterly review committees.
- **Litigation relevance:** Fresh policy likely to be cited in writs on demolition/eviction notices and regularisation refusals; interplay with gairan/grazing-land PIL directions and with the 12.07.2011 GR (item 4).
- **मराठी सारांश:** दि. ०१/०१/२०११ पर्यंतची, सध्या अस्तित्वात असलेली निवासी अतिक्रमणे ५०० चौ.फुटांपर्यंत मोफत व १५०० चौ.फुटांपर्यंत शुल्कासह नियमित करण्याची योजना; भोगवटादार वर्ग-२, ५ वर्षे हस्तांतरणबंदी, एक कुटुंब-एक लाभ; अर्जाची मुदत ३१.१२.२०२६. (अधिकृत प्रत पडताळलेली नाही.)

## 4. GR dated 12 July 2011 — Encroachments on Government/gairan land (after Jagpal Singh v. State of Punjab) — शासकीय/गायरान जमिनीवरील अतिक्रमणे, शासन निर्णय दि. १२/०७/२०११
- **Type / No.:** GR dated 12.07.2011; GR number NOT stated in the judgment read; issuing dept presumed Revenue & Forest — **[SEC]/[court-referenced]**.
- **Official URL:** not found. Referred to in *Vishwanath Bhavanaji Thorat v. State of Maharashtra*, W.P. 8945 of 2016, Bombay HC (Aurangabad), decided 26.03.2026 (read via a non-official judgments mirror; official judgment not located).
- **Status:** Referred to as operative in March 2026 judgment; whether superseded by item 3 for any category: not verified.
- **Key provisions (as described in the judgment — verify):** issued pursuant to *Jagpal Singh* (2011) 11 SCC 396; State's position: gairan encroachments cannot ordinarily be regularised, but the Supreme Court allowed an exception for landless labourers and SC/ST persons; HC quashed Collector's order of 16.03.2016 and directed fresh reasoned decision within 3 months, noting SC-landless petitioner in possession since 1978.
- **Litigation relevance:** Frequently cited in gairan/grazing-land eviction writs; Bombay HC has also flagged lakhs of encroachments on grazing land (news-reported PIL 204/2010 Bhuskate — not read).
- **मराठी सारांश:** गायरान/शासकीय जमिनीवरील अतिक्रमणे सर्वसाधारणपणे नियमित होत नाहीत; भूमिहीन अनुसूचित जाती/जमातीसाठी अपवाद. उच्च न्यायालयाने (मार्च २०२६) या शासन निर्णयाच्या चौकटीत जिल्हाधिकाऱ्यांना पुन्हा निर्णय घेण्यास सांगितले. शा.नि. क्रमांक पडताळलेला नाही.
