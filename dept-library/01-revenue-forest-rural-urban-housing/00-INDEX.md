## Verification legend (used in every file)
- **[V-IC]** Act number/year/enactment date read on the India Code record page this session.
- **[V-OFF]** read on an official page (e.g., MHADA).
- **[V-OFF-LIST]** title/PDF link seen on an official listing; PDF not opened (CDN PDFs not fetchable here); GR number/date therefore "not verified".
- **[V-COPY] / [V-NEWS] / [SEC]** read from non-official copy / news / secondary source — a lead, to be confirmed on gr.maharashtra.gov.in or the Gazette.
- All "key provisions" are *summary — verify against original*. Section numbers marked "verify" are from memory-level knowledge and not checked against the text.
