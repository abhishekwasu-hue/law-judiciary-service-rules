## 1. Maharashtra Regional and Town Planning Act, 1966 (MRTP Act) — महाराष्ट्र प्रादेशिक व नगर रचना अधिनियम, १९६६
- **Type / No.:** Maharashtra Act XXXVII of 1966 (37 of 1966), enacted 11 Jan 1967 per India Code [V-IC].
- **Issuing dept:** Urban Development Dept.
- **Official URL (PDF):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/16117/5/town.pdf (alt: https://indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/16117/3/the_maharashtra_regional_.pdf)
- **Status:** In force; very frequently amended (2015–2026 Bills on the PRS archive — not read).
- **Key provisions (summary — verify section numbers):** Regional Plans and Development Plans (preparation, sanction, modification — s.22, s.26–31, s.37 incl. 37(1AA) used to notify UDCPR); development permission (s.43–45); unauthorised development — penalty and removal (s.52–53); reservation/designation and acquisition — s.126 acquisition, **s.127 lapse of reservation** (notice and 12-month period), s.128; State's power of directions (s.154) — Chandrapur listing cites s.154 for building/control lines on highways (circular 18.12.2018).
- **Litigation relevance:** The commonest town-planning statute in Bombay HC writs: lapse of reservation, modification of DP, TDR, unauthorised construction, development-permission refusals, compensation for reservations.
- **मराठी सारांश:** विकास योजना, आरक्षण, बांधकाम परवानगी, अनधिकृत बांधकाम (कलम ५२-५३) व आरक्षित जमिनीचे संपादन/व्यपगत (कलम १२६-१२७) यांचा मूळ कायदा. नगर नियोजनाच्या रिट याचिकांत सर्वाधिक उद्धृत.

## 2. Unified Development Control and Promotion Regulations for Maharashtra State (UDCPR), 2020 — एकत्रित विकास नियंत्रण व प्रोत्साहन नियमावली (UDCPR), २०२०
- **Type / No.:** Notification under MRTP Act (s.37(1AA)-type sanction) dated **2 December 2020** [V-NEWS; Hitavada 6 Dec 2020]. Notification number: **not verified**. A "UDCPR-2020" notification and an "Updated UDCPR 30.01.25" (39.4 MB) consolidated version are listed on the PMRDA official page (https://www.pmrda.gov.in/?p=5594), PDF URLs not visible; Chandrapur's official page lists "UDCPR-2020" dated 30.01.2022 (that date is the page's, not the notification's).
- **Official URL:** none opened. Official listing: https://www.pmrda.gov.in/?p=5594 . (Non-official mirrors exist — not used.)
- **Status:** In force, amended; consolidation "updated up to 30.01.2025" per PMRDA page.
- **Key provisions (summary — verify):** one rulebook for all planning authorities in Maharashtra **except** Greater Mumbai (MCGM — DCPR 2034), MIDC, NAINA, JNPT, hill-station councils, eco-sensitive areas notified by MoEF and Lonavala (per Hitavada); FSI, premium FSI, TDR, setbacks, parking, affordable housing, cluster development, industrial-land change of use (e.g., Nagpur closed industrial units — 15% of ASR premium), metro-corridor FSI limits; the 25.03.2026 encroachment GR makes UDCPR zoning a condition for regularisation.
- **Litigation relevance:** Challenges to building permissions, FSI/TDR use, premium demands, modifications; interplay with DCPR 2034 in Mumbai.
- **मराठी सारांश:** मुंबई महापालिका क्षेत्र वगळता संपूर्ण महाराष्ट्रासाठी एकच विकास नियंत्रण नियमावली (२ डिसेंबर २०२०). FSI, TDR, प्रीमियम, पार्किंग, परवडणारी घरे यांच्या तरतुदी. अधिसूचना क्रमांक पडताळलेला नाही.

## 3. Development Control and Promotion Regulations for Greater Mumbai, 2034 (DCPR 2034) — बृहन्मुंबई विकास नियंत्रण व प्रोत्साहन नियमावली २०३४
- **Type / No.:** Sanctioned by State Govt (UD Dept) under MRTP; sanction date and notification number **not verified** in this session (only press headlines seen in search results — June 2018 "new DP to come into effect from Sept", Nov 2018 controversy over state "changes" — so 2018 is a lead, [SEC], not confirmed). Regulation 33(7) (cessed buildings), 33(9) (cluster), 33(5) (MHADA layouts), 33(10) (slums) are the housing-redevelopment pillars (see Housing file).
- **Official URL:** MCGM portal documents (e.g., Estates Dept circular on 33(12)(B): https://www.mcgm.gov.in/irj/go/km/docs/documents/Estates-Dept/33(12)(B)%20circular%20no%2030.pdf) — a circular, not the DCPR itself. DCPR text official URL not located.
- **Status:** In force (with modifications; "Notice under s.37(1AA)" modifications exist — contents not read).
- **Litigation relevance:** Redevelopment, FSI and amenity-space disputes in Mumbai; society and tenants' writs.
- **मराठी सारांश:** बृहन्मुंबईसाठी स्वतंत्र विकास नियंत्रण नियमावली (DCPR २०३४); पुनर्विकास नियम ३३(७), ३३(९), ३३(५) येथे आहेत. मंजुरी दिनांक/क्रमांक पडताळलेला नाही.

## 4. Maharashtra Municipal Corporations Act, 1949 (Bombay Provincial Municipal Corporations Act, 1949) — महाराष्ट्र महानगरपालिका अधिनियम, १९४९
- **Type / No.:** Bombay Act LIX of 1949 (59 of 1949), 29 Dec 1949 [V-IC].
- **Official URL:** record page https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/16071 (PDF link not seen).
- **Status:** In force; powers of State Election Commission shifted to State Govt by 2022 amendments (LA Bill 5 of 2022 on PRS — not read).
- **Key provisions (summary — verify):** constitution and powers of corporations other than Mumbai; Commissioner's powers; municipal servants and service conditions; property tax; building regulation and dangerous-building removal; taxation; appeals to Tribunals.
- **Litigation relevance:** Property-tax, encroachment/demolition, municipal employee service and pension writs (municipal-employee service writs often turn on the Corporation's own service regulations).
- **मराठी सारांश:** मुंबई वगळता महानगरपालिकांच्या कारभाराचा कायदा — आयुक्तांचे अधिकार, कर आकारणी, कर्मचारी सेवा, धोकादायक इमारती व अतिक्रमण.

## 5. Mumbai Municipal Corporation Act, 1888 — मुंबई महानगरपालिका अधिनियम, १८८८
- **Type / No.:** Bombay Act III of 1888 (3 of 1888), 14 Sep 1888 [V-IC].
- **Official URL:** record page https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/16119 (PDF link not seen).
- **Status:** In force; amended.
- **Key provisions (summary — verify):** structure of BMC; **s.354** dangerous buildings (cited on MHADA's cessed-building page); building and property-tax provisions; employee service provisions (BMC service regulations).
- **Litigation relevance:** Dangerous-building notices, property tax, hawkers/encroachment, BMC employees' service writs.
- **मराठी सारांश:** मुंबई महापालिकेची रचना, कर, धोकादायक इमारती (कलम ३५४) व कर्मचारी सेवा यांचा कायदा.

## 6. Maharashtra Municipal Councils, Nagar Panchayats and Industrial Townships Act, 1965 — महाराष्ट्र नगरपरिषदा, नगरपंचायती व औद्योगिक नगरी अधिनियम, १९६५
- **Type / No.:** Maharashtra Act XL of 1965 (40 of 1965), 10 Sep 1965 [V-IC].
- **Official URL (PDF):** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/16073/3/maharashtra_municipa.pdf
- **Status:** In force; amended 2020–2026 (several Ordinances/Bills on PRS — not read).
- **Key provisions (summary — verify):** Chief Officer and councillors; state-cadre services for councils (Maharashtra Municipal Councils, Nagar Panchayats and Industrial Townships Services); taxation; disqualification of councillors; State's supervisory/dissolution powers.
- **Litigation relevance:** Council employee writs (cadre, pay, pension, compassionate appointment), councillor disqualification, tax and encroachment cases.
- **मराठी सारांश:** नगरपरिषद/नगरपंचायतींचा कारभार, मुख्याधिकारी, राज्य संवर्ग सेवा, करआकारणी व नगरसेवक अपात्रता यांचा कायदा.
