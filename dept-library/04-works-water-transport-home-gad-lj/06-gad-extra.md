### G1. Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015 (Mah. Act XXXI of 2015)
- MR: महाराष्ट्र लोकसेवा हक्क अधिनियम, २०१५
- Ref: Gazette header (tool-read, legacy-font garbled): Mah. Act No. XXXI of 2015, dated 21 August 2015; s.1 - deemed in force from 28 April 2015 (date of the earlier Ordinance). Issuer: GAD (administered via Aaple Sarkar / RTS Commission).
- URL: https://maharashtra.gov.in/Site/Upload/Pdf/Maharashtra_Right_to_public_services_Act_2015.pdf ; GAD copy (Marathi/English): https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s398e6f17209029f4ae6dc9d88ec8eac2c/uploads/2023/06/2023060444.pdf
- Status: in force.
- Key provisions (summary - verify): notified services delivered within a notified time limit; designated officer, first and second appellate authorities; third appeal/jurisdiction to the State Right to Services Commission (1 Chief Commissioner + 6 Commissioners, HQ Mumbai, offices at six division HQs - per maharashtra.gov.in page); Commission has civil-court-type powers; penalty up to Rs 5,000 per case on defaulting officer (per portal page - amount not legible in the PDF read); departmental action for negligence. Sections numbering not reliably read (garbled font).
- Why for litigation: delay/refusal of a notified service (e.g., certificates, ST/caste related, service book copies) is appealable under this Act; writ petitions often get disposed with a direction to use RTS remedies; officers face penalty.
- मराठी: अधिसूचित सेवा ठराविक मुदतीत देणे बंधनकारक; विलंब/नकार झाल्यास प्रथम व द्वितीय अपील, त्यानंतर राज्य सेवा हक्क आयोगाकडे अपील; दोषी अधिकाऱ्यास प्रति प्रकरण कमाल रु.५,००० दंड (पोर्टलनुसार).
