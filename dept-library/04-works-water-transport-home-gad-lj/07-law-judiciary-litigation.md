## PART A - Department orientation (VERIFIED from lj.maharashtra.gov.in FAQ / Objectives pages)
- Functions (Objectives page): (1) monitor/supervise Government litigation in all courts; (2) legal advice to Government/departments on legal & legislative matters; (3) administer Government Pleader offices (district, High Court, Supreme Court), Charity Commissioner, Registrar of Firms, Administrator General & Official Trustee; (4) appoint law officers, special counsel, special public prosecutors; (5) set up courts, provide infrastructure, appoint subordinate-court judges in consultation with the High Court.
- Head: Remembrancer of Legal Affairs (RLA) who is ex-officio Principal Secretary (FAQ). Heads of departments may approach PS & RLA directly only in matters pending in court where State/officers are parties; other references go through the administrative department, which decides whether to seek RLA's opinion. If a matter is likely to go to Cabinet the referring note should say so (FAQ).
- Regional split (FAQ): litigation from Nagpur and Amravati divisions is first handled by the Joint/Deputy Secretary, Law & Judiciary Dept at Nagpur; from the Aurangabad Bench area by the equivalent officers at Aurangabad.
- Branch structure (Performance Budget, data c. 2014-2016 - old): "A" Branch opinion wing + criminal litigation (acquittal appeals, enhancement of sentence); "E" Branch city litigation & conveyancing (HC original/appellate side, City Civil Court, Small Causes); "M" Branch mofussil civil litigation (excluding Vidarbha/Marathwada); Nagpur and Aurangabad branches under a Joint Secretary; "B" Branch drafting.
- Advocate General: appointed under Art. 165 of the Constitution, holds office during pleasure of Government (lj site, Advocate General page).

## PART C - LITIGATION-HANDLING INSTRUCTIONS (consolidated; each line traced)

C1. Who handles what (FAQ, VERIFIED): Mumbai - main department branches; Nagpur & Amravati division matters first at Nagpur office (Jt/Dy Secretary); Aurangabad Bench matters at Aurangabad office.
C2. Adverse judgments (FAQ, VERIFIED): when a decision is wholly/partly against the State, the Government Pleader must obtain the uncertified copy of judgment/decree and report on appeal feasibility to the Collector/officer concerned and to RLA / Jt-Dy Secretary; the report must state when certified copies were applied for, expected, and actually delivered. (Also Rule 14(9)(h) per Yavatmal disclosure: apply for certified copy on day of decision; communicate result to administrative dept with brief grounds in important cases.)
C3. Concessions: no concession (including bail) without prior permission of competent officer/RLA; oral permission to be confirmed in writing within seven days (Rule 14(9)(d), per Yavatmal disclosure).
C4. Money decrees and stays: pursue Government recoveries with speed; ensure decrees against the State are satisfied within time fixed by CPC s.82; where a stay affects Government interest, immediately seek security (Rule 14(9)(i), 14(3)(c)).
C5. Reporting: periodic progress reports to RLA / Jt-Dy Secretary Nagpur-Aurangabad; special reports on unjust penalties, grossly irregular conduct of lower courts/officers, ultra vires laws, policy points (Rule 14(9)(g),(k)).
C6. Government servants: head of department to be told once a final order is passed against a Government servant (Rule 14(5)(x)).
C7. Criminal side: s.321 CrPC withdrawal only with Home Dept sanction through DM/CP; acquittal appeals in HC without further instructions only where Central Government directs and case was investigated by DSPE/central agency (Rule 14(4)(b), 14(5)(v)).
C8. Briefing: AG distributes briefs among GPs, AGPs and panel counsel; one junior counsel may be engaged subject to approval rules (Advocate General page, VERIFIED). Rule 15(1): one court per counsel per day unless reasons recorded.
C9. Opinion route: department -> (administrative dept decides) -> RLA; direct approach only in pending court matters (FAQ).
C10. Compliance with court orders / contempt / filing appeals within limitation: the specific official GR(s) were NOT located in this run. Candidates to check: litigation-policy GRs (L6), GR 2021 (L7), service-matter GR 2017 (L8). Do not cite any 'limitation GR' number until confirmed. Statutory limitation periods (Limitation Act 1963) are central law - verify independently.
C11. Finance linkage: pension-related litigation guidelines (L10); fee payment to law officers (L3, L4).
