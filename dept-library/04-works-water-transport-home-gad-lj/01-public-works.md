### P4. Manual of Financial Powers 1978 (Part I, Section I) and Finance Dept Resolution DFP-1089/(56)/Viniyam, 6 Nov 1990
- Official PWD RTI-manual PDFs: Appendix C: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ea80421fc06a2ed5c7adc1018e0026df/uploads/2025/03/20250315153249931.pdf ; Part I Section I: .../20250315246123765.pdf (not opened); Manual I "Particulars of organisation of PWD": .../202503151466635165.pdf (not opened); "Functioning of PWD at Government level": .../20250315467567731.pdf (server error).
- READ (Appendix C, summary - verify): Finance Dept Resolution DFP-1089/(56)/Viniyam, 6 Nov 1990 revises powers under the Bombay Financial Rules 1959 on the Committee on Delegation of Administrative and Financial Powers; revised powers form part of Manual of Financial Powers 1978 Part I Section I; sanctions must follow standards, be by the empowered officer (Secretary in administrative departments), be copied to audit officer and Finance Dept, and cite Manual serial no., resolution no./date and Annexure item no.; existing more liberal powers continue; monetary limits not to be exceeded; periodic review by administrative departments. Earlier refs: GAD circular DCN-1075/O&M 9-4-1975; Finance circular DFP-1076/1392/896/GEN-5 14-2-1977; GAD circular DLP-1088/12 28-7-1988.
- Why: validity of sanction orders.
- मराठी: आर्थिक अधिकार नियमपुस्तिका १९७८ व वित्त विभागाचा ६ नोव्हेंबर १९९० चा निर्णय - मंजुरी आदेशात अनुक्रमांक व निर्णयाचा संदर्भ देणे आवश्यक.
