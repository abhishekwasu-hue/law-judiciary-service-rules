## A. Caste certificate and validity - statute, rules, amendment

### 1. Maharashtra SC, ST, De-notified Tribes (Vimukta Jatis), Nomadic Tribes, OBC and Special Backward Category (Regulation of Issuance and Verification of) Caste Certificate Act, 2000
- **Marathi:** महाराष्ट्र अनुसूचित जाती, अनुसूचित जमाती, विमुक्त जाती, भटक्या जमाती, इतर मागासवर्ग व विशेष मागास प्रवर्ग (जातीच्या प्रमाणपत्राचे विनियमन व पडताळणी) अधिनियम, २०००
- **Act no.:** Maharashtra Act No. XXIII of 2001 [V-text]; President's assent 16-05-2001, published 23-05-2001, in force 18-10-2001 (per text read - verify). Dept: Tribal Development (India Code lists TDD) / now SJSA administers.
- **URLs:** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/19293 ; Law & Judiciary text "as modified up to 5 Feb 2016": https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s36a4cbdaedcbda0fa8ddc7ea32073c475/uploads/2025/02/20250210318435.pdf ; SJSA copy (URL composed from SJSA listing page; file not individually opened): https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3b090409688550f3cc93f4ed88ec6cafb/uploads/2025/01/2025011333462527.pdf
- **Status:** In force; the text read **does not include later amendments** (Ord. 17 of 2018 surfaced on PRS; 2026 Amendment Bill - see item 3).
- **Key provisions [V-text] (summary - verify against original):**
  - s. 4: only the Competent Authority may issue caste certificates; certificates of any other authority are invalid; a certificate needs verification and a **validity certificate** from the Scrutiny Committee (s. 6).
  - s. 5: appeal against rejection within 30 days to Appellate Authority; decision within 3 months.
  - s. 7: Scrutiny Committee may cancel and confiscate a fraudulently obtained certificate after hearing; order final subject to Art. 226 writ.
  - s. 8: burden of proving caste/tribe claim is on the claimant.
  - s. 9: authorities have civil-court powers (summons, affidavits).
  - s. 10: on cancellation - debarment from institution / **discharge from employment**, benefits withdrawn; scholarships recoverable as arrears of land revenue; qualifications obtained on the strength of the certificate cancelled; election deemed terminated.
  - s. 11-12: offences (6 months-2 years RI and/or fine Rs 2,000-20,000), cognizable and non-bailable, summary trial; prosecution on complaint of Scrutiny Committee; s. 13 penalty for issuing false certificates (Govt sanction); s. 15 bar on civil courts.
- **Why it matters for litigation:** the backbone of every caste-validity service writ - termination for invalid claims, protection/supernumerary posts, burden of proof, limitation of delay in verification, Art. 226 as the only challenge route.
- **मराठी सारांश:** जात प्रमाणपत्र केवळ सक्षम प्राधिकाऱ्याने द्यावे; त्याची पडताळणी व वैधता प्रमाणपत्र छाननी समितीकडून; जात सिद्ध करण्याची जबाबदारी दावेदाराची; अवैध ठरल्यास सेवेतून कमी करणे/लाभ काढून घेणे व शिक्षेची तरतूद (कलम १०-१२); समितीच्या आदेशाविरुद्ध अनुच्छेद २२६ नुसार उच्च न्यायालयात दाद.

### 3. Caste Certificate (Amendment) Bill, 2026 - L.A. Bill No. LXI of 2026
- **Status:** Introduced in Legislative Assembly **09-07-2026** [V-text of a PRS-hosted copy of the Gazette English translation - PRS is not an official site; used only to read the Bill]. The Maharashtra Legislature site lists it under Assembly Bills "passed by the Legislative Council" (listing title seen, not opened): https://mls.org.in/council-bill/2026/विधानसभेची सन २०२६ ची विधानपरिषदेने संमत केलेली विधेयके/वि. स. वि. क्र. ६१/वि. स. वि. क्र. ६१   - English.pdf . **Assent, Act number and commencement date: NOT verified** - treat as pending until the Gazette notification is seen.
- **Key provisions of the Bill (summary - verify against original):**
  - Defines "fake" (never issued by the authority) and "false" (obtained by fraud/false information) caste and validity certificates.
  - Scrutiny Committee may review its own validity orders in specified cases after hearing.
  - New **Appellate Authority**: appeals within 90 days, decided within 3 months; final subject to writ to High Court.
  - Competent Authority/Scrutiny Committee must file FIRs; courts take cognizance on complaint/police report; officers failing to file FIR liable to Rs 20,000 penalty and disciplinary action.
  - Institutions must withdraw benefits within 30 days after cancellation and exhaustion of appeal.
  - Fines raised to Rs 20,000-50,000; BNSS replaces CrPC references.
- **Why it matters:** will change forum/limitation for validity challenges (appeal instead of direct writ) and make employer action time-bound.
- **मराठी:** बनावट व खोट्या जात/वैधता प्रमाणपत्रांवर कारवाईसाठी २०२६ चे सुधारणा विधेयक - नवीन अपील प्राधिकारी (९० दिवस), एफआयआर बंधनकारक, ३० दिवसांत लाभ काढणे, वाढीव दंड. कायदा म्हणून अंमलात आल्याची खात्री राजपत्रावरून करावी.

## B. Maratha / SEBC / Kunbi

### 6. Maharashtra State Reservation for Socially and Educationally Backward Classes Act, 2024
- **Marathi:** महाराष्ट्र राज्य सामाजिक व शैक्षणिकदृष्ट्या मागास वर्ग आरक्षण अधिनियम, २०२४
- **Act no.:** Maharashtra Act No. XVI of 2024 [V-text via PRS-hosted English translation; PRS says translation not independently verified]. Gazetted after Governor's assent on **26-02-2024** (as stated in the text); in force from publication (s. 1(2)).
- **Official URLs:** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/19785 ; PDF https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19785/1/the_maharashtra_state_reservation_for_socially_and_educationally_backward_classes_act,_2024.pdf (seen; robots-blocked). University copy seen: https://www.sndt.ac.in/pdf/special-cell/admission/2024/sebc-reservation-act-2024.pdf
- **Status:** In force on its face; **court-challenge status (Bombay HC / Supreme Court) NOT verified.** Repeals the 2018 SEBC Act (Mah. LXII of 2018) (s. 20).
- **Key provisions (summary - verify against original):**
  - s. 3: Maratha community specified as an SEBC.
  - s. 4: applies to direct recruitment to public posts (excluding super-specialty posts, transfer/deputation, appointments under 45 days, single isolated posts) and admissions incl. private aided/unaided institutions (minority Art. 30(1) institutions excluded); OBC reservation under 2001/2006 Acts unaffected.
  - s. 5(1): **10%** of seats and 10% of direct-recruitment posts reserved; proviso excludes Scheduled-Area posts reserved for ST; s. 5(2): creamy layer excluded.
  - s. 6: SEBC candidates selected on merit do not count against reserved quota.
  - s. 8: unfilled reserved vacancies carried forward up to 5 years.
  - s. 10: wilful contravention punishable (up to 90 days / Rs 25,000, sanction required); s. 14: admissions/appointments in breach are void; s. 18 saving for processes begun earlier.
  - EWS is not mentioned in the text read.
- **Why it matters:** roster/carry-forward, interplay with the 10% EWS and 50% ceiling, validity of appointments made before commencement (s. 18), SEBC candidates' certificates (s. 12).
- **मराठी सारांश:** मराठा समाजाला सामाजिक व शैक्षणिकदृष्ट्या मागास वर्ग (SEBC) म्हणून शैक्षणिक प्रवेश व थेट सेवाभरतीत १०% आरक्षण; क्रिमीलेयर वगळले; न भरलेल्या जागा ५ वर्षांपर्यंत पुढे ओढणे; २०१८ चा कायदा रद्द. न्यायालयीन आव्हानाची सद्यस्थिती पडताळावी.
