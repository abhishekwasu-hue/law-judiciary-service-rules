## How reliable is each entry
- **Read in full or in part ([V-text])**: MEPS Act (only via SC judgment extract), Maharashtra RTE Rules 2011 (excerpt), MPU Act 2016 (early part), Nursing Homes Act 1949, Caste Certificate Act 2000 (L&J text up to 2016), SEBC Act 2024 (PRS copy), Caste Certificate (Amendment) Bill 2026 (PRS copy), SC judgment in Sant Bhagwan Baba Shikshan Mandal v. Gunwant (2024).
- **Title/metadata only ([V-meta])**: almost every GR. Full GR text was not retrievable (archive/original PDFs are outside the fetch allowlist). Hence the "key provisions" for GRs are the subject of the GR title, flagged "verify against original". No GR number, date, amount or quote has been supplied from memory; where two dates may exist (upload vs GR date) the 18-digit code's first 8 digits give the upload date.
- **Departmental GR numbers** (e.g. "xxxx/प्र.क्र.xx/..") were not visible in any metadata - read from the PDF.

## Discrepancies with the brief
- "RTE Maharashtra rules 2013": the official rules found are the **2011 Rules** (notified 11-10-2011). A 2013 version/amendment is not verified.
- The MEPS Act 1977 appears on India Code as **Act No. 3 of 1978** (title year 1977).
- Caste Certificate Act is titled 2000 but is Maharashtra Act **XXIII of 2001**.
