### 1. Maharashtra Public Universities Act, 2016
- **Marathi:** महाराष्ट्र सार्वजनिक विद्यापीठ अधिनियम, २०१६
- **Act no.:** Maharashtra Act No. VI of 2017 [V-text]; Governor's assent 10-01-2017, gazetted 11-01-2017, in force 01-03-2017 (footnote). Text seen is "as amended up to 05-12-2024".
- **Issuing dept:** Higher & Technical Education (Law & Judiciary text).
- **URL (official dept copy):** https://htedu.maharashtra.gov.in/Main/DocMasters/Website/Media/1/MPU%20Act%202016%20Updated.pdf ; India Code PDF seen in search: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/16664/1/the_maharashtra_public_universities_act.pdf
- **Status:** In force (amended; Ordinances/Bills of 2018, 2020, 2023, 2024 and a 2026 Legislative Council Bill surfaced in search - not analysed).
- **Key provisions (summary - verify against original):**
  - Ch. III: Chancellor (s. 9), Vice-Chancellor (ss. 11-12), Pro-VC (s. 13), Registrar (s. 14).
  - Ch. IV authorities: Senate, Management Council, Academic Council, Faculties, Boards.
  - s. 7(2): university must follow Government reservation policy (SC, ST, DT, NT, OBC) in staff appointments and admissions.
  - s. 8(3): State may prescribe a **Standard Code** on selection, pay, duties, conduct and other service conditions of university officers, teachers and employees; Code prevails over inconsistent Statutes/Ordinances/Regulations. s. 8(1)(b)/8(3) mention post-retirement benefits.
  - Ch. VII (Grievances): Grievances Committee, **University and College Tribunal (s. 80)**, appeal rights, tribunal decision final and binding (s. 84).
  - Ch. IX: selection committees for university teachers (ss. 102-105).
  - Detailed pension/service chapters were **not visible** in the portion read.
- **Why it matters for litigation:** Forum for service disputes of university and college employees is the University and College Tribunal (writ maintainability/alternate remedy objections); Standard Code governs pay/promotion/discipline; reservation compliance claims.
- **मराठी सारांश:** विद्यापीठांचे अधिकारी, अधिकार मंडळे, शिक्षक-कर्मचाऱ्यांच्या सेवाशर्तींसाठी 'मानक संहिता' (कलम ८(३)), आरक्षण धोरण पालन (कलम ७(२)) आणि शिक्षक/कर्मचाऱ्यांच्या तक्रारींसाठी विद्यापीठ व महाविद्यालय न्यायाधिकरण (कलम ८०) अशा तरतुदी या अधिनियमात आहेत.
