- **URL:** https://archive.org/download/in.gov.maharashtra.gr.202311241802433330/202311241802433330.pdf

- **Related (GAD, 23-09-2021, 202109231447429007 [V-meta]):** Monitoring Committee for monitoring enquiries of sexual harassment / ICCs - covers All India Services officers. https://archive.org/download/in.gov.maharashtra.gr.202109231447429007/202109231447429007.pdf
