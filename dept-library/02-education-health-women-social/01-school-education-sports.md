## A. Statutes and Rules

### 1. Maharashtra Employees of Private Schools (Conditions of Service) Regulation Act, 1977 (MEPS Act)
- **Marathi:** महाराष्ट्र खाजगी शाळांतील कर्मचारी (सेवाशर्ती) विनियमन अधिनियम, १९७७
- **Act no.:** Maharashtra Act No. III of 1978 (India Code: Act No. 3, Year 1978; enactment date 20-03-1978 as shown on India Code) [V-meta; short title carries "1977"].
- **Issuing dept:** School Education & Sports Dept.
- **URL:** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/16727 (PDF seen in search results: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/16727/1/employees_of_private_schools.pdf)
- **Status:** In force (amended, e.g. Maharashtra Act XIV of 2007 added "Shikshan Sevak" to s. 2(24A) - per Supreme Court judgment below).
- **Key provisions (summary - verify against original):**
  - Governs conditions of service of employees (teaching and non-teaching) of recognised private schools, aided or unaided.
  - s. 5(1) [V-text via SC judgment]: before a permanent vacancy is filled, management must first take the surplus-persons list from the Education Officer; direct advertisement ignoring the surplus list is contrary to the Act.
  - s. 5(2), 5(2A), 5(4A) [V-text via SC judgment]: Shikshan Sevak serves 3-year probation and is then deemed confirmed.
  - Provides a School Tribunal forum for employees aggrieved by termination/dismissal/reduction etc. (existence of School Tribunal, Latur, confirmed in the SC judgment; exact section numbers for appeal/tribunal **not verified**).
  - Approval of appointments by Education Officer; surplus-employee absorption; management's duties - details **not verified**.
- **Why it matters for litigation:** Foundation for almost every aided-school employee writ (termination, approval of appointment/seniority, surplus absorption, Shikshan Sevak confirmation). State is usually respondent through Education Officer/Director; forum questions (Tribunal vs writ) arise constantly.
- **Reference case (official SC site):** Sant Bhagwan Baba Shikshan Mandal v. Gunwant, Civil Appeal 2225/2011, 2024 INSC 405, decided 03-04-2024 (Kohli & Amanullah JJ.) - held qualified non-teaching employee could be appointed Shikshan Sevak; s. 5(1) surplus-list rule applied; Rs 10 lakh compensation and notional appointment from 01-01-2010. PDF: https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2009/37521/37521_2009_11_102_51839_Judgement_03-Apr-2024.pdf
- **मराठी सारांश:** खाजगी शाळांतील शिक्षक व शिक्षकेतर कर्मचाऱ्यांच्या सेवाशर्ती, नियुक्ती, अतिरिक्त (सरप्लस) कर्मचाऱ्यांचे समायोजन व सेवासमाप्तीविरुद्ध शाळा न्यायाधिकरणात दाद मागण्याची तरतूद या अधिनियमात आहे. शिक्षण सेवकाची ३ वर्षांची परिविक्षा व नंतर नियमित समजणे ही तरतूद सुधारित कायद्यात आहे. (कलम क्रमांक मूळ अधिनियमातून तपासावेत.)

### 3. Maharashtra Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Rules, 2011 (user brief said "2013" - see note)
- **Marathi:** महाराष्ट्र बालकांना मोफत व सक्तीचे शिक्षण मिळण्याच्या हक्काचे नियम, २०११
- **Notification:** No. PRE-2010/C.R-211(B)/PE-1, dated 11-10-2011, School Education & Sports Dept [V-text; MoE-hosted copy].
- **URL:** https://www.education.gov.in/hi/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/upload_document/ccs_rte-rules_maharashtra_1.pdf
- **Status:** In force as amended. **Note:** the original rules found are of **2011**; any "2013" amendment/new rules **not verified** (search results mentioned a "2013 rules" only via non-official sites).
- **Key provisions (summary - verify against original):**
  - Rule 13: School Management Committee in non-permanently-unaided schools, 75% parent members, at least half women, monthly meetings.
  - 25% of entry-level (Class I) seats reserved for neighbourhood children of weaker/disadvantaged groups in unaided schools (Form II conditions).
  - Neighbourhood norm: classes I-V about 1 km, VI-VIII about 3 km.
  - Rules 15, 17: academic authority prescribes minimum teacher qualifications; teachers lacking them get 5 years.
  - No detention/expulsion before completing elementary education.
- **Why it matters:** 25% admission reimbursement disputes, teacher qualification (TET) and recognition issues; State is the respondent in school-recognition and reimbursement writs.
- **मराठी सारांश:** मोफत व सक्तीच्या शिक्षणासाठी शाळा व्यवस्थापन समिती, २५% राखीव प्रवेश, शेजारची शाळा (१ किमी/३ किमी), शिक्षकांची किमान अर्हता व पूर्ण प्राथमिक शिक्षणापर्यंत नापास/काढून टाकण्यास मनाई अशा तरतुदी या नियमांत आहेत.
