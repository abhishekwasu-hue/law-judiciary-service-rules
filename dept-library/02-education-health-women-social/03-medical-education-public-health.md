### 1. Maharashtra Nursing Homes Registration Act, 1949 (Bombay Act No. XV of 1949)
- **Marathi:** महाराष्ट्र शुश्रूषागृहे नोंदणी अधिनियम, १९४९ (मुंबई अधिनियम क्र. XV, १९४९)
- **Dept:** Public Health | **Text read:** [V-text] Law & Judiciary compilation "as modified up to 7 May 2013" | enacted 06-05-1949.
- **URL:** https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s36a4cbdaedcbda0fa8ddc7ea32073c475/uploads/2025/02/20250210201904388.pdf ; India Code PDF (seen): https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/17485/3/nursing.pdf ; another copy: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3feecee9f1643651799ede2740927317a/uploads/2025/07/202507221267254973.pdf
- **Status:** In force (amended by Bom. 42 of 1959, Mah. 2 of 2006, Mah. 24 of 2012 title change, etc.). **For the central Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2010 (India Code https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/7798/1/201023_clinical_establishments_(registration_and_regulation)_act,_2010.pdf) - adoption/commencement in Maharashtra NOT verified; the 1949 Act is the state registration law actually read.**
- **Key provisions (summary - verify against original):**
  - s. 2(4): "nursing home" = premises for receiving persons with sickness/injury/infirmity for treatment and nursing, includes maternity home.
  - s. 3: no one may carry on a nursing home unless registered; annual application to local supervising authority (s. 4); certificate valid to 31 March of the third year (s. 5(2)).
  - s. 9: Health Officer/Civil Surgeon/authorised officer may inspect premises and records (not patients' medical records).
  - Penalties: unregistered operation up to 6 months / Rs 10,000 / both (s. 6 as substituted); general contravention fine up to Rs 5,000 + Rs 50/day (s. 12).
- **Why it matters:** Registration/cancellation challenges by hospitals; medical-negligence and overcharging disputes reference registration conditions; Public Health Dept/Municipal Commissioners as respondents.
- **मराठी सारांश:** शुश्रूषागृह (नर्सिंग होम) चालविण्यासाठी नोंदणी आवश्यक; वार्षिक अर्ज, तपासणीचे अधिकार, नोंदणीशिवाय चालविल्यास कारावास/दंडाची शिक्षा अशा तरतुदी. केंद्रीय Clinical Establishments Act, २०१० महाराष्ट्रात लागू झाल्याचे पडताळलेले नाही.

### 4. Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MJPJAY) integrated with Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY
- **Marathi:** महात्मा ज्योतिराव फुले जन आरोग्य योजना (एकत्रित आयुष्मान भारत - PMJAY)
- **Dept:** Public Health | State Health Assurance Society.
- **Sources read [V-text, official district portal summarising the scheme]:** https://zpjalna.maharashtra.gov.in/en/scheme/pradhan-mantri-jan-arogya-yojana-and-mahatma-jyotirao-phule-jan-arogya-yojana/ . Brochure page: https://phd.maharashtra.gov.in/en/publication/mahatma-jyotirao-phule-jan-arogya-yojana/ and NHM https://nhm.maharashtra.gov.in/en/publication/mahatma-jyotirao-phule-jan-arogya-yojana/ (page text only).
- **Key points (as stated on that page - summary - verify against GRs):**
  - Pilot 8 districts 02-07-2012; statewide 21-11-2013; AB-PMJAY launched 23-09-2018; earlier integration per GR dated 26-02-2019.
  - Expansion to all families per GR dated **28-07-2023**; integrated scheme from **01-07-2024**; cover Rs 5 lakh per family per year (floater); pre-existing diseases from day one; hospitalisation across 34 specialties.
- **GR documents:** the GRs of 26-02-2019 and 28-07-2023 were **not located** on archive/official lists in this pass (gap).
- **Why it matters:** Writs by empanelled hospitals (payment, de-empanelment) and patients (package denial); not employee-service related except staff of the Society.
- **मराठी:** राज्यातील कुटुंबांना वार्षिक रु. ५ लाखांपर्यंत मोफत उपचार; २८ जुलै २०२३ च्या निर्णयानुसार व्याप्ती वाढवून १ जुलै २०२४ पासून एकात्मिक योजना (तारखा संबंधित शासन निर्णयाशी पडताळाव्यात).
