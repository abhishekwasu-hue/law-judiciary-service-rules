ORAL JUDGMENT: (Per: S.B. SHUKRE, J.)

1. Heard Shri N.S. Rao, learned Assistant Government

Pleader for the petitioners and Shri Amol Patil, learned counsel

who appears by waiving notice for the respondents.

2. Rule. Rule made returnable forthwith. Heard finally

by consent of learned counsel for the parties.

3. The issue involved in both these petitions is of

entitlement of the respondents to receive the benefit of time

bound promotion as per Government Resolution dated

08.06.1995 and also the benefit of Assured Career Progressive

Scheme as per Government Resolution dated 20.07.2001.

4. By the impugned judgment Maharashtra

Administrative Tribunal has found that these issues have been

concluded by the view taken by Division Bench of this Court at

Aurangabad in case of Mukund Shankarlal Daima Vs. State of

Maharashtra and others in Writ Petition No.3643/2009

decided on 21.11.2017.

5. This very issue was also involved in bunch of Writ

Petitions, one of them being Writ Petition No.5031/2021

decided by common judgment and order dated 25.01.2022 by
 3 wp 3269.22.jud.odt..odt
 4

 Co-ordinate Bench of this Court. The Co-ordinate Bench of this

 Court found the finding recorded on this issue by Maharashtra

 Administrative Tribunal that the issue is already concluded by

 the view taken by judgment of the Division Bench of this Court

 at Aurangabad in the case of Mukund Shankarlal Daima

 (supra) is correct. In other words, the issue involved in both

 these petitions is squarely covered by the view taken by

 Co-ordinate Bench of this Court in the aforestated Writ Petition

 No.5031/2021.

 6. Learned counsel for the respondents also agrees.

 7. Therefore, we find that there is no scope for making

 any interference with the impugned judgments and orders of

 the Maharashtra Administrative Tribunal.

 8. In view of above, both writ petitions stand

 dismissed.

 Rule stands discharged. No order as to costs.
