 P. C.

 Rule. Rule made returnable forthwith and heard the
 learned counsel for the parties.

 2. The petitioners seek second benefit under the Assured
 Career Progression scheme in terms of the Government
 Resolution dated 02.03.2019.

 3. The petitioners seek to rely upon the orders dated
 15.01.2018 passed in Writ Petition No.7123/2017 (Vikas B.
 Jadhav and ors. vs. State of Maharashtra and others) and dated
 25.11.2019 passed in Writ Petition 7118/2017 (Gopal Ananda
 Shinde vs. State of Maharashtra and others ). Undisputedly, the
 petitioners have received the first benefit in terms of the said
 scheme.

 4. It is to be noted that in the meanwhile, vide Government
 Resolution dated 02.03.2019 the Assured Career Progression
 Scheme has been modified. The benefit of Assured Career
 2 26 wp1717.2024.odt

 Progression Scheme under the recommendations of the Seventh
 Pay Commission is admissible after a period of 10/20/30 years
 of service. The Scheme has been made applicable from
 01.01.2016.

 5. In view of aforesaid, we find that the following order
 would serve the interests of justice:

 (i) The respondent nos.2 and 3 shall examine entitlement and
 eligibility of the petitioners to receive the second benefit under
 the Assured Career Progression Scheme in the light of the
 Government Resolution dated 02.03.2019 and other relevant
 Government Resolutions. The respondent nos.2 and 3 shall
 consider the aforesaid entitlement within a period of eight
 weeks of receiving copy of this order. If the petitioners are found
 entitled to receive such benefit, the same shall be released in
 their favour within a period of four months of such decision
 being taken.

 6. Rule stands disposed of in aforesaid terms with no order as
 to costs.
